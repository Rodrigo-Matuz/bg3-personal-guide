# Baldur's Gate 3 — Personal Guide

A personal reference guide for **Baldur's Gate 3**, focused on understanding classes, subclasses, feats, mechanics, quests, items, and other useful information while playing with a **heavily modded setup**.

The goal isn't to be a complete or universally accurate BG3 wiki. This repository exists primarily as a **quick-reference guide for myself and friends** playing the same [modlist](https://www.nexusmods.com/games/baldursgate3/collections/pns4qv), so I can look something up later without having to rediscover how everything works.

## Purpose

This repository is meant to answer questions like:

- _What does this class actually do?_
- _Which subclass should I pick?_
- _What stats are important for this build?_
- _Which feats are worth taking?_
- _How does this mechanic actually work?_
- _What items are particularly useful for this character?_
- _Are there important choices or consequences I should remember?_

Rather than trying to memorize everything, the idea is to keep useful information in one place and make it easy to reference during a playthrough.

Depending on the class, notes may include:

## Feats

Feats are documented with practical explanations of when they're useful.

Rather than assuming every feat is equally valuable, recommendations may include:

- Highly recommended
- Generally useful
- Build-dependent
- Defensive / situational
- Usually not worth taking

**Recommendations are not absolute rules**. A feat that is mediocre for one character can be excellent for another.

## Philosophy

This is a **reference guide, not a rulebook**.

Recommendations are based on practical usefulness, experimentation, and the specific modded environment being played.

When something is particularly important, the notes should explain **why** rather than simply saying:

> "Take this."

For example:

> **War Caster — Must get**

is less useful than:

> **War Caster:** Excellent for concentration-heavy spellcasters. It helps maintain Concentration and improves the reliability of spells that require Concentration, making it especially valuable for characters who regularly maintain buffs, debuffs, or persistent damage spells.

The goal is to make future decisions easier, even after I've forgotten the details.
