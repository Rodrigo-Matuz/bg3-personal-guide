# [[Warlock]]

[[Warlock]] is a Charisma-based caster whose power comes from a pact with a supernatural patron. Unlike other full casters, Warlocks rely heavily on **short-rest resources, powerful cantrips, Eldritch Invocations, and a small number of highly effective spell slots**. This makes them particularly effective at repeatedly using strong abilities without needing to conserve resources for every individual encounter.

The class can be built as a ranged spellcaster, melee hybrid, battlefield controller, summoner, or support character depending on the patron and pact. [[Eldritch Blast]] provides a reliable baseline attack, while the subclass determines much of the Warlock's secondary role.

In combat, [[Warlock]] can fill several different roles depending on its subclass:

- **Ranged damage dealer** — Uses [[Eldritch Blast]] and offensive spells for reliable damage from a distance.
- **Battlefield controller** — Uses curses, fear, charms, forced movement, and area spells to disrupt enemies.
- **Melee damage dealer** — Uses weapons and Warlock magic together, especially through [[The Hexblade]].
- **Summoner** — Uses supernatural or undead creatures to add additional bodies and actions to the battlefield.
- **Support caster** — Provides healing, buffs, defensive effects, or restorative magic through specialized patrons.
- **Necromancer** — Raises and commands undead through death-focused patrons and expanded spell lists.
- **Party face** — Uses high Charisma for dialogue, persuasion, deception, intimidation, and other social checks.
- **Utility caster** — Uses invocations, mobility, invisibility, curses, and supernatural abilities to solve exploration problems.

## [[The Archfey]]

[[The Archfey]] is a trickster-oriented Warlock whose patron grants powers associated with the Feywild. Its toolkit revolves around charm, deception, teleportation, invisibility, and manipulating enemies rather than simply overpowering them. Outside combat, it is useful for social encounters, infiltration, and situations where magical deception is valuable.

### Combat

[[The Archfey]] is primarily a control and mobility Warlock. It can interfere with enemies through charm and other mental effects while using teleportation and invisibility to avoid unfavorable positions.

It works particularly well for a Warlock that wants to remain mobile instead of standing in one location and repeatedly casting offensive spells.

### Progression

**Early:** Establish a ranged combat routine with [[Eldritch Blast]] while using Fey abilities for mobility and control.

**Mid:** Stronger charm, invisibility, and teleportation effects give the subclass more ways to manipulate positioning and disable enemies.

**End:** The full toolkit allows the Warlock to control encounters through mobility, deception, and battlefield manipulation while retaining strong baseline damage.

**Best for:**

- Crowd control
- Teleportation
- Invisibility
- Social utility
- Mobile ranged combat

## [[The Celestial]]

[[The Celestial]] turns the Warlock into a hybrid offensive and support caster. Its celestial patron grants radiant and restorative abilities, allowing the Warlock to heal allies without abandoning its normal Warlock damage.

### Combat

[[The Celestial]] can spend turns dealing damage normally and use its support abilities when an ally needs help. This gives it considerably more party utility than a purely offensive Warlock.

Its main advantage is role compression: the same character can provide ranged magical damage while also covering emergency healing and support.

### Progression

**Early:** Use celestial abilities for healing while relying on normal Warlock spells for damage.

**Mid:** Improved restorative and radiant abilities allow the subclass to contribute more consistently as a secondary support caster.

**End:** Stronger healing and radiant magic make the subclass a reliable offensive/support hybrid capable of adapting to the party's needs.

**Best for:**

- Secondary healing
- Radiant damage
- Party support
- Ranged spellcasting
- Flexible party composition

## [[The Dread Overlord]]

[[The Dread Overlord]] is a necromancy-focused Warlock built around death, souls, and undead armies. Unlike a conventional Warlock subclass, its BG3 implementation significantly expands the summoning side of the class with numerous undead creatures, specialized spells and equipment, and extremely powerful late-game summons.

### Combat

[[The Dread Overlord]] is primarily a summoner. Instead of relying exclusively on its own actions, it builds a battlefield presence through undead creatures that can attack, occupy space, absorb damage, and pressure enemies.

This creates a strong action-economy advantage when multiple summons are active. The drawback is that managing a large number of creatures can make turns more complicated and encounters more dependent on positioning.

### Progression

**Early:** Begin with basic necromantic abilities and establish the subclass's focus on undead magic.

**Mid:** Additional undead summons and necromantic spells allow the Warlock to field a much larger supernatural force.

**End:** Powerful undead, including exceptional creatures such as dracoliches and skeletal giants, turn the subclass into an army-focused summoner capable of overwhelming encounters through numbers and specialized summons.

**Best for:**

- Undead armies
- Summoning
- Necromancy
- Long-term battlefield pressure
- Players who enjoy managing multiple companions

## [[The Fiend]]

[[The Fiend]] is the straightforward destructive Warlock. Its infernal patron grants powerful offensive magic, particularly fire and necrotic effects, while allowing the Warlock to gain temporary durability from defeating enemies.

### Combat

[[The Fiend]] is primarily an offensive caster. Its strength is maintaining pressure through repeated damage while gaining additional survivability from enemies that die during combat.

Area damage makes it especially useful against groups, while [[Eldritch Blast]] provides reliable damage when stronger spell slots are not necessary.

### Progression

**Early:** Focus on consistent ranged damage and use Fiend abilities to improve survivability.

**Mid:** Stronger fire and offensive spells allow the Warlock to deal substantial area and single-target damage.

**End:** High-level offensive magic combined with temporary durability makes the subclass an aggressive caster that can continue fighting effectively through difficult encounters.

**Best for:**

- Fire damage
- AoE damage
- Ranged offense
- Straightforward Warlock gameplay
- Aggressive playstyles

## [[The First Vampire]]

[[The First Vampire]] turns the Warlock into a supernatural predator. Its powers focus on draining life, darkness, charm, invisibility, and sustaining itself through enemies. The subclass also expands the Warlock's spell options with effects such as [[False Life]], [[Bane]], [[Bestow Curse]], [[Gaseous Form]], and [[Greater Invisibility]].

### Combat

[[The First Vampire]] works best at weakening enemies while keeping itself alive through vampiric abilities. Life drain allows the Warlock to recover from damage while curses and control effects make individual targets easier to eliminate.

It can operate at range but has strong thematic synergy with aggressive positioning and staying close enough to exploit weakened enemies.

### Progression

**Early:** Use life-draining abilities and defensive effects to improve survivability.

**Mid:** Curses, mobility, and invisibility expand the subclass's ability to isolate and eliminate targets.

**End:** Greater supernatural mobility, stronger life drain, and powerful control effects create a self-sustaining predator that is difficult to remove.

**Best for:**

- Life drain
- Self-sustain
- Darkness
- Invisibility
- Vampiric-themed builds

## [[The Great Old One]]

[[The Great Old One]] is a psychic and eldritch Warlock focused on fear, mental manipulation, curses, and supernatural reactions to critical hits. It is less about raw elemental damage and more about making enemies lose control of the encounter.

### Combat

[[The Great Old One]] is strongest when exploiting enemies that are vulnerable to mental effects. Fear and psychic abilities can disrupt groups while its critical-hit interactions can create additional control opportunities.

It works especially well alongside characters that frequently score critical hits, as those attacks can trigger additional effects from the patron.

### Progression

**Early:** Use fear and psychic effects to supplement [[Eldritch Blast]] and conventional Warlock spells.

**Mid:** Stronger mental manipulation makes the subclass increasingly effective at disrupting enemy formations.

**End:** High-level control combined with critical-hit interactions creates a Warlock that can repeatedly destabilize enemy groups.

**Best for:**

- Fear effects
- Psychic damage
- Mental control
- Critical-hit synergy
- Eldritch-themed builds

## [[The Hexblade]]

[[The Hexblade]] is the primary martial Warlock option. It combines weapon combat with curses, shadow magic, defensive abilities, and Charisma-based weapon scaling. This allows the Warlock to invest heavily in Charisma while still functioning effectively as a melee character.

### Combat

[[The Hexblade]] can fight directly in melee while retaining access to Warlock spellcasting. Its curses make priority targets easier to kill, while weapon-enhancing abilities allow it to maintain strong physical damage.

The subclass is particularly effective when you want one character to combine martial attacks and spellcasting rather than committing fully to either role.

### Progression

**Early:** Establish a weapon-based combat routine while using Warlock magic for ranged attacks and utility.

**Mid:** Curses, weapon enhancements, and defensive abilities make melee combat increasingly effective.

**End:** Strong weapon attacks and high-level Warlock magic combine into a flexible spellsword capable of switching between melee and ranged combat.

**Best for:**

- Melee Warlock
- Charisma-based weapon builds
- Curses
- Hybrid martial/caster gameplay
- Spellsword builds

## [[The Necrobroker]]

[[The Necrobroker]] is a specialized necromantic Warlock tied to Valkrana, the Necrobroker. It combines necromancy with divination, allowing the Warlock to extract information, manipulate souls, curse enemies, and command the dead.

### Combat

[[The Necrobroker]] combines direct spellcasting with undead utility. Its dedicated necromancy spellbook and [[Command Undead]] integration allow it to gain additional battlefield presence while still using normal Warlock abilities.

Compared with [[The Dread Overlord]], it is more focused on the interaction between **knowledge, curses, souls, and necromancy** rather than simply building the largest possible undead army.

### Progression

**Early:** Establish a foundation of necromantic spells and supernatural utility.

**Mid:** Additional curses, divination effects, and undead abilities make the subclass increasingly versatile.

**End:** The expanded necromancy toolkit allows it to combine battlefield control, information gathering, curses, and undead manipulation.

**Best for:**

- Necromancy
- Curses
- Divination
- Commanding undead
- Supernatural utility

## [[The Undying Light]]

[[The Undying Light]] reverses the traditional dark Warlock archetype by drawing power from overwhelming radiant energy. It combines radiant and fire magic with healing and defensive effects, creating a Warlock that survives through positive energy rather than darkness or necromancy.

### Combat

[[The Undying Light]] is primarily an offensive elemental caster with additional healing and defensive options. Its expanded spell selection includes effects such as [[Burning Hands]], [[Flaming Sphere]], [[Daylight]], [[Fire Shield]], and [[Flame Strike]].

The subclass is especially useful when you want a Warlock with strong fire and radiant options instead of the traditional necrotic or shadow-focused toolkit.

### Progression

**Early:** Use fire and radiant effects to supplement [[Eldritch Blast]] while benefiting from improved survivability.

**Mid:** Stronger fire spells and defensive effects allow the Warlock to handle both groups of enemies and dangerous melee attackers.

**End:** High-level radiant and fire spells make the subclass a strong offensive caster with additional healing and defensive tools.

**Best for:**

- Radiant damage
- Fire damage
- Healing
- Defensive magic
- Alternative to dark-themed Warlocks
