### [[The Archfey]]

A Warlock whose patron is a powerful **Fey entity**, granting supernatural charm, deception, and mobility. **Arcane + enchantment/illusion magic**, focused on charming enemies, teleportation, invisibility, and controlling the battlefield through trickery.

---
### [[The Celestial]]

A Warlock whose patron is a **powerful celestial being**, granting access to radiant power and restorative magic. **Arcane + radiant/healing magic**, making this a strong support-oriented Warlock capable of healing allies while still dealing reliable magical damage.

---
### [[The Dread Overlord]]

A Warlock who has made a pact with an entity of **death and necromantic power**, commanding the souls of the dead. **Arcane + necromantic magic**, heavily focused on raising and empowering undead, commanding powerful summons, curses, and building an army of the dead. The BG3 mod goes considerably further than a normal Warlock subclass, adding numerous undead summons, unique spells, items, and even greater undead such as dracoliches and skeletal giants.

---
### [[The Fiend]]

A Warlock who draws power from a **demonic or infernal patron**, gaining destructive magic and strength from the suffering of their enemies. **Arcane + fire/necrotic magic**, focused on explosive damage, temporary HP, curses, and overwhelming groups of enemies.

---
### [[The First Vampire]]

A Warlock who has entered into a pact with the **First Vampire**, gaining supernatural predatory abilities at the cost of embracing vampiric nature. **Arcane + necrotic/vampiric magic**, specializing in life drain, darkness, charm, invisibility, and sustaining yourself through the life force of your enemies. The BG3 implementation includes abilities such as Drain Life, enhanced darkvision, and an expanded spell list containing effects like False Life, Bane, Bestow Curse, Gaseous Form, and Greater Invisibility.

---
### [[The Great Old One]]

A Warlock who has made contact with an **ancient, incomprehensible entity**, gaining powers that mortals were never meant to possess. **Arcane + psychic/eldritch magic**, focused on fear, mental manipulation, curses, and punishing enemies who suffer critical hits.

---
### [[The Hexblade]]

A martial Warlock whose patron is connected to a **sentient supernatural weapon or shadowy force**, allowing them to fight effectively with weapons while channeling Warlock magic. **Physical + shadow/curse magic**, specializing in melee combat, weapon enhancement, curses, defensive magic, and turning your Charisma into a weapon-focused stat. The current BG3 implementation also includes Hexblade-specific invocations and Cursebringer functionality.

---
### [[The Necrobroker]]

A Warlock who has entered a pact with **Valkrana, the calculating Necrobroker**, combining necromancy with divination to extract secrets from the dead and living alike. **Arcane + necromantic/divination magic**, focused on souls, undead, auguries, curses, and uncovering or exploiting the weaknesses of your targets. The BG3 implementation adds a dedicated necromancy spellbook and Command Undead integration.

---
### [[The Undying Light]]

A Warlock connected to the **Positive Plane and overwhelming radiant energy**, turning the traditional dark Warlock archetype toward light and life. **Arcane + radiant/fire magic**, focused on radiant damage, healing, fire spells, and surviving through the power of positive energy. Its BG3 implementation follows the Unearthed Arcana version and includes spells such as Burning Hands, Flaming Sphere, Daylight, Fire Shield, and Flame Strike.
