### [[Oath of the Ancients]]

A nature-bound Paladin sworn to **preserve life, protect the natural world, and resist darkness**. **Physical + nature/radiant magic**, with strong healing, defensive abilities, and spells that provide excellent party support and survivability.

---
### [[Oath of the Bleak Walkers]]

A grim Paladin who believes **mercy is weakness and fear is a necessary tool for maintaining order**. **Physical + necrotic/dark magic**, focused on intimidation, punishing enemies, and turning overwhelming force against those who stand in your way.

---
### [[Oath of Conquest]]

A ruthless Paladin who seeks to **dominate enemies through fear and overwhelming strength**. **Physical + fear/control magic**, specializing in frightening enemies, locking down the battlefield, and crushing opponents who cannot escape your control.

---
### [[Oath of Crown]]

A Paladin devoted to **duty, loyalty, and protecting their allies and the order they serve**. **Primarily physical + defensive/divine magic**, built around protecting teammates, controlling enemies, and standing as the party's frontline defender.

---
### [[Oath of Devotion]]

The classic **holy knight**, sworn to honesty, courage, compassion, and righteous combat. **Physical + radiant/divine magic**, with strong weapon attacks, protective abilities, healing, and reliable radiant damage.

---
### [[Oath of Glory]]

A heroic Paladin driven by **legendary deeds, physical excellence, and inspiring others to greatness**. **Primarily physical + radiant/buff magic**, emphasizing mobility, powerful melee attacks, athletic feats, and party-enhancing abilities.

---
### [[Oath of Pestilence]]

A corrupted or plague-focused Paladin who embraces **disease, poison, and decay as weapons**. **Physical + poison/necrotic magic**, specializing in spreading debilitating effects, weakening enemies, and turning prolonged fights in your favor.

---
### [[Oath of Redemption]]

A defensive Paladin who believes **even enemies can be saved**, preferring to prevent violence whenever possible. **Physical + defensive/divine magic**, focused on damage mitigation, protecting allies, disabling enemies, and punishing those who attack your party.

---
### [[Oath of Vengeance]]

A relentless hunter who dedicates themselves to **destroying those who have committed grave wrongs**. **Physical + radiant/divine magic**, specializing in single-target burst damage, advantage, mobility, and hunting down priority enemies.

---
### [[Oath of the Watchers]]

A vigilant Paladin sworn to **protect the mortal world from extraplanar threats**. **Physical + anti-magic/divine abilities**, with tools for resisting supernatural enemies, protecting the party, and shutting down creatures that rely on magical or otherworldly powers.

---
### [[Oath of Zeal]]

A fervent warrior who channels **unrestrained divine conviction into overwhelming martial power**. **Physical + radiant/fire-like divine magic**, focused heavily on aggressive combat, powerful Smites, and maximizing damage rather than playing defensively.

---
### [[Oathbreaker]]

A Paladin who has **abandoned their sacred oath and embraced darker powers**, commanding the forces of death and undeath. **Physical + necrotic/dark magic**, with abilities that empower undead, drain life, inflict fear, and turn the Paladin into a formidable dark frontline warrior.
