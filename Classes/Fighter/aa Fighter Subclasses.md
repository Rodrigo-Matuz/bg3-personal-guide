# [[Fighter]]

The [[Fighter]] is a dedicated martial class built around weapon combat, physical durability, and consistent action economy. It can specialize in melee, ranged combat, defense, battlefield control, or limited magical abilities depending on its subclass.

Fighters generally excel at using their Actions efficiently. Multiple attacks, strong weapon proficiency, [[Action Surge]], and subclass-specific combat abilities allow them to deal substantial damage without relying on spell slots. They are also less dependent on a complicated resource system than many hybrid classes.

The main difference between Fighter subclasses is how much they add to this basic martial foundation. Some remain extremely straightforward, while others introduce spells, companions, magical positioning, defensive mechanics, or tactical maneuvers.

In combat, [[Fighter]] can fill several different roles depending on its subclass:

- **Melee damage dealer** — Uses repeated weapon attacks to eliminate enemies quickly.
- **Ranged damage dealer** — Focuses on bows and other ranged weapons.
- **Tank** — Uses armor, durability, and defensive abilities to protect allies.
- **Battlefield controller** — Uses maneuvers, positioning, or magical effects to disrupt enemies.
- **Hybrid martial/caster** — Combines weapon attacks with arcane or occult abilities.
- **Companion fighter** — Adds a combat pet to the Fighter's action economy.

## [[Arcane Archer]]

The [[Arcane Archer]] combines traditional archery with magical ammunition. Instead of simply increasing weapon damage, it uses special arrows and arcane effects to give ranged attacks additional utility.

The subclass is primarily a ranged combatant and works best when it can maintain distance from enemies. Its magical shots can add damage, control targets, or provide situational utility without turning the Fighter into a full spellcaster.

### Combat

The [[Arcane Archer]] is a **ranged damage and control specialist**.

Its core gameplay is straightforward: stay at range, use your bow or other ranged weapon, and use specialized shots when their effects are more valuable than ordinary attacks.

This gives the Fighter additional ways to influence combat without sacrificing its normal martial action economy. The main limitation is that its strongest subclass effects are tied to ranged attacks, so it loses much of its identity when forced into melee.

### Progression

**Early:** Magical arrows immediately add utility to your ranged attacks.

**Mid:** Stronger Arcane Shots give you more ways to combine damage with battlefield control.

**End:** The subclass becomes a highly flexible ranged Fighter capable of dealing consistent weapon damage while applying magical effects.

**Best for:**

- Archery
- Ranged damage
- Magical ammunition
- Battlefield control
- Martial characters with limited magic

## [[Banneret]]

The [[Banneret]] turns the Fighter into a **martial support character**. Instead of relying entirely on personal damage, it uses commands, rallying abilities, and healing to improve the party.

This is a good choice when you want Fighter durability and weapon combat but still want meaningful party utility.

### Combat

The [[Banneret]] is a **martial support specialist**.

Its abilities allow the Fighter to assist allies while continuing to contribute normal weapon attacks. This means you do not have to abandon your offensive role just because you are playing a supportive Fighter.

The subclass is especially useful in parties that already have enough dedicated damage dealers but would benefit from another character capable of providing emergency support.

Its main drawback is that its personal offensive specialization is less focused than subclasses built purely around damage.

### Progression

**Early:** Rallying and supportive abilities give the Fighter immediate party utility.

**Mid:** Stronger healing and ally-focused effects make the subclass increasingly useful in difficult encounters.

**End:** The Banneret becomes a durable martial character capable of contributing damage while keeping allies fighting.

**Best for:**

- Martial support
- Party utility
- Emergency healing
- Defensive leadership
- Support-focused Fighters

## [[Battle Master]]

The [[Battle Master]] is the most tactically flexible traditional Fighter. It uses specialized combat maneuvers to deal damage, control enemies, improve defenses, and create openings for the rest of the party.

This subclass rewards players who actively manage their actions and choose the appropriate maneuver for each situation.

### Combat

The [[Battle Master]] is a **tactical damage and control specialist**.

Maneuvers let you do more than simply attack. You can knock enemies down, push them, disarm them, improve your own attacks, or create advantages for allies depending on your available options.

This makes the subclass highly effective at converting limited resources into meaningful tactical advantages. It also works well with [[Action Surge]] because powerful maneuvers can be combined with multiple attacks during important turns.

The main drawback is that the subclass requires more decision-making than a Champion or Brute.

### Progression

**Early:** Maneuvers immediately expand the Fighter's combat options.

**Mid:** Additional maneuvers and improved combat resources allow you to control more situations.

**End:** The Battle Master has a broad collection of offensive, defensive, and control tools that remain useful throughout the game.

**Best for:**

- Tactical combat
- Battlefield control
- Damage
- Disarming and knocking enemies down
- Players who want many combat decisions

## [[Brute]]

The [[Brute]] is a straightforward Fighter focused on **raw physical power**. It removes much of the tactical complexity found in other subclasses and instead improves the effectiveness of direct attacks.

The result is an easy-to-use martial character that spends most turns doing what Fighters already do best: attacking enemies with weapons.

### Combat

The [[Brute]] is a **pure damage-oriented martial**.

Its abilities improve the impact and reliability of weapon attacks while also increasing physical durability. You generally do not need complicated setup or resource management.

This makes it particularly effective for players who want a Fighter that is strong without requiring extensive tactical planning.

### Progression

**Early:** Improved physical attacks establish the subclass as a straightforward damage dealer.

**Mid:** Stronger attacks and durability make sustained melee combat more effective.

**End:** The Brute becomes an extremely reliable physical combatant with few unnecessary mechanics.

**Best for:**

- Simple Fighter gameplay
- Melee damage
- Physical power
- Durable weapon users
- Players who dislike complicated mechanics

---

## [[Cavalier]]

The [[Cavalier]] is a defensive Fighter specializing in **battlefield positioning, protection, and mounted combat**. It is designed to control where enemies can move and punish enemies that try to bypass you.

The subclass benefits most from open battlefields where movement and positioning matter.

### Combat

The [[Cavalier]] is a **tank and battlefield-control Fighter**.

Its abilities encourage enemies to engage with you instead of simply running past you toward weaker party members. Mounted combat adds mobility and lets you reposition quickly when the battlefield changes.

The subclass is particularly effective at holding chokepoints and protecting vulnerable allies.

Its effectiveness can be reduced in cramped environments where mounting and movement are difficult.

### Progression

**Early:** Defensive abilities establish the Cavalier as a frontline protector.

**Mid:** Stronger control tools make it harder for enemies to ignore you.

**End:** The subclass combines high durability, mobility, and enemy control to become a reliable frontline anchor.

**Best for:**

- Tanking
- Mounted combat
- Protecting allies
- Chokepoints
- Battlefield positioning

## [[Champion]]

The [[Champion]] is the simplest Fighter specialization. It improves the Fighter's natural strengths without introducing a complicated new resource system.

Its main focus is **consistent physical damage and improved critical hits**, making it particularly attractive for builds that already have strong weapon damage and critical-hit bonuses.

### Combat

The [[Champion]] is a **straightforward damage dealer**.

You can focus almost entirely on attacking because the subclass does not require elaborate setup. Improved critical-hit mechanics increase the frequency with which your attacks produce particularly strong results.

This makes the Champion easy to play and highly compatible with equipment or abilities that further reward critical hits.

The downside is reduced tactical utility compared with subclasses such as Battle Master or Echo Knight.

### Progression

**Early:** Improved critical-hit potential immediately increases offensive consistency.

**Mid:** Additional physical improvements strengthen your basic attacks.

**End:** The Champion remains one of the simplest ways to build a reliable weapon-focused Fighter.

**Best for:**

- Critical-hit builds
- Simple gameplay
- Consistent weapon damage
- Heavy weapons
- Players who want minimal complexity

## [[Echo Knight]]

The [[Echo Knight]] creates a magical **echo** that can attack and reposition around the battlefield. The echo effectively gives the Fighter another position from which to threaten enemies.

This makes the subclass unusually mobile and gives it strong tactical options both in melee and around difficult terrain.

### Combat

The [[Echo Knight]] is a **mobile melee Fighter and battlefield-control specialist**.

You can use the echo to attack from unexpected positions, threaten distant enemies, and reposition without moving your main character normally. This allows you to control more space than a conventional Fighter.

Its main strength is positioning rather than raw damage. The subclass can make enemies reconsider where they stand because your threat range effectively extends through the echo.

### Progression

**Early:** The echo immediately provides unusual mobility and positioning options.

**Mid:** Additional echo-based abilities expand your ability to attack and reposition.

**End:** The subclass becomes extremely effective at controlling battlefield space while maintaining the Fighter's normal attack output.

**Best for:**

- Mobility
- Teleportation
- Battlefield control
- Tactical positioning
- Unusual melee builds

## [[Eldritch Knight]]

The [[Eldritch Knight]] combines Fighter weapon mastery with **arcane spellcasting**. It retains the Fighter's strong physical foundation while gaining defensive, offensive, and utility spells.

The subclass is ideal for players who want a martial character that can use magic without becoming dependent on spellcasting.

### Combat

The [[Eldritch Knight]] is a **martial/caster hybrid**.

You can use weapons for sustained damage and spells when a magical solution is more effective. Defensive spells can improve survivability, while offensive spells provide damage types and effects unavailable to a purely physical Fighter.

The main challenge is action economy. Casting a spell uses the same Action that could have been used for attacks, so you need to choose between magical effects and weapon damage rather than automatically using both.

### Progression

**Early:** Defensive and utility magic adds versatility to your weapon-based combat.

**Mid:** More powerful spells give you additional offensive and defensive options.

**End:** The subclass becomes a durable martial character with enough arcane magic to solve situations that conventional Fighters cannot.

**Best for:**

- Martial/caster hybrids
- Defensive magic
- Arcane utility
- Weapon-focused spellcasters
- Players who want Fighter with magic

---

## [[Guardian]]

The [[Guardian]] is a dedicated defensive Fighter built specifically around **protecting allies and controlling enemy aggression**. It sacrifices some offensive specialization in exchange for stronger defensive tools.

The subclass works best when positioned between enemies and the rest of the party.

### Combat

The [[Guardian]] is a **dedicated tank**.

Its abilities allow it to absorb punishment and make it more difficult for enemies to reach vulnerable allies. Rather than maximizing personal damage, you focus on remaining in the most important position on the battlefield.

This is especially effective when paired with ranged characters and spellcasters who need time and space to operate.

The main drawback is that a defensive specialization can deal less damage than more aggressive Fighter subclasses.

### Progression

**Early:** Defensive abilities immediately make the Fighter more reliable on the frontline.

**Mid:** Stronger protective effects allow you to shield allies more consistently.

**End:** The Guardian becomes increasingly difficult to remove while providing strong protection for nearby party members.

**Best for:**

- Tanking
- Ally protection
- Defensive parties
- Holding chokepoints
- Frontline control

## [[Houndmaster]]

The [[Houndmaster]] combines Fighter weapon combat with a **trained combat hound**. The hound provides another source of damage and battlefield presence while also offering tracking and utility outside combat.

The subclass is straightforward to understand: fight with your weapon while your companion fights beside you.

### Combat

The [[Houndmaster]] is a **martial companion class**.

The hound can attack independently, giving the party another combat body without requiring the Fighter to spend every Action controlling it. This can increase your effective battlefield presence and allow the hound to harass vulnerable enemies.

Positioning is important because the hound can be used to pressure enemies from another angle while the Fighter attacks elsewhere.

The main drawback is that the companion adds another unit that can be targeted or killed.

### Progression

**Early:** The combat hound immediately provides additional damage and utility.

**Mid:** The companion becomes stronger and more useful for controlling enemy positions.

**End:** The hound becomes a meaningful secondary combatant rather than merely an exploration companion.

**Best for:**

- Pet gameplay
- Martial combat
- Tracking
- Additional battlefield presence
- Companion-focused parties

## [[Living Crucible]]

The [[Living Crucible]] transforms the Fighter into a **living alchemical weapon**. Instead of using conventional magic, it channels unusual physical and elemental effects through its body.

The subclass is more experimental than a standard Fighter and works well for players who want a supernatural martial character without using conventional spellcasting.

### Combat

The [[Living Crucible]] is a **physical/elemental hybrid**.

It maintains the Fighter's normal weapon-based combat while adding alchemical effects that can provide additional damage or utility.

This gives it more variety than a pure martial subclass without requiring the full spellcasting system of an Eldritch Knight.

### Progression

**Early:** Alchemical abilities add unusual effects to conventional Fighter attacks.

**Mid:** Stronger elemental abilities give the subclass more ways to handle different enemies.

**End:** The Living Crucible becomes a versatile martial character capable of combining physical attacks with supernatural elemental effects.

**Best for:**

- Hybrid martial builds
- Elemental damage
- Unusual Fighter mechanics
- Alchemical themes
- Players who want something different from standard Fighters

## [[Psi Warrior]]

The [[Psi Warrior]] channels psionic energy through its body and weapons. It combines physical attacks with telekinetic and defensive abilities, giving the Fighter tools that do not rely on conventional magic.

Its powers are useful for both offense and battlefield manipulation.

### Combat

The [[Psi Warrior]] is a **martial/psionic hybrid**.

Psionic abilities can enhance attacks, protect allies, manipulate positioning, and provide additional battlefield control. This allows the Fighter to contribute more than raw weapon damage without becoming a traditional caster.

Its versatility is its main strength, but it requires more resource management and tactical decisions than simpler martial subclasses.

### Progression

**Early:** Psionic abilities provide immediate offensive and defensive utility.

**Mid:** Stronger telekinetic effects give you greater battlefield control.

**End:** The subclass combines high martial damage with enough psionic utility to influence positioning and survivability.

**Best for:**

- Psionic characters
- Telekinesis
- Battlefield control
- Martial/psychic hybrids
- Tactical Fighters

## [[Rune Knight]]

The [[Rune Knight]] uses ancient giant runes to empower its body and weapons. Its abilities can increase physical power, provide elemental effects, and improve defenses.

The subclass is particularly effective when you want a Fighter that can temporarily become much larger and more imposing.

### Combat

The [[Rune Knight]] is a **durable strength-based Fighter**.

Rune abilities give you additional offensive and defensive options while its size-increasing abilities improve your physical presence. Increased size also works well with abilities and equipment that benefit from high Strength and throwing power.

The subclass can therefore function as either a durable melee character or a more specialized giant-themed build.

### Progression

**Early:** Runes provide additional defensive and utility abilities.

**Mid:** Stronger runes and increased size improve both damage and battlefield presence.

**End:** The subclass becomes a powerful physical combatant with several magical defensive and elemental tools.

**Best for:**

- Strength builds
    
- Giant-themed characters
    
- Large weapons
    
- Defensive utility
    
- Throwing builds

## [[Samurai]]

The [[Samurai]] focuses on **determination, accuracy, and short bursts of exceptional martial performance**. It is designed around creating important turns where your attacks become much more reliable and dangerous.

The subclass remains primarily physical and does not require complicated magical mechanics.

### Combat

The [[Samurai]] is a **burst-damage Fighter**.

Its abilities improve your ability to land attacks when accuracy matters most, making it particularly effective when you need to eliminate an important target quickly.

It works well with multiple attacks because a strong offensive turn can be used to make several reliable weapon attacks in succession.

The main drawback is that its strongest tools are more valuable when saved for important turns rather than used indiscriminately.

### Progression

**Early:** Improved reliability gives the Samurai strong burst potential.

**Mid:** Additional offensive tools make important turns increasingly dangerous.

**End:** The subclass becomes a highly reliable weapon attacker capable of producing powerful burst turns.

**Best for:**

- Burst damage
    
- Advantage-based attacks
    
- Precision weapon builds
    
- Single-target elimination
    
- Martial characters without magic

## [[Sharpshooter]]

The [[Sharpshooter]] is a Fighter dedicated entirely to **ranged weapon combat**. It focuses on maximizing bow and ranged weapon damage while eliminating enemies before they can reach the party.

Unlike the Arcane Archer, it does not rely on magical ammunition. Its strength comes from pure ranged weapon specialization.

### Combat

The [[Sharpshooter]] is a **dedicated ranged damage dealer**.

Its gameplay is simple: maintain distance, find advantageous positions, and use repeated ranged attacks to eliminate priority targets.

The subclass benefits heavily from high ground, line of sight, and effects that improve ranged accuracy or damage. It is particularly effective when another party member can keep enemies occupied at close range.

Its major weakness is dependence on positioning. Enemies that close the distance or block line of sight can reduce its effectiveness.

### Progression

**Early:** Strong ranged specialization makes bows and other ranged weapons your primary damage source.

**Mid:** Improved accuracy and damage increase your ability to eliminate targets from range.

**End:** The subclass becomes a highly specialized ranged Fighter capable of producing substantial sustained weapon damage.

**Best for:**

- Archery
    
- Ranged weapon damage
    
- High-ground combat
    
- Priority-target elimination
    
- Pure physical ranged builds

## [[Witch Knight]]

The [[Witch Knight]] combines Fighter weapon combat with **witchcraft, curses, and occult magic**. Instead of using conventional arcane spellcasting, it focuses on supernatural effects that complement martial attacks.

This makes it a good option for a Fighter who wants magical abilities without feeling like a traditional wizard-style hybrid.

### Combat

The [[Witch Knight]] is an **occult martial hybrid**.

You use weapons as your primary source of damage while applying curses and supernatural effects to enemies. These effects can weaken targets and give your attacks additional utility.

The subclass is particularly effective against individual priority targets because curses allow you to focus additional pressure on an important enemy.

The main drawback is that its magical tools are more specialized than those of an Eldritch Knight.

### Progression

**Early:** Curses and occult abilities add magical utility to your weapon attacks.

**Mid:** Stronger supernatural effects improve your ability to weaken priority targets.

**End:** The Witch Knight becomes a flexible martial character capable of combining consistent weapon damage with powerful occult effects.

**Best for:**

- Occult martial builds
    
- Curses
    
- Dark magic
    
- Hybrid weapon/magic gameplay
    
- Supernatural Fighters
