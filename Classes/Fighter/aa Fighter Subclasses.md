### [[Arcane Archer]]

A ranged Fighter who combines **archery with arcane magic**, empowering arrows with magical effects and special shots. **Physical + arcane magic**, focused on ranged damage, utility, and controlling the battlefield from a distance.

---
### [[Banneret]]

A martial leader who inspires allies through **rallying commands, healing, and supportive abilities**. **Purely physical with support-focused abilities**, making it a strong choice for a Fighter who wants to help the party without becoming a spellcaster.

---
### [[Battle Master]]

A highly versatile Fighter who turns **superior combat technique into powerful maneuvers**, controlling enemies and exploiting openings. **Purely physical**, with excellent options for damage, crowd control, defense, and tactical utility.

---
### [[Brute]]

A straightforward Fighter built around **raw physical power and overwhelming attacks**. **Purely physical**, sacrificing complex mechanics for stronger hits, improved durability, and consistent melee damage.

---
### [[Cavalier]]

A defensive mounted warrior specializing in **protecting allies, controlling enemies, and dominating the battlefield from horseback**. **Purely physical**, with an emphasis on durability, positioning, mounted combat, and forcing enemies to deal with you.

---
### [[Champion]]

The quintessential **straightforward martial Fighter**, improving critical hits and physical combat without adding complicated mechanics. **Purely physical**, ideal for consistent weapon damage, critical-hit builds, and players who want a simple but powerful Fighter.

---
### [[Echo Knight]]

A supernatural Fighter who creates a **magical echo of themselves**, allowing them to attack and reposition through it. **Physical + teleportation/temporal magic**, focused on mobility, battlefield control, and attacking from unexpected positions.

---
### [[Eldritch Knight]]

A Fighter who combines **weapon mastery with arcane spellcasting**, using magic to enhance both offense and defense. **Physical + arcane magic**, with access to spells such as defensive wards, elemental attacks, mobility, and battlefield utility.

---
### [[Guardian]]

A heavily defensive Fighter designed to **protect allies and control enemies**, turning its durability into a weapon. **Primarily physical with defensive/protective abilities**, best suited for a front-line tank who wants to keep enemies away from the party.

---
### [[Houndmaster]]

A Fighter who fights alongside a **trained combat hound**, adding a loyal companion to their martial toolkit. **Primarily physical**, combining weapon attacks with a pet for additional damage, tracking, positioning, and battlefield pressure.

---
### [[Living Crucible]]

A Fighter who has transformed their body into a **living vessel of alchemical power**, using unusual physical and elemental effects in combat. **Physical + alchemical/elemental abilities**, offering a more unconventional hybrid Fighter playstyle.

---
### [[Psi Warrior]]

A Fighter who channels **psionic energy through their mind and body**, augmenting attacks, movement, and defenses with invisible force. **Physical + psionic/force abilities**, combining martial damage with telekinetic effects, defensive powers, and battlefield manipulation.

---
### [[Rune Knight]]

A Fighter who harnesses **ancient giant runes** to empower their body and weapons. **Physical + magical rune abilities**, with options for increased size, elemental effects, defensive powers, and battlefield control.

---
### [[Samurai]]

A disciplined warrior focused on **determination, precision, and overwhelming bursts of martial power**. **Purely physical**, excelling at reliable weapon damage, advantage, and powerful turns where you need to finish an enemy quickly.

---
### [[Sharpshooter]]

A Fighter dedicated entirely to **long-range weapon combat**, specializing in precision, powerful shots, and eliminating enemies before they reach the party. **Purely physical**, focused on ranged weapon damage and maximizing the effectiveness of bows and other ranged weapons.

---
### [[Witch Knight]]

A Fighter who combines **martial combat with witchcraft and supernatural curses**, bringing strange magical effects into the battlefield. **Physical + dark/occult magic**, suited for a hybrid Fighter who wants curses, magical utility, and weapon combat rather than traditional wizardry.
