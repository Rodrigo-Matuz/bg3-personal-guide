# [[Ranger]]

[[Ranger]] is a martial class built around ranged and melee weapon combat, exploration, tracking, and limited nature-based spellcasting. It combines reliable weapon damage with mobility, stealth, battlefield positioning, and utility spells such as [[Hunter's Mark]], [[Longstrider]], [[Pass Without Trace]], and [[Spike Growth]]. Rangers are primarily martial characters, with spellcasting serving as a supplement rather than replacing their weapon attacks.

The class is especially effective when it can control distance and choose favorable positions before a fight begins. Depending on the subclass, a [[Ranger]] can become a dedicated ambusher, companion-based fighter, elemental specialist, defensive frontliner, or flexible ranged damage dealer.

In combat, [[Ranger]] can fill several different roles depending on its subclass:

- **Ranged damage dealer** — Uses bows, crossbows, or other ranged weapons to deal consistent damage from a safe distance.
- **Melee damage dealer** — Uses weapon attacks and Extra Attack to fight effectively at close range.
- **Mobile skirmisher** — Uses movement, teleportation, and positioning tools to attack vulnerable targets and relocate.
- **Stealth specialist** — Uses stealth, darkness, invisibility, and ambushes to gain an advantage before combat.
- **Companion fighter** — Fights alongside an animal, drake, or other summoned ally that adds additional battlefield pressure.
- **Battlefield controller** — Uses spells, terrain effects, forced movement, slowing effects, and other abilities to control enemy positioning.
- **Elemental damage dealer** — Adds cold, lightning, radiant, or other elemental effects to conventional Ranger attacks.
- **Exploration specialist** — Excels at tracking, perception, stealth, animal interaction, and navigating hostile environments.

## [[Beast Master]]

[[Beast Master]] turns the [[Ranger]]'s relationship with nature into a permanent combat companion. The subclass is built around choosing an animal that complements your preferred fighting style, with options such as bears, wolves, ravens, boars, and spiders. The companion is useful both in combat and exploration, giving the party another creature capable of attacking, scouting, controlling space, or helping allies. In BG3, the companion acts independently rather than requiring the Ranger to spend an action commanding it.

**Combat**

The companion effectively adds another combat unit to the party. Different beasts provide different utility: some focus on durability, others on mobility, prone effects, webs, blindness, or damage. The Ranger can continue making normal weapon attacks while the companion attacks on its own turn, making the subclass particularly strong at maintaining pressure across multiple targets. Later improvements give companions better defenses, damage, mobility, and additional attacks.

**Progression**

**Early:** Your animal companion immediately adds another source of damage and utility. Choose the beast based on whether you want durability, control, mobility, or damage.

**Mid:** The companion becomes considerably more durable and receives stronger abilities, allowing it to remain relevant rather than functioning as a temporary summon.

**End:** The companion gains stronger combat abilities and an additional attack, making the Ranger + beast combination a consistent two-unit fighting style.

**Best for:**

- Companion-focused gameplay
- Extra battlefield presence
- Ranged Ranger + melee companion
- Animal-themed characters
- Players who enjoy managing multiple combatants

## [[Drakewarden]]

[[Drakewarden]] replaces the traditional animal companion with a growing draconic ally. The drake adds elemental damage, additional battlefield presence, and supernatural utility while the Ranger remains capable of fighting normally with weapons. Outside combat, the draconic theme also gives the subclass a stronger identity than a conventional nature-focused Ranger.

**Combat**

The drake works as a second combat unit that complements either melee or ranged attacks. Its elemental abilities give it more offensive flexibility than a conventional companion, while its positioning can help control enemies and support the Ranger. The subclass works particularly well when you use the drake to occupy enemies while the Ranger attacks from a favorable position.

**Progression**

**Early:** The drake provides immediate additional damage and elemental utility while the Ranger remains the primary damage dealer.

**Mid:** The companion becomes tougher and more useful as its abilities improve, allowing it to function as a reliable secondary combatant.

**End:** The drake becomes a major part of the Ranger's battlefield presence, combining improved survivability, elemental damage, and support with the Ranger's own attacks.

**Best for:**

- Dragon-themed characters
- Companion gameplay
- Elemental damage
- Ranged Ranger + melee companion
- Flexible battlefield positioning

## [[Fey Wanderer]]

[[Fey Wanderer]] combines conventional Ranger combat with Feywild-inspired magic and supernatural social abilities. Instead of focusing entirely on killing enemies efficiently, the subclass adds charm, fear, psychic damage, and other effects that allow the Ranger to manipulate encounters. This also makes it more useful outside combat when dealing with NPCs and situations where social abilities matter.

**Combat**

The subclass adds mental and magical effects to the Ranger's normal weapon attacks. It is particularly useful when you want to weaken or disrupt enemies while continuing to deal weapon damage. Rather than replacing the Ranger's martial role, the Fey abilities give you additional ways to influence a fight.

**Progression**

**Early:** You gain Fey-themed abilities that add control and utility to otherwise conventional Ranger attacks.

**Mid:** Your magical and defensive options become more useful, allowing you to influence enemies without sacrificing your normal weapon damage.

**End:** The subclass becomes a flexible hybrid capable of combining reliable martial damage with mental control and supernatural utility.

**Best for:**

- Hybrid martial/magic gameplay
- Charm and fear effects
- Psychic damage
- Social utility
- Fey-themed characters

## [[Gloom Stalker]]

[[Gloom Stalker]] is the Ranger's dedicated ambush and stealth specialization. It excels at starting fights from advantageous positions and eliminating important targets before they can respond. Superior darkvision, invisibility, increased mobility, and strong opening-round abilities make it especially effective for stealth-oriented parties.

**Combat**

The subclass is strongest at the beginning of combat. Its opening-round bonuses reward attacking quickly, while stealth and invisibility make it easier to choose when and where the fight begins. The additional mobility also helps the Ranger reach high ground, escape melee threats, or reposition after eliminating a target.

It is particularly effective with ranged weapons because you can attack from stealth, gain favorable positioning, and continue dealing damage without exposing yourself to unnecessary retaliation.

**Progression**

**Early:** You gain strong stealth tools, improved darkvision, increased initiative, and powerful opening-round attacks.

**Mid:** Mobility and defensive abilities improve, while [[Misty Step]] gives you an efficient way to reposition around the battlefield.

**End:** The subclass becomes an extremely effective ambusher capable of delivering multiple attacks quickly and remaining difficult to pin down.

**Best for:**

- Stealth
- Ambushes
- Ranged damage
- High initiative
- Assassin-style gameplay
- Eliminating priority targets

## [[Horizon Walker]]

[[Horizon Walker]] uses planar magic to make the Ranger exceptionally mobile. The subclass combines normal weapon combat with teleportation, force damage, and supernatural movement, making positioning one of its primary strengths.

**Combat**

The subclass is designed to move around the battlefield rather than remain in one position. Teleportation and planar abilities allow you to reach distant enemies, escape dangerous situations, and attack targets from unexpected angles. Force-based attacks also give the Ranger a reliable supernatural damage option.

**Progression**

**Early:** Your attacks begin gaining planar properties, adding force damage and supernatural utility.

**Mid:** Teleportation and mobility become more important, letting you reposition efficiently between targets.

**End:** The subclass becomes a highly mobile martial character capable of crossing the battlefield quickly while maintaining strong weapon damage.

**Best for:**

- High mobility
- Teleportation
- Force damage
- Hit-and-run combat
- Melee or ranged skirmishing

## [[Hunter]]

[[Hunter]] is the straightforward, flexible [[Ranger]] subclass. Instead of introducing a major secondary mechanic, it improves the Ranger's ability to deal with different types of enemies. This makes it a reliable choice for players who want strong martial combat without managing a companion or heavily specialized magical system.

**Combat**

The subclass focuses on sustained weapon damage and situational offensive bonuses. Its strength is consistency: you can choose the option that best matches the encounter and continue fighting normally with your weapons.

**Progression**

**Early:** You gain a straightforward offensive specialization that improves your ability to punish certain enemy groups or individual targets.

**Mid:** Defensive and offensive options expand, helping the Ranger remain effective against increasingly dangerous encounters.

**End:** You gain powerful multi-target or high-damage options, allowing the Hunter to remain a reliable damage dealer throughout longer fights.

**Best for:**

- Simple Ranger gameplay
- Consistent weapon damage
- Flexible combat
- Ranged builds
- Players who do not want companion management

## [[Monster Slayer]]

[[Monster Slayer]] specializes in identifying and dismantling dangerous enemies. It is designed around the idea of studying a target, exposing its weaknesses, and systematically removing its advantages. This makes it particularly appropriate for campaigns with powerful monsters, spellcasters, or other high-priority enemies.

**Combat**

The subclass is strongest when concentrating your attention on an important target. Marking and anti-monster abilities make the Ranger more effective against powerful enemies while providing tools for disrupting their abilities. It is less focused on general battlefield manipulation than [[Swarmkeeper]] or [[Gloom Stalker]].

**Progression**

**Early:** You gain tools for identifying and focusing on dangerous enemies.

**Mid:** Your abilities become more effective at disrupting supernatural creatures and protecting yourself against their abilities.

**End:** The subclass becomes a dedicated priority-target hunter capable of consistently pressuring powerful enemies.

**Best for:**

- Boss enemies
- Monster hunting
- Single-target damage
- Anti-magic utility
- Focus-fire gameplay

## [[Primeval Guardian]]

[[Primeval Guardian]] turns the Ranger into a massive nature-based defender. Instead of relying on speed and ranged positioning, this subclass sacrifices mobility for increased size, reach, durability, and battlefield control. It is effectively a Ranger built to hold ground.

**Combat**

The subclass works best when you want to occupy a dangerous area and force enemies to fight around you. Increased reach lets you threaten more space, while defensive abilities make you harder to remove. This is a significant departure from the Ranger's usual mobile skirmisher role.

**Progression**

**Early:** You begin gaining defensive and guardian-oriented abilities that make standing in melee safer.

**Mid:** Your increased reach and durability become more important as enemies become harder to control.

**End:** You function as a durable nature-themed frontliner capable of controlling a large area while continuing to deal weapon damage.

**Best for:**

- Frontline tanking
- Battlefield control
- High durability
- Melee Ranger builds
- Nature-themed guardians

## [[Spiritmaster]]

[[Spiritmaster]] combines Ranger weapon combat with spiritual companions and supernatural forces. Rather than relying on one physical animal, the subclass uses magical spirits to add additional effects and battlefield support.

**Combat**

Spiritual abilities give the Ranger additional ways to influence the battlefield while continuing to use normal weapons. The spirits can supplement damage, provide utility, or create favorable situations for the rest of the party.

**Progression**

**Early:** Spiritual abilities add extra utility to the Ranger's standard attacks.

**Mid:** Your supernatural options become more influential, giving you more ways to support the party and pressure enemies.

**End:** The subclass becomes a flexible hybrid capable of combining weapon attacks with several supernatural effects.

**Best for:**

- Spirit-themed characters
- Summoning
- Hybrid martial/magic gameplay
- Battlefield support
- Nature and supernatural utility

## [[Stargazer]]

[[Stargazer]] channels celestial and cosmic power through conventional Ranger abilities. It combines ranged weapon combat with radiant damage and supernatural effects, giving the Ranger a more magical offensive identity without becoming a full spellcaster.

**Combat**

The subclass works well from range, using weapons as its primary source of damage while adding radiant and celestial effects. It can pressure enemies from a safe distance and use magical abilities when conventional attacks are not enough.

**Progression**

**Early:** Celestial abilities supplement your normal weapon attacks with radiant effects.

**Mid:** Your magical damage and utility become more significant, giving you more options against different enemy types.

**End:** You become a versatile ranged martial character capable of combining weapon damage with strong radiant and celestial abilities.

**Best for:**

- Ranged combat
- Radiant damage
- Celestial themes
- Magical archers
- Hybrid martial/magic builds

## [[Swarmkeeper]]

[[Swarmkeeper]] fights alongside a magical swarm that can attack enemies, reposition the Ranger, or provide defensive and utility effects. The subclass is particularly flexible because different swarm types provide different damage and control options. In BG3, the swarm can also apply effects such as [[Shocked]], [[Blinded]], or [[Slowed]], depending on the chosen type.

**Combat**

The swarm acts as an extension of your normal attacks rather than replacing them. You can use it to deal additional damage, move yourself or an enemy, or apply disabling effects. This makes [[Swarmkeeper]] especially good at manipulating positioning while continuing to attack normally.

The subclass also benefits from [[Hunter's Mark]], allowing swarm damage to benefit from the Ranger's marked-target damage bonus.

**Progression**

**Early:** The swarm adds extra damage, forced movement, or utility to your normal attacks.

**Mid:** Your swarm becomes more useful for controlling positioning and applying additional effects.

**End:** The swarm gains stronger versions of its effects, making it a reliable source of damage and battlefield control alongside your normal weapon attacks.

**Best for:**

- Battlefield control
- Forced movement
- Ranged combat
- Extra damage
- Flexible utility
- Nature-themed characters

---

## [[Winter Walker]]

[[Winter Walker]] uses frost and winter magic to supplement conventional Ranger attacks. The subclass is built around slowing enemies, dealing cold damage, and making terrain more difficult to cross, turning positioning into one of its main advantages.

**Combat**

The subclass is strongest when you can keep enemies at range and make approaching you difficult. Cold effects can slow opponents and create dangerous areas, allowing the Ranger and the rest of the party to control enemy movement.

**Progression**

**Early:** You gain frost-based abilities that add cold damage and basic control to your weapon attacks.

**Mid:** Your cold effects become more effective at slowing enemies and controlling the battlefield.

**End:** You can combine strong ranged weapon damage with significant cold damage and terrain control, making it difficult for enemies to reach your party.

**Best for:**

- Cold damage
- Ranged combat
- Slowing enemies
- Terrain control
- Elemental Ranger builds
