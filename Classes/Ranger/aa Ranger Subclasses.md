### [[Beast Master]]

A Ranger who fights alongside a **loyal animal companion**, turning their bond with the beast into a major part of their combat style. **Primarily physical + nature magic**, with the Ranger and companion working together to control the battlefield and overwhelm enemies.

---
### [[Drakewarden]]

A Ranger bonded to a **draconic companion** that grows alongside them and gains increasingly powerful abilities. **Physical + elemental/dragon magic**, combining ranged or melee attacks with a versatile drake that can deal elemental damage and support its master.

---
### [[Fey Wanderer]]

A Ranger touched by the **mysterious power of the Feywild**, gaining supernatural abilities that blur the line between hunter and trickster. **Physical + fey/enchantment magic**, with charm, fear, psychic damage, and social utility alongside normal Ranger combat.

---
### [[Gloom Stalker]]

A master of **darkness, ambushes, and subterranean hunting**, striking enemies before they can react. **Primarily physical + shadow magic**, specializing in stealth, initiative, darkvision, invisibility, and devastating opening-round damage.

---
### [[Horizon Walker]]

A Ranger capable of manipulating **planar and teleportation magic**, moving across the battlefield in ways ordinary hunters cannot. **Physical + force/planar magic**, combining mobility, teleportation, and magically enhanced weapon attacks.

---
### [[Hunter]]

The classic **versatile monster-hunting Ranger**, adapting their techniques to exploit different types of enemies. **Primarily physical + nature magic**, with flexible offensive options and reliable sustained damage rather than a specialized gimmick.

---
### [[Monster Slayer]]

A dedicated **supernatural monster hunter** who studies dangerous creatures and learns how to dismantle their defenses. **Physical + anti-monster/divine magic**, focused on marking priority targets, disrupting spells and abilities, and dealing consistent damage against powerful foes.

---
### [[Primeval Guardian]]

A Ranger who becomes a **massive, tree-like guardian**, sacrificing mobility for extraordinary durability and battlefield control. **Physical + nature magic**, designed as a defensive frontliner with increased reach, toughness, and the ability to protect an area.

---
### [[Spiritmaster]]

A mystical Ranger who commands **spiritual companions and supernatural forces** to aid them in battle. **Physical + spirit/nature magic**, combining conventional Ranger attacks with summoned spirits and magical battlefield support.

---
### [[Stargazer]]

A Ranger who draws power from the **stars and celestial forces**, using cosmic energy to enhance their hunting abilities. **Physical + radiant/celestial magic**, with ranged combat, magical attacks, and celestial utility.

---
### [[Swarmkeeper]]

A Ranger accompanied by a **magical swarm of tiny creatures**, which they can manipulate to attack, defend, and reposition themselves. **Physical + nature/swarm magic**, using the swarm for extra damage, forced movement, defense, and battlefield control.

---
### [[Winter Walker]]

A Ranger empowered by **winter and frost**, turning the cold into both an offensive weapon and a means of controlling the battlefield. **Physical + cold/elemental magic**, specializing in frost damage, slowing enemies, and creating dangerous terrain.
