### [[Aberrant Mind]]

A Sorcerer whose innate magic comes from **psionic or otherworldly influence**, giving them supernatural control over the minds of others. **Psychic + arcane magic**, specializing in mental manipulation, psychic damage, telepathy, and bypassing traditional magical defenses.

---
### [[Clockwork Soul]]

A Sorcerer infused with the power of **cosmic order and perfect balance**, manipulating the rules of reality to eliminate chaos. **Arcane + order/control magic**, focused on protecting allies, correcting failed rolls, removing advantages and disadvantages, and maintaining consistency on the battlefield.

---
### [[Divine Right]]

A Sorcerer who possesses an innate **divine authority**, channeling celestial power without needing to serve a traditional deity. **Divine + radiant magic**, focused on powerful blessings, radiant damage, healing, and asserting supernatural authority over enemies.

---
### [[Divine Soul]]

A Sorcerer whose innate magic comes from a **connection to divine power**, giving them access to both arcane and divine spellcasting. **Arcane + divine magic**, combining Sorcerer Metamagic with healing, buffs, radiant damage, and powerful Cleric spells.

---
### [[Draconic Bloodline]]

A Sorcerer whose magic comes from **draconic ancestry**, granting them the physical traits and elemental power of their dragon lineage. **Arcane + elemental magic**, specializing in a chosen damage type, enhanced elemental spells, natural defenses, and eventually gaining draconic wings.

---
### [[Favored Soul]]

A Sorcerer with a natural connection to **divine power**, gaining supernatural gifts normally associated with Clerics while retaining spontaneous Sorcerer casting. **Divine + arcane magic**, focused on healing, buffs, radiant damage, and supporting the party while still benefiting from Metamagic.

---
### [[Lunar Sorcery]]

A Sorcerer whose magic is tied to the **phases and mystical power of the moon**, shifting between different lunar influences. **Arcane + lunar magic**, focused on manipulating spells according to lunar phases, gaining different offensive, defensive, and control effects depending on the current aspect.

---
### [[Shadow Magic]]

A Sorcerer whose power comes from the **Shadowfell and the essence of darkness**, allowing them to survive and move through shadows. **Shadow + necrotic magic**, specializing in darkness, stealth, teleportation, survivability, and devastating enemies from within the darkness.

---
### [[Storm Sorcery]]

A Sorcerer born with the **raw power of storms**, naturally channeling thunder and lightning through their magic. **Lightning + thunder magic**, focused on explosive elemental damage, mobility, and repositioning after casting spells.

---
### [[Wild Magic (Sor)]]

A Sorcerer whose magic is **unstable and unpredictable**, causing random magical effects whenever their power surges. **Pure arcane + chaotic magic**, combining powerful conventional spellcasting with unpredictable Wild Magic effects that can dramatically alter the battlefield.
