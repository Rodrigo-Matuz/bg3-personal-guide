# [[Sorcerer]]

[[Sorcerer]] is a spontaneous arcane caster built around **Charisma, Metamagic, and a limited but highly focused spell selection**. Unlike a Wizard, it does not prepare a large library of spells; instead, it knows a smaller set and uses Metamagic to make those spells more effective. Sorcerer subclasses significantly change how that limited spell selection is used, ranging from elemental damage and mobility to divine support, psychic control, and defensive utility.

The class is particularly strong at converting its limited actions into high-value turns. Metamagic can improve spell range, duplicate spells, increase their reliability, or allow additional spellcasting opportunities, making Sorcerer one of the strongest classes for explosive spellcasting when its resources are managed correctly.

In combat, [[Sorcerer]] can fill several different roles depending on its subclass:

- **Arcane damage dealer** — Uses powerful spells and Metamagic to deal high burst damage.
- **Crowd controller** — Uses enchantments, psychic effects, fear, and control spells to disable groups of enemies.
- **Elemental specialist** — Builds around a specific damage type and amplifies it through subclass features.
- **Support caster** — Provides healing, buffs, protection, and utility through divine or defensive subclass abilities.
- **Battlefield controller** — Uses area spells, positioning tools, and movement effects to control where enemies can fight.
- **Party face** — Uses high Charisma for dialogue, persuasion, deception, intimidation, and other social checks.
- **Utility caster** — Uses teleportation, invisibility, defensive magic, and specialized subclass abilities to solve exploration problems.

## [[Aberrant Mind]]

[[Aberrant Mind]] is a psychic-focused [[Sorcerer]] whose magic comes from an alien or otherworldly influence. It emphasizes mental manipulation, telepathy, psychic damage, and unusual magical effects. Outside combat, its supernatural communication and mind-oriented abilities make it useful for social encounters, investigation, and situations where manipulating a creature is more useful than fighting it directly.

### Combat

[[Aberrant Mind]] works best as a control-oriented caster. Psychic damage gives it a distinct offensive option while its mental effects can disable or manipulate enemies. It is particularly useful when you want to spend actions controlling important targets rather than simply dealing damage.

Its main strength is flexibility: the subclass can combine normal Sorcerer burst casting with mental effects that attack enemies through their minds.

### Progression

**Early:** Focus on reliable control spells and psychic effects while keeping conventional Sorcerer damage available.

**Mid:** The subclass becomes more effective at manipulating individual targets and exploiting enemies with mental defenses.

**End:** Strong control, psychic damage, and Metamagic combine into a flexible caster capable of disabling important enemies while still providing conventional Sorcerer burst.

**Best for:**

- Psychic damage
- Mental manipulation
- Crowd control
- Telepathy and supernatural utility
- Flexible spellcasting

## [[Clockwork Soul]]

[[Clockwork Soul]] is a Sorcerer empowered by cosmic order and perfect balance. Instead of specializing in one damage type, it focuses on preventing randomness from working against the party. Its abilities are particularly useful for protecting allies, correcting failed rolls, and removing advantages or disadvantages.

### Combat

[[Clockwork Soul]] is primarily a defensive control Sorcerer. It can interfere with the normal flow of dice-based combat, helping allies succeed when they would otherwise fail and making enemies less reliable.

This makes the subclass especially valuable when combined with powerful concentration spells or important saving throws. Rather than maximizing personal damage every turn, it improves the consistency of the entire party.

### Progression

**Early:** Focus on defensive effects and reliable control while using normal Sorcerer spells for damage.

**Mid:** Roll manipulation and defensive tools become more valuable as enemy abilities become stronger.

**End:** The subclass provides strong consistency across difficult encounters, protecting allies from bad rolls while maintaining Sorcerer's normal offensive capabilities.

**Best for:**

- Defensive support
- Roll manipulation
- Reliable spellcasting
- Battlefield control
- Protecting important allies

## [[Divine Right]]

[[Divine Right]] is a Sorcerer with innate divine authority rather than conventional arcane origins. It combines spontaneous Sorcerer casting with radiant and divine effects, giving the class access to both offensive and supportive tools.

### Combat

[[Divine Right]] can alternate between damaging enemies with radiant magic and supporting allies through divine abilities. Its biggest advantage is that it does not need to choose between being an offensive caster and having useful support options.

Metamagic makes its divine spells more flexible, allowing important buffs, attacks, or healing effects to become significantly more effective when the situation demands it.

### Progression

**Early:** Use divine abilities to supplement conventional Sorcerer damage and provide basic support.

**Mid:** Expanded divine options allow the subclass to contribute more consistently as both damage dealer and support caster.

**End:** Powerful radiant effects, healing, and Metamagic make it a flexible divine caster capable of adapting its role from encounter to encounter.

**Best for:**

- Radiant damage
- Divine support
- Healing
- Flexible spellcasting
- Hybrid offense/support

## [[Divine Soul]]

[[Divine Soul]] combines innate Sorcerous magic with divine spellcasting. This gives it access to healing, buffs, protection, and other divine tools while retaining Metamagic. The result is one of the most flexible support-oriented Sorcerer options.

### Combat

[[Divine Soul]] can function as a conventional offensive Sorcerer until the party needs support. It can then switch to healing, defensive buffs, or other divine effects without giving up its normal arcane spellcasting.

The main drawback is that Sorcerers know relatively few spells, so the subclass benefits greatly from deliberate spell selection. Its strength comes from choosing a compact set of spells that cover multiple situations.

### Progression

**Early:** Combine offensive Sorcerer spells with a small number of high-value healing or support options.

**Mid:** The larger selection of divine tools makes the subclass increasingly useful outside pure damage casting.

**End:** Strong Metamagic combined with high-level divine and arcane spells creates an effective offensive/support hybrid.

**Best for:**

- Healing
- Party support
- Buffing
- Offensive spellcasting
- Flexible party composition

## [[Draconic Bloodline]]

[[Draconic Bloodline]] gains its power from draconic ancestry, specializing in a chosen elemental damage type while gaining additional durability and eventually draconic mobility. It is one of the most straightforward offensive Sorcerer subclasses and rewards building around its chosen element.

### Combat

[[Draconic Bloodline]] is primarily an elemental damage dealer. Its subclass features improve survivability while allowing its associated elemental spells to hit harder.

The subclass works especially well when the party can reduce or bypass enemy resistance to the chosen damage type. When the correct element is available, its damage becomes more consistent and efficient.

### Progression

**Early:** Gain increased durability and establish your preferred elemental damage type.

**Mid:** Elemental damage becomes more significant as stronger spells become available.

**End:** Powerful elemental spells combine with subclass bonuses and increased mobility to create a highly effective damage-focused Sorcerer.

**Best for:**

- Elemental damage
- Simple offensive casting
- Durable Sorcerer builds
- Element-specific builds
- High burst damage

## [[Favored Soul]]

[[Favored Soul]] is a divine-powered Sorcerer that retains spontaneous arcane casting while gaining divine gifts. It is designed for players who want the flexibility of Sorcerer casting without giving up healing and party support.

### Combat

[[Favored Soul]] can begin encounters as a normal offensive Sorcerer and switch to support when necessary. Healing, buffs, and radiant effects allow it to contribute even when direct damage is not the best use of its action.

Metamagic gives these support spells additional flexibility, making the subclass particularly useful in parties that lack a dedicated divine caster.

### Progression

**Early:** Combine offensive spells with basic healing and support.

**Mid:** Expanded divine abilities make the subclass increasingly effective as a secondary support caster.

**End:** High-level spellcasting and Metamagic allow it to alternate between major offensive turns and powerful party support.

**Best for:**

- Secondary healer
- Radiant damage
- Buffing
- Flexible party support
- Hybrid caster builds

## [[Lunar Sorcery]]

[[Lunar Sorcery]] draws power from the phases of the moon, changing its magical capabilities according to different lunar influences. Rather than focusing on one permanent specialization, it provides a more flexible toolkit that can shift between offense, defense, and control.

### Combat

[[Lunar Sorcery]] is strongest when you actively adapt your spell selection and tactics to the current lunar aspect. Different effects can support different types of encounters, allowing you to emphasize damage, protection, or control as needed.

This makes it less straightforward than a dedicated elemental Sorcerer but potentially more adaptable across a long adventuring day.

### Progression

**Early:** Use the lunar effects to supplement conventional Sorcerer spells and establish a flexible toolkit.

**Mid:** Additional lunar options allow the subclass to change its role more effectively between encounters.

**End:** The full lunar toolkit gives the Sorcerer strong offensive, defensive, and control options without locking it into one specialization.

**Best for:**

- Flexible spellcasting
- Offensive/control hybrids
- Defensive utility
- Adaptable builds
- Thematic lunar magic

## [[Shadow Magic]]

[[Shadow Magic]] draws its power from darkness and the Shadowfell, specializing in stealth, darkness, survival, and supernatural mobility. It is particularly effective for characters who want to control encounters from inside magical darkness.

### Combat

[[Shadow Magic]] can use darkness to interfere with enemy attacks while exploiting the same environment for its own advantage. Teleportation and shadow-based movement make it difficult to pin down.

The subclass is especially effective when the party is designed to function inside darkness. It can combine defensive positioning with offensive spells while enemies struggle to respond.

### Progression

**Early:** Use shadow abilities for survival and positioning while building toward a darkness-focused playstyle.

**Mid:** Shadow movement and supernatural defensive tools become increasingly useful.

**End:** Stronger mobility and shadow abilities make the subclass difficult to lock down and highly effective at controlling fights through darkness.

**Best for:**

- Darkness-based combat
- Stealth
- Teleportation
- Survivability
- Shadow-themed parties

---

## [[Storm Sorcery]]

[[Storm Sorcery]] channels lightning, thunder, and wind into offensive magic and rapid repositioning. It is an aggressive elemental Sorcerer that rewards constant movement rather than remaining stationary after casting.

### Combat

[[Storm Sorcery]] is strongest when using lightning and thunder spells while taking advantage of its movement tools to reposition after casting. This lets the Sorcerer attack from advantageous locations without spending as much of its turn simply moving.

It works particularly well in encounters where enemies are spread out or where maintaining distance is important.

### Progression

**Early:** Use storm magic for elemental damage while exploiting mobility to stay out of melee.

**Mid:** Lightning and thunder effects become more dangerous, while storm defenses improve survivability.

**End:** High-level elemental spells and storm abilities turn the subclass into a mobile burst-damage caster with strong repositioning.

**Best for:**

- Lightning damage
- Thunder damage
- Mobile spellcasting
- Burst damage
- Hit-and-reposition tactics

## [[Wild Magic (Sor)]]

[[Wild Magic (Sor)]] is the unpredictable Sorcerer subclass. Its magic can produce unexpected effects when its power surges, making every fight less predictable. It retains the normal Sorcerer ability to deliver powerful spells while adding a layer of randomness that can help or disrupt the party.

### Combat

[[Wild Magic (Sor)]] is less about perfect consistency and more about exploiting powerful opportunities when they appear. Wild Magic effects can significantly alter an encounter, sometimes providing a major advantage and sometimes creating a new problem that the party must handle.

This makes it less predictable than other Sorcerer subclasses. It is particularly suited to players who enjoy adapting to unexpected situations rather than executing the same optimized sequence every fight.

### Progression

**Early:** Conventional Sorcerer spells remain your main source of damage and control while Wild Magic adds unpredictability.

**Mid:** More powerful spells give the random effects a stronger impact on already chaotic encounters.

**End:** High-level Sorcerer casting combined with increasingly significant Wild Magic effects creates a powerful but unpredictable caster.

**Best for:**

- Unpredictable combat
- Chaotic encounters
- Experimental builds
- Players who enjoy adapting
- High-risk/high-variance spellcasting
