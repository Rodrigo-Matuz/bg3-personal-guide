# [[Pugilist]]

[[Pugilist]] is a Strength-focused unarmed martial class built around fighting with fists, improvised weapons, and sheer physical toughness. Unlike [[Monk]], it generally trades mobility and defensive finesse for heavier hits, greater durability, and a more aggressive street-fighting style. Its core resource, Moxie, fuels special techniques and lets the Pugilist push beyond what ordinary unarmed attacks can do.

The class is strongest when fighting at close range and maintaining pressure on enemies. Its subclasses determine whether that pressure comes from tracking wounded targets, fighting alongside a hound, using dark magic, wearing heavy armor, employing dirty tricks, supporting allies, or specializing in boxing techniques.

In combat, [[Pugilist]] can fill several different roles depending on its subclass:

- **Melee damage dealer** — Uses powerful unarmed attacks and Moxie-fueled techniques to overwhelm enemies at close range.
- **Bruiser** — Uses high durability and physical toughness to stay in melee and absorb punishment.
- **Mobile pursuer** — Chases weakened or fleeing enemies and keeps pressure on targets trying to escape.
- **Companion fighter** — Fights alongside a loyal hound that adds attacks and battlefield pressure.
- **Debuffer** — Uses fear, darkness, dirty tricks, and other effects to make enemies easier to defeat.
- **Tank** — Uses armor and defensive abilities to remain on the frontline while enemies attack them.
- **Support** — Uses healing and defensive abilities to protect allies while continuing to fight.
- **Boxer** — Focuses on precise punches, combinations, counters, and knockouts.

## [[Bloodhound Bruiser]]

A relentless Pugilist who approaches combat like a hunter tracking prey. [[Bloodhound Bruiser]] combines strong unarmed combat with exceptional awareness, initiative, and tools for identifying and exploiting vulnerable enemies. It also provides useful exploration utility through skills such as Investigation, Insight, and Perception.

### Combat

[[Bloodhound Bruiser]] excels at starting fights aggressively and focusing down important targets. Its initiative bonuses and advantages against enemies that have not acted yet make it particularly effective during the opening round. Later abilities allow it to scrutinize a target, gaining advantages against that enemy while making the target less effective against the Pugilist.

This makes the subclass especially effective when you identify one dangerous enemy and keep attacking them rather than constantly changing targets.

### Progression

**Early:** Strong opening-round performance with improved initiative and better awareness.

**Mid:** Gains stronger tools for focusing on individual enemies and controlling the advantage/disadvantage relationship between the Pugilist and its target.

**End:** Becomes an extremely reliable hunter with improved perception, darkvision, resistance to enemy tactics, and tools for dealing with invisible creatures.

**Best for:**

- Single-target damage
- High initiative
- Hunting dangerous enemies
- Exploration and investigation
- Players who want a detective-style brawler

## [[Dog & Hound]]

A Pugilist who fights alongside a trained canine companion. [[Dog & Hound]] adds a second combatant to the class, allowing you to pressure enemies from two positions while maintaining the Pugilist's normal unarmed fighting style. The hound also provides useful tracking and exploration flavor.

### Combat

The hound gives the subclass additional battlefield presence rather than simply increasing the Pugilist's personal damage. It can attack enemies, benefit from certain Pugilist abilities, and gain advantages when attacking creatures that the Pugilist is already engaging.

The subclass becomes more effective at coordinating attacks as it progresses. Your own actions can improve the hound, while later features allow it to contribute additional attacks and punish enemies that attack it.

The main drawback is action economy: managing a companion adds another unit to control, and some encounters may make it difficult for the hound to reach useful targets.

### Progression

**Early:** Gains the hound immediately, providing another body on the battlefield and a strong thematic identity.

**Mid:** The hound becomes more closely integrated with the Pugilist's Moxie abilities and gains stronger offensive capabilities.

**End:** The companion becomes significantly more durable and mobile, while the Pugilist gains additional ways to punish enemies that attack it.

**Best for:**

- Companion gameplay
- Two-unit melee pressure
- Tracking and exploration
- Battlefield flanking
- Players who want a combat pet

## [[Hand of Dread]]

A sinister Pugilist who channels supernatural darkness through their fists. [[Hand of Dread]] adds necrotic and fear-based abilities to the class, turning unarmed attacks into tools for weakening and frightening enemies.

### Combat

[[Hand of Dread]] keeps the Pugilist's normal melee pressure but adds supernatural effects that make enemies less capable of fighting back. Fear and dark abilities give the subclass additional control without requiring it to abandon its unarmed combat identity.

The subclass works best when you combine its debilitating effects with repeated melee attacks. Rather than functioning as a traditional spellcaster, it uses supernatural abilities to make its physical attacks more effective.

### Progression

**Early:** Establishes the dark Pugilist identity with supernatural abilities supporting normal unarmed combat.

**Mid:** Gains stronger fear, necrotic, and weakening effects that make prolonged melee engagements more favorable.

**End:** Combines high physical damage with increasingly dangerous supernatural effects, giving the Pugilist reliable ways to disrupt enemies.

**Best for:**

- Dark-themed characters
- Fear effects
- Necrotic damage
- Debuffing enemies
- Supernatural melee builds

## [[Mailed Fist]]

A heavily armored Pugilist who combines traditional boxing with armor and brute force. [[Mailed Fist]] removes much of the usual distinction between an unarmed brawler and a heavily equipped warrior, allowing you to stay in the middle of combat while relying on your fists rather than conventional weapons.

### Combat

[[Mailed Fist]] is one of the simplest Pugilist subclasses to understand: get into melee and hit things. Heavy armor makes the subclass more comfortable absorbing attacks, while its unarmed attacks continue to provide the primary offensive pressure.

The additional durability makes it less dependent on avoiding attacks than more evasive martial builds. This is useful when you want your Pugilist to hold a frontline position instead of constantly repositioning.

### Progression

**Early:** Establishes a durable armored brawler capable of fighting directly in melee.

**Mid:** Improves the effectiveness of its armored fighting style while maintaining strong unarmed damage.

**End:** Becomes a durable frontline bruiser capable of taking significant punishment while continuing to deal heavy physical damage.

**Best for:**

- Tanky unarmed builds
- Heavy armor
- Straightforward melee combat
- High durability
- Players who want a heavily armored boxer

## [[Piss & Vinegar]]

A dirty street fighter who uses insults, distractions, and unconventional attacks to win fights. [[Piss & Vinegar]] focuses on psychological pressure and dirty tricks rather than honorable martial technique.

Outside combat, its aggressive personality fits intimidation-focused characters. In combat, it provides more tactical options than simply attacking every turn.

### Combat

The subclass uses abilities such as enhanced [[Vicious Mockery]], [[Bane]], blinding effects, prone attacks, and other dirty tricks to disrupt enemies. Some of these techniques also interact with Moxie, allowing the Pugilist to recover resources while fighting.

This gives the subclass a flexible action economy. You can attack normally when damage is the priority, or spend actions and bonus actions on disruptive techniques when disabling an enemy is more valuable.

### Progression

**Early:** Gains offensive insults and dirty tricks that add control to the Pugilist's normal attacks.

**Mid:** Gains stronger disruptive abilities, including tools for blinding and manipulating enemies.

**End:** Becomes a highly unconventional melee fighter with access to strong control effects and powerful manipulation abilities.

**Best for:**

- Dirty fighting
- Crowd control
- Intimidation
- Debuffs
- Players who want unconventional combat options

## [[Street Saint]]

A Pugilist who turns street fighting into a form of practical protection and support. [[Street Saint]] combines unarmed martial combat with limited divine abilities, allowing the character to heal, protect, and strengthen allies without becoming a conventional Cleric or Paladin.

### Combat

[[Street Saint]] can remain in melee while still contributing to party survival. Its support abilities give the Pugilist something useful to do when another attack is not the best option.

This makes it particularly effective in parties that already have strong damage dealers but want another character capable of providing emergency healing or defensive support.

### Progression

**Early:** Adds basic restorative and supportive abilities to the Pugilist's melee toolkit.

**Mid:** Improves its ability to support allies while maintaining strong unarmed attacks.

**End:** Becomes a hybrid frontline fighter capable of dealing significant physical damage while providing meaningful defensive and restorative utility.

**Best for:**

- Hybrid damage/support
- Emergency healing
- Defensive melee
- Good-aligned street-fighter concepts
- Parties needing extra support

## [[The Sweet Science]]

A dedicated boxer focused on technique, footwork, combinations, and precise unarmed attacks. [[The Sweet Science]] is the most direct choice for players who want the Pugilist to feel like an actual boxer rather than a supernatural martial artist or specialized brawler.

### Combat

[[The Sweet Science]] focuses heavily on improving the effectiveness of repeated punches. Its abilities reward maintaining pressure, chaining attacks together, and exploiting openings created by previous strikes.

The subclass is particularly well suited to the Pugilist's natural action economy because it does not require you to completely change how the class fights. You continue punching while using its specialized techniques to make those punches more effective.

### Progression

**Early:** Establishes a straightforward boxing playstyle focused on repeated unarmed attacks.

**Mid:** Gains stronger techniques for controlling and disrupting enemies through precise punches.

**End:** Becomes a dedicated close-range damage dealer with powerful combinations and reliable knockout potential.

**Best for:**

- Pure boxing
- Maximum unarmed damage
- Repeated attacks
- Knockout-focused builds

- Players who want the most straightforward Pugilist experience
