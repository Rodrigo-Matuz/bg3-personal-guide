### [[Bloodhound Bruiser]]

A relentless brawler who fights like a **trained hunting dog**, tracking down wounded enemies and refusing to let them escape. **Purely physical**, focused on pursuit, close-range pressure, and exploiting enemies who are already injured.

---
### [[Dog & Hound]]

A Pugilist who fights alongside a **loyal canine companion**, combining their own brutal fists with the coordinated attacks of their hound. **Purely physical**, built around teamwork between the Pugilist and their companion, with strong tracking and battlefield pressure.

---
### [[Hand of Dread]]

A sinister Pugilist who channels **fear and supernatural darkness through their fists**. **Physical + necrotic/dark magic**, specializing in frightening enemies, weakening them, and turning unarmed attacks into something genuinely terrifying.

---
### [[Mailed Fist]]

A heavily armored Pugilist who proves that **you don't need a weapon when your fists are weapons**. **Purely physical**, combining armor, powerful punches, and durability to create a much tougher front-line brawler.

---
### [[Piss & Vinegar]]

A foul-mouthed, hard-headed **street fighter fueled by sheer attitude and aggression**. **Purely physical**, focused on dirty tricks, brutal attacks, intimidation, and refusing to back down when things get ugly.

---
### [[Street Saint]]

A Pugilist who has turned **street fighting into a form of unconventional martial discipline**, protecting others while remaining perfectly comfortable fighting dirty. **Physical + limited divine/support abilities**, combining brutal unarmed combat with defensive and restorative techniques.

---
### [[The Sweet Science]]

A dedicated **boxer**, focusing on footwork, combinations, counters, and landing devastating punches. **Purely physical**, specializing in precise unarmed attacks, defensive technique, and chaining punches together for increasingly powerful combinations.
