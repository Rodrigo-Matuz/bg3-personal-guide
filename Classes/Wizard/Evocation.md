## Desc.

A dedicated arcane damage dealer who specializes in powerful area-of-effect and elemental spells. Evocation Wizards excel at dealing massive magical damage while their Evocation features allow them to cast destructive spells more safely around allies.

**Playstyle:** Stay at range, maintain concentration on powerful spells, and use high-damage AoE and elemental spells to control and destroy groups of enemies. Position yourself carefully and use Sculpt Spells to unleash AoE magic without worrying as much about hitting your allies.

**Notes:**

- Focused spellcaster/damage dealer.
- Excellent AoE and elemental damage.
- Sculpt Spells makes AoE spells much safer to use around allies.
- Extremely versatile thanks to the Wizard spellbook and ability to learn spells from scrolls.
- Relies heavily on **INT** for spell accuracy and damage.
- Fragile compared to martial classes, so positioning and defensive spells are important.
- Not particularly dependent on Charisma, making it ideal as a non-party-face character.

## Feats

- [[Ability Improvement]]: **Always good** -> prioritize INT
- [[Alert]]: Excellent for going early and getting your AoE/control spells online before enemies can act
- [[Elemental Adept]]: Excellent if you heavily specialize in a specific elemental damage type
- [[Spell Sniper]]: Good option for improving spell critical-hit potential
- [[Tough]]: More HP if you want additional survivability
- [[War Caster]]: **Excellent** for maintaining concentration
- [[Resilient]]: Excellent if taken for **CON**, especially for concentration saves

> **Note:** These are merely universal suggestions. The best feats may vary depending on your build, equipment, party composition, chosen spells, elemental specialization, preferred playstyle etc...
