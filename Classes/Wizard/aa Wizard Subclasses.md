### [[Abjuration]]

A defensive Wizard who specializes in **wards, protection, and countering hostile magic**. **Pure arcane magic**, focused on Arcane Ward, damage mitigation, defensive spells, and keeping both yourself and your allies alive.

---
### [[Bladesinging]]

A magical **spellblade** who combines Wizard spellcasting with agile weapon combat. **Physical + arcane magic**, using Bladesong to become faster and harder to hit while fighting with weapons and spells together.

---
### [[Chronurgy]]

A Wizard who manipulates **time itself**, bending the flow of reality to alter outcomes and give themselves or allies an advantage. **Arcane + time/dunamancy magic**, focused on controlling timing, manipulating rolls and actions, and temporarily freezing or disrupting enemies.

---
### [[Conjuration]]

A Wizard specializing in **summoning, teleportation, and creating things from nothing**. **Pure arcane magic**, focused on summons, battlefield positioning, teleportation, and producing useful objects or effects.

---
### [[Divination]]

A Wizard who sees **possible futures and hidden truths**, manipulating fate before events happen. **Pure arcane magic**, specializing in Portent, information gathering, prediction, and turning important rolls in your favor.

---
### [[Enchantment]]

A Wizard who specializes in **controlling the minds of others**, bending enemies to their will rather than simply destroying them. **Arcane + enchantment/psychic magic**, focused on charms, incapacitation, manipulation, and turning enemies against each other.

---
### [[Evocation]]

A destructive Wizard who specializes in **raw magical firepower**, unleashing powerful spells without worrying about catching allies in the blast. **Pure arcane elemental magic**, focused on maximizing spell damage, AoE, and reliable offensive casting.

---
### [[Hedge Mage]]

A Wizard who combines **arcane scholarship with practical druidic magic**, living somewhere between a traditional Wizard and a hedge witch. **Arcane + nature magic**, with Druid spells, plant-based summons, nature utility, and magical herbal elixirs.

---
### [[Hexcraft]]

A Wizard who studies **curses and forbidden magical arts**, turning hexes and debilitating effects into their primary weapons. **Arcane + curse/necrotic magic**, focused on weakening enemies, stacking curses, and exploiting afflicted targets rather than relying solely on raw damage.

---
### [[Hierophant]]

A Wizard who has studied **divine magic alongside the arcane**, learning to reproduce miracles through scholarly understanding rather than traditional Cleric devotion. **Arcane + divine magic**, combining Wizard spellcasting with healing, restoration, radiant/necrotic spells, and the ability to turn supernatural enemies.

---
### [[Illusion]]

A deceptive Wizard who bends **perception and reality**, creating false images and making themselves difficult to predict or attack. **Pure arcane + illusion magic**, specializing in deception, invisibility, misdirection, and confusing enemies.

---
### [[Necrobroker]]

A Wizard who combines **necromancy with divination**, treating souls and the secrets they contain as valuable information. **Arcane + necromantic/divination magic**, focused on extracting knowledge from the dead, auguries, curses, undead servants, and uncovering enemy secrets.

---
### [[Necromancy]]

A Wizard who manipulates **life, death, and the undead**, turning fallen enemies into additional resources. **Pure arcane + necrotic magic**, focused on necrotic damage, healing through kills, raising undead, and building an army of servants.

---
### [[Order of Scribes]]

A Wizard who treats their **spellbook as a magical entity in its own right**, using written knowledge and a spectral manifestation of their book to enhance their casting. **Pure arcane magic**, focused on versatility, spellbook-based abilities, changing how spells interact with damage types, and casting through their Spectral Mind. The BG3 version necessarily adapts some tabletop mechanics to fit the game's systems.

---
### [[Psionics]]

A Wizard who replaces traditional arcane specialization with **raw mental power**, manipulating minds and objects through psionic energy. **Psychic + arcane magic**, focused on telekinetic effects, mental attacks, psionic defenses, and controlling enemies without relying exclusively on conventional spells. The BG3 implementation is based on the Unearthed Arcana Psionics Wizard and supports progression to level 20.

---
### [[Transmutation]]

A Wizard who specializes in **altering matter, bodies, and physical properties**. **Pure arcane + transmutation magic**, focused on buffs, changing forms, crafting, alchemical utility, and manipulating creatures and the environment.

---
### [[War Magic]]

A battle-trained Wizard who focuses on **surviving combat while maintaining powerful spellcasting**, combining defensive reactions with aggressive magical tactics. **Pure arcane magic**, built around protecting yourself from attacks, improving initiative and defenses, and becoming particularly effective when fighting under pressure.
