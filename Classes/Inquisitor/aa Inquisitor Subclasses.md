### [[Tactics Inquisition]]

A team-oriented Inquisitor who turns **Judgments into powerful buffs for the entire party**. **Physical + divine support magic**, focused on Shared Judgment, party buffs, defensive protection, improved criticals, and enabling allies to perform better against your chosen targets.

---
### [[Vengeance Inquisition]]

A relentless **single-target monster hunter** who marks enemies as a Vengeful Quarry and becomes increasingly effective at killing them. **Physical + divine offensive magic**, specializing in identifying weaknesses, gaining advantage, improving critical hits, bypassing defenses, and relentlessly pursuing priority targets.

---
### [[Zeal Inquisition]]

An exceptionally durable Inquisitor who **turns suffering into power**, absorbing damage and becoming stronger as the fight progresses. **Physical + divine/force magic**, focused on self-healing, delayed damage, protecting allies, taunting enemies, and eventually unleashing stored damage back onto the battlefield.
