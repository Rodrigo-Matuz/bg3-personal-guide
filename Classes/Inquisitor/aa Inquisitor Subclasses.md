# [[Inquisitor]]

The [[Inquisitor]] is a divine martial class focused on hunting dangerous enemies, identifying weaknesses, and turning divine judgment into combat advantages. It combines weapon-based combat with divine abilities, allowing it to function as a damage dealer, monster hunter, or party-support specialist depending on its Inquisition.

The class is strongest when it can identify important targets and focus its abilities on them. Rather than relying on broad spellcasting like a **Cleric**, the [[Inquisitor]] generally uses divine powers to improve martial attacks, punish specific enemies, and provide targeted support.

In combat, [[Inquisitor]] can fill several different roles depending on its subclass:

- **Single-target damage dealer** — Focuses on eliminating priority enemies quickly.
- **Party support** — Uses Judgments and divine abilities to improve allies.
- **Defensive frontline** — Absorbs damage and protects weaker party members.
- **Monster hunter** — Specializes in exploiting enemy weaknesses and bypassing defenses.
- **Critical-hit specialist** — Uses judgments and subclass abilities to improve offensive reliability.

---

## [[Tactics Inquisition]]

The [[Tactics Inquisition]] turns the Inquisitor's Judgment mechanic into a **party-wide support tool**. Instead of keeping its divine advantages for itself, the Inquisitor shares them with allies, making the entire group more effective against selected targets.

This subclass is best played as a frontline or midline commander who identifies important enemies and keeps the party focused on them.

### Combat

The [[Tactics Inquisition]] is a **martial support specialist**.

Its Shared Judgment abilities allow the Inquisitor's chosen target to become a source of benefits for the entire party. Depending on the available Judgment effects, allies can gain offensive improvements, defensive benefits, or better critical-hit potential.

This makes target selection important. You get the most value when the party is concentrating on one or a few dangerous enemies rather than spreading damage across the battlefield.

The subclass still contributes normal weapon damage, so supporting the party does not mean giving up your own offensive role.

### Progression

**Early:** Judgments provide useful combat advantages, establishing the subclass as a party-oriented Inquisitor.

**Mid:** Shared Judgment becomes increasingly valuable as more allies can benefit from the Inquisitor's chosen targets.

**End:** The subclass becomes a strong party enabler, combining martial damage with powerful group-wide offensive and defensive benefits.

**Best for:**

- Party support
- Coordinated attacks
- Judgment-based buffs
- Critical-hit support
- Frontline commanders

## [[Vengeance Inquisition]]

The [[Vengeance Inquisition]] is the dedicated **single-target hunter**. It marks important enemies as Vengeful Quarry and builds its combat strategy around killing those targets as efficiently as possible.

This subclass works particularly well when the party needs a character dedicated to eliminating bosses, elite enemies, or dangerous spellcasters.

### Combat

The [[Vengeance Inquisition]] is a **single-target damage specialist**.

Vengeful Quarry focuses the Inquisitor's abilities on a specific enemy, improving your ability to hit, deal critical hits, and exploit weaknesses. Additional abilities can help bypass defenses, making the subclass particularly effective against difficult targets.

The action economy is straightforward: mark the priority enemy, close the distance or attack from range, and spend your combat resources on eliminating that target.

Its main weakness is that much of its specialization is concentrated on individual targets. It is less efficient when fights contain many equally important enemies.

### Progression

**Early:** Vengeful Quarry establishes the subclass's focus on hunting specific enemies.
**Mid:** Improved offensive tools make marked targets increasingly vulnerable to the Inquisitor's attacks.
**End:** The subclass becomes an effective elite and boss killer, combining strong weapon attacks with improved accuracy, critical-hit potential, and defense-piercing abilities.
**Best for:**

- Boss killing
- Single-target damage
- Monster hunting
- Critical-hit builds
- Priority-target elimination

## [[Zeal Inquisition]]

The [[Zeal Inquisition]] is the most defensive Inquisitor, turning **damage taken into a resource**. Rather than avoiding all incoming damage, it can absorb punishment, protect allies, and eventually convert stored damage into additional offensive power.

This makes it well suited to prolonged frontline encounters where the Inquisitor can remain engaged with enemies.

### Combat

The [[Zeal Inquisition]] is a **durable frontline tank and damage absorber**.

Its abilities allow it to survive incoming attacks while recovering health, protecting allies, and encouraging enemies to focus on it. Damage that would normally be purely negative can instead contribute to the subclass's later offensive abilities.

This creates a different action economy from the Vengeance Inquisition. Instead of maximizing damage immediately, the Zeal Inquisitor can spend turns establishing defensive effects, absorbing punishment, and preparing a stronger retaliation.

Its main weakness is that it performs best when enemies are actually attacking it. Encounters where enemies ignore the Inquisitor or focus heavily on ranged attacks can reduce the value of its damage-storage mechanics.

### Progression

**Early:** Defensive abilities and self-healing establish the subclass as a durable frontline combatant.

**Mid:** Damage absorption and ally-protection tools become more effective, allowing the Inquisitor to deliberately take punishment while remaining active.

**End:** Stored damage can be released offensively, giving the subclass a strong defensive-to-offensive conversion while maintaining its durability.

**Best for:**

- Tanking
- Damage absorption
- Protecting allies
- Self-sustain
- Retaliatory damage

## Choosing an [[Inquisitor]]

|Subclass|Description|
|---|---|
|[[Tactics Inquisition]]|Choose it if you want to improve the entire party through Judgment-based buffs and coordinated attacks.|
|[[Vengeance Inquisition]]|Choose it if you want to specialize in hunting and eliminating high-priority single targets.|
|[[Zeal Inquisition]]|Choose it if you want a durable frontline Inquisitor that turns incoming damage into power.|

The [[Inquisitor]] is a divine martial hunter that uses Judgment to either empower allies, destroy priority targets, or absorb punishment and turn it against enemies.
