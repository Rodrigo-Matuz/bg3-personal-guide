# [[Monk]]

The [[Monk]] is a highly mobile martial class that uses unarmed strikes, weapons, movement, and Ki-based abilities to control the battlefield. Its core strengths are action efficiency, mobility, multiple attacks, and the ability to spend Ki to add damage, defense, or utility to its turns.

Unlike conventional weapon-based martial classes, the Monk can fight effectively without relying on heavy equipment. Its subclasses determine whether it specializes in pure unarmed damage, elemental abilities, stealth, healing, defensive play, ranged attacks, or supernatural effects.

Monks are particularly effective when they can move freely around the battlefield. They can reach vulnerable targets, attack multiple times, disengage from dangerous positions, and use control effects to disrupt enemies. Their main limitation is resource management: many of their strongest abilities consume Ki, so careless use can leave the Monk with fewer options later in an encounter.

In combat, [[Monk]] can fill several different roles depending on its subclass:

- **Melee damage dealer** — Uses multiple attacks and powerful unarmed strikes to quickly eliminate targets.
    
- **Mobile skirmisher** — Reaches vulnerable enemies and escapes before opponents can punish it.
    
- **Crowd controller** — Uses martial abilities and Ki effects to disable or reposition enemies.
    
- **Tank/bruiser** — Uses defensive abilities and durability to remain in melee.
    
- **Support** — Heals, protects, or enhances allies through specialized subclass abilities.
    
- **Elemental damage dealer** — Adds elemental or radiant damage to martial attacks.
    
- **Stealth specialist** — Uses darkness, invisibility, and mobility to approach enemies from advantageous positions.

## [[Way of the Ascendant Dragon]]

The [[Way of the Ascendant Dragon]] turns the Monk into a **draconic elemental martial artist**. It channels acid, cold, fire, lightning, and poison through its attacks while gaining breath-based and dragon-themed abilities.

Its ability to change elemental damage types makes it particularly adaptable when facing enemies with different resistances.

### Combat

The [[Way of the Ascendant Dragon]] is an **elementally flexible melee damage dealer**.

Its martial attacks can be enhanced with different elemental damage types, allowing you to choose an appropriate element for the encounter. Breath attacks provide an additional area-damage option when several enemies are grouped together.

The subclass remains primarily martial, so its elemental abilities supplement rather than replace its normal attacks. This makes it less dependent on spell slots than a conventional elemental caster.

### Progression

**Early:** Gains basic draconic abilities and elemental options that enhance normal Monk combat.

**Mid:** Stronger elemental attacks and breath abilities provide more ways to deal with groups and resistant enemies.

**End:** Becomes a flexible elemental martial capable of changing damage types and combining strong melee attacks with powerful area effects.

**Best for:**

- Elemental damage
    
- Flexible damage types
    
- Breath attacks
    
- Martial characters with dragon abilities
    
- Adaptable melee builds

## [[Way of the Astral Self]]

The [[Way of the Astral Self]] manifests an **astral body** that changes how the Monk attacks and interacts with the battlefield. Its supernatural limbs extend its reach and allow it to make greater use of Wisdom while dealing force damage.

It is an excellent choice for a Monk who wants its unarmed combat to have a distinctly supernatural identity.

### Combat

The [[Way of the Astral Self]] is an **enhanced unarmed combat specialist**.

Its astral abilities improve the Monk's reach and provide additional offensive and defensive tools. Force damage also gives the subclass a useful damage type that is less commonly resisted than many elemental types.

The subclass rewards staying active in melee and taking advantage of its extended reach. It can attack enemies from positions that would normally be outside standard melee range while maintaining the Monk's usual mobility.

### Progression

**Early:** Astral abilities enhance reach and provide new options for unarmed attacks.

**Mid:** Improved astral features increase both offensive output and defensive utility.

**End:** Becomes a powerful supernatural unarmed fighter with strong reach, force damage, and Wisdom-based capabilities.

**Best for:**

- Unarmed combat
    
- Force damage
    
- Extended melee reach
    
- Wisdom-based Monks
    
- Supernatural martial builds

## [[Way of the Brewmaster]]

The [[Way of the Brewmaster]] turns the Monk into a **durable frontliner** who uses alcohol and concoctions to improve survivability and combat performance.

Rather than maximizing burst damage, it focuses on absorbing punishment while remaining active in melee.

### Combat

The [[Way of the Brewmaster]] is a **durable Monk tank/bruiser**.

Its abilities allow it to withstand punishment and remain effective while surrounded by enemies. Concoctions provide additional effects that support its defensive playstyle.

The subclass works best when it stays close to enemies and uses its mobility and defensive tools together. It is more forgiving than fragile Monk builds, but generally sacrifices some offensive specialization in exchange for durability.

### Progression

**Early:** Establishes its defensive identity through tougher combat abilities and brewing mechanics.

**Mid:** Gains stronger ways to absorb damage and remain effective in prolonged fights.

**End:** Becomes a durable frontline Monk capable of surviving sustained pressure while continuing to attack.

**Best for:**

- Tanking
    
- Durable melee builds
    
- Frontline combat
    
- Defensive Monk gameplay
    
- Players who enjoy Brewmaster-style mechanics

## [[Way of the Drunken Master]]

The [[Way of the Drunken Master]] uses **unpredictable movement, intoxication, and disruptive martial techniques**. It is designed around moving through enemies and creating opportunities rather than standing in one place and trading attacks.

Its playstyle fits a highly mobile Monk that constantly changes position.

### Combat

The [[Way of the Drunken Master]] is a **mobile disruption specialist**.

Its abilities make it easier to move around enemies and exploit openings. Intoxication and related effects can weaken opponents while the Monk continues attacking and repositioning.

Rather than focusing entirely on maximum single-target damage, it gains value by disrupting groups and making enemies easier for the rest of the party to exploit.

### Progression

**Early:** Gains mobility and basic intoxication-based effects.

**Mid:** Stronger disruption abilities make it easier to move through groups and interfere with enemy actions.

**End:** Becomes a highly mobile battlefield nuisance capable of attacking, repositioning, and disrupting several enemies in quick succession.

**Best for:**

- Mobility
    
- Hit-and-run combat
    
- Enemy disruption
    
- Unpredictable melee combat
    
- Intoxication-based abilities

## [[Way of the Forge]]

The [[Way of the Forge]] combines martial arts with **fire and magical craftsmanship**. It adds offensive fire abilities while also providing ways to strengthen the equipment of allies.

This gives the Monk a useful combination of personal damage and party utility.

### Combat

The [[Way of the Forge]] is an **offensive/support hybrid**.

Fire abilities add another source of damage to the Monk's normal attacks, while equipment-enhancement abilities can improve the effectiveness of party members.

The subclass is useful when you want a Monk that contributes more than personal damage. Its support abilities can benefit weapon-focused allies while its own martial abilities remain effective in close combat.

### Progression

**Early:** Introduces fire-based attacks and basic equipment support.

**Mid:** Stronger fire effects and equipment improvements increase both personal and party effectiveness.

**End:** Becomes a capable melee damage dealer that also provides meaningful magical equipment support.

**Best for:**

- Fire damage
    
- Equipment enhancement
    
- Martial support
    
- Melee combat
    
- Hybrid Monk builds

## [[Way of the Four Elements]]

The [[Way of the Four Elements]] channels **fire, water, air, and earth** through Ki. It is the most traditional elemental Monk, providing ranged attacks, area damage, control, and utility alongside normal martial abilities.

It is a flexible choice for players who want a Monk with more magical options without becoming a full caster.

### Combat

The [[Way of the Four Elements]] is an **elemental hybrid**.

It can spend Ki on ranged elemental attacks, AoE effects, crowd control, and utility abilities. This gives it answers to situations where simply approaching an enemy and punching it is not ideal.

The major trade-off is Ki consumption. Elemental techniques compete for the same resource as other important Monk abilities, so efficient resource management is important.

### Progression

**Early:** Gains elemental techniques that expand the Monk beyond melee combat.

**Mid:** Stronger elemental attacks and control effects make the subclass more versatile.

**End:** Becomes a flexible elemental martial capable of dealing damage, controlling groups, attacking at range, and solving utility problems.

**Best for:**

- Elemental magic
    
- Ranged Monk attacks
    
- AoE
    
- Crowd control
    
- Versatile combat

## [[Way of the Kensei]]

The [[Way of the Kensei]] focuses on **weapon mastery** rather than supernatural effects. Selected weapons become extensions of the Monk's martial techniques, allowing it to specialize in precise melee or ranged attacks.

It is the best fit for players who want a weapon-focused Monk rather than an unarmed specialist.

### Combat

The [[Way of the Kensei]] is a **weapon-focused martial Monk**.

Its abilities improve the effectiveness of selected weapons, allowing the Monk to combine its normal mobility and attack economy with stronger weapon-based combat.

It can work with both melee and ranged weapons, giving the subclass more flexibility than a purely unarmed build.

### Progression

**Early:** Establishes weapon specialization and improves the Monk's effectiveness with selected weapons.

**Mid:** Gains stronger martial features and additional ways to enhance weapon attacks.

**End:** Becomes a highly efficient weapon-based Monk capable of maintaining strong damage while retaining the class's mobility.

**Best for:**

- Weapon-focused Monk builds
    
- Ranged weapons
    
- Precise martial combat
    
- High mobility
    
- Players who prefer weapons over unarmed strikes

## [[Way of the Long Death]]

The [[Way of the Long Death]] uses **necrotic energy, fear, and stolen life force** to make the Monk exceptionally difficult to kill.

It is one of the strongest choices for a Monk that wants to remain in dangerous melee situations for extended periods.

### Combat

The [[Way of the Long Death]] is a **durable necrotic bruiser**.

Temporary HP and life-draining abilities allow it to sustain itself while fighting. Fear effects provide additional crowd control, giving the Monk ways to weaken enemies while maintaining pressure.

The subclass becomes especially effective when surrounded by several enemies because its defensive mechanics allow it to continue fighting despite taking substantial damage.

### Progression

**Early:** Gains necrotic effects and defensive tools that improve survivability.

**Mid:** Stronger temporary HP, fear, and life-draining abilities make it increasingly difficult to kill.

**End:** Becomes a highly durable melee Monk capable of surviving dangerous encounters while using necrotic power against enemies.

**Best for:**

- Tanking
    
- Necrotic damage
    
- Self-sustain
    
- Fear effects
    
- Durable melee builds

## [[Way of the Mercy]]

The [[Way of the Mercy]] manipulates **life force**, using it either to heal allies or inflict necrotic wounds on enemies.

This makes it one of the most effective choices for a Monk that wants meaningful party support without abandoning martial combat.

### Combat

The [[Way of the Mercy]] is a **melee damage/support hybrid**.

It can use its martial abilities to attack enemies while spending its specialized techniques to either restore allies or apply debilitating effects to opponents.

This makes its action economy particularly flexible. A turn can be used for offensive pressure when the party is healthy or redirected toward healing when an ally is in danger.

### Progression

**Early:** Gains basic healing and necrotic techniques that establish the subclass's dual role.

**Mid:** Stronger healing and life-force manipulation make it increasingly useful in prolonged encounters.

**End:** Becomes a reliable melee combatant capable of simultaneously providing significant healing and offensive pressure.

**Best for:**

- Healing
    
- Martial support
    
- Necrotic damage
    
- Flexible combat roles
    
- Self-sufficient parties

## [[Way of the Mistweaver]]

The [[Way of the Mistweaver]] is a support-focused Monk based around **healing, restoration, and spiritual mist**. It retains the Monk's mobility and martial abilities while placing much greater emphasis on keeping allies alive.

It is particularly suited to players who want a healer that can still participate directly in melee.

### Combat

The [[Way of the Mistweaver]] is a **mobile melee healer**.

Its healing abilities allow it to restore allies while its martial abilities keep it active in the fight. Mobility is especially valuable because the Monk can move between injured party members without sacrificing the entire turn to repositioning.

The subclass generally gives up some offensive specialization in exchange for stronger support capabilities.

### Progression

**Early:** Establishes reliable healing while retaining the Monk's normal martial mobility.

**Mid:** Gains stronger restorative abilities and more ways to support allies during combat.

**End:** Becomes a highly mobile healer that can move around the battlefield while still contributing through martial attacks.

**Best for:**

- Healing
    
- Mobile support
    
- Melee healers
    
- Sustained party support
    
- Mistweaver-style gameplay

## [[Way of the Open Hand]]

The [[Way of the Open Hand]] is the most direct expression of **unarmed Monk combat**. It focuses on enhancing punches, spending Ki efficiently, and using martial techniques to deal damage and control enemies.

It is the simplest choice for players who want the Monk's core gameplay without adding a separate magical specialization.

### Combat

The [[Way of the Open Hand]] is a **pure melee damage and control specialist**.

Its primary strength is making unarmed attacks extremely effective. Multiple attacks allow it to generate substantial damage during a single turn, while control effects can knock enemies down, push them away, or otherwise disrupt their positioning.

The subclass gets excellent value from the Monk's Bonus Action economy because additional unarmed attacks can be combined with its normal attacks.

### Progression

**Early:** Strong unarmed attacks and basic control effects establish the subclass.

**Mid:** Improved martial techniques significantly increase damage and battlefield control.

**End:** Becomes an extremely effective unarmed damage dealer capable of producing high attack volume while controlling enemy positioning.

**Best for:**

- Unarmed combat
    
- High damage
    
- Multiple attacks
    
- Knockdowns and pushes
    
- Straightforward Monk gameplay

## [[Way of the Shadow]]

The [[Way of the Shadow]] specializes in **stealth, darkness, teleportation, and invisibility**. It turns the Monk into an infiltrator that can move through dangerous areas and appear behind enemies.

It is particularly useful outside combat because its mobility and stealth tools can help with scouting and infiltration.

### Combat

The [[Way of the Shadow]] is a **stealth-based mobile skirmisher**.

Darkness and invisibility allow it to approach enemies safely, while teleportation lets it rapidly move between advantageous positions. This makes it effective at reaching backline targets and escaping after attacking.

Its damage is still primarily martial, but its utility comes from deciding where and when the Monk engages.

### Progression

**Early:** Gains stealth and shadow abilities that improve infiltration and positioning.

**Mid:** Teleportation and stronger darkness/invisibility effects make the Monk increasingly difficult to pin down.

**End:** Becomes a highly mobile assassin-style character capable of repeatedly appearing in advantageous positions.

**Best for:**

- Stealth
    
- Assassination
    
- Teleportation
    
- Infiltration
    
- Hit-and-run combat

## [[Way of the Steel]]

The [[Way of the Steel]] focuses on **durability and direct physical combat**. It sacrifices the more specialized magical features of other Monk paths in exchange for stronger defenses and straightforward martial power.

### Combat

The [[Way of the Steel]] is a **durable melee bruiser**.

Its defensive abilities allow it to remain in combat longer while its physical attacks provide consistent damage. It is less dependent on elemental, magical, or complicated mechanics than many other Monk subclasses.

The subclass is best when you want the Monk to stay in melee and consistently trade attacks without relying on elaborate setups.

### Progression

**Early:** Gains defensive improvements that make direct melee combat safer.

**Mid:** Stronger physical abilities increase both durability and sustained damage.

**End:** Becomes a tough martial Monk capable of remaining in melee through prolonged encounters.

**Best for:**

- Durability
    
- Straightforward melee combat
    
- Physical damage
    
- Frontline bruisers
    
- Low-complexity builds

## [[Way of the Sun Soul]]

The [[Way of the Sun Soul]] channels Ki into **radiant energy**, allowing the Monk to attack effectively from range while retaining its normal melee capabilities.

It is one of the strongest choices for a Monk that wants ranged options without becoming a conventional spellcaster.

### Combat

The [[Way of the Sun Soul]] is a **ranged and melee hybrid**.

Radiant attacks give it a way to damage enemies without spending every turn moving into melee. This is particularly useful against flying, elevated, or otherwise difficult-to-reach targets.

Its AoE abilities also allow it to punish groups of enemies, while its normal martial attacks remain available when melee is preferable.

### Progression

**Early:** Gains ranged radiant attacks that immediately expand the Monk's engagement range.

**Mid:** Stronger radiant abilities and AoE effects improve its ranged damage.

**End:** Becomes a versatile radiant martial capable of switching between ranged attacks and high-volume melee combat.

**Best for:**

- Ranged Monk builds
    
- Radiant damage
    
- AoE
    
- Hybrid melee/ranged combat
    
- Fighting enemies at a distance
    

---

## [[Way of the Windwalker]]

The [[Way of the Windwalker]] focuses on **speed, wind, rapid attacks, and constant movement**. It is designed for players who want the Monk to maximize mobility and maintain pressure through repeated attacks.

### Combat

The [[Way of the Windwalker]] is a **high-mobility melee damage dealer**.

Wind-based abilities improve movement and allow the Monk to rapidly reposition between attacks. This makes it particularly effective against spread-out enemies and targets that would normally require multiple turns to reach.

Its gameplay rewards chaining attacks and movement together rather than standing still and repeatedly attacking one target.

### Progression

**Early:** Gains movement-focused abilities that improve the Monk's ability to engage and disengage.

**Mid:** Stronger wind effects and attack chains increase mobility and damage.

**End:** Becomes an extremely mobile martial character capable of crossing the battlefield quickly while maintaining strong melee pressure.

**Best for:**

- Maximum mobility
    
- Melee DPS
    
- Hit-and-run combat
    
- Wind abilities
    
- Rapid attack chains
