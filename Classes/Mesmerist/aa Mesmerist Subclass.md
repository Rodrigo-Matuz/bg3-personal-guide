### [[Aspect of the Daredevil]]

A fearless Mesmerist who turns **danger and reckless behavior into an advantage**, manipulating both enemies and the battlefield through supernatural confidence. **Psychic + physical abilities**, focused on aggressive play, risky maneuvers, forcing enemies to react, and exploiting moments of vulnerability.

---
### [[Aspect of the Eyebiter]]

A sinister Mesmerist who weaponizes **the power of their gaze**, attacking the mind simply by looking at their enemies. **Psychic + debuff magic**, specializing in fear, mental control, disabling effects, and punishing enemies who fall under their gaze.

---
### [[Aspect of the Trickster]]

A master of **illusions, deception, and psychological manipulation**, making enemies question what is real. **Psychic + illusion magic**, focused on confusion, misdirection, charms, mental debuffs, and controlling encounters without relying purely on physical damage.
