# [[Mesmerist]]

The [[Mesmerist]] is a psychic-focused class built around manipulating enemies through fear, deception, mental pressure, and supernatural influence. Rather than relying primarily on conventional weapon damage, it uses psychological effects to disrupt enemies and create openings for itself and the party.

The class is strongest when it can control the flow of an encounter instead of simply trading damage. Its subclasses determine whether that manipulation comes through reckless aggression, debilitating gaze attacks, or illusions and deception.

In combat, [[Mesmerist]] can fill several different roles depending on its subclass:

- **Crowd controller** — Disables, frightens, charms, or otherwise disrupts enemies.
- **Debuffer** — Weakens enemies and makes them easier for the party to defeat.
- **Psychic damage dealer** — Uses mental abilities to directly harm targets.
- **Battlefield manipulator** — Forces enemies to react or changes how they approach the fight.
- **Trickster** — Uses illusions and deception to create advantageous situations.
- **Aggressive disruptor** — Takes risks to force enemies into unfavorable positions.

---

## [[Aspect of the Daredevil]]

The [[Aspect of the Daredevil]] is built around **confidence, risk-taking, and aggressive psychological manipulation**. Instead of avoiding danger, it uses dangerous situations as opportunities to gain an advantage.

This makes it the most aggressive of the Mesmerist aspects. It is suited to players who want to stay active in combat, take calculated risks, and manipulate enemies while operating closer to the action.

### Combat

The [[Aspect of the Daredevil]] is an **aggressive psychic disruptor**.

Its abilities reward risky positioning and reckless maneuvers, allowing you to turn dangerous situations into opportunities. Rather than playing entirely from the backline, you can pressure enemies directly and force them to respond to you.

Its strength comes from creating openings rather than simply maximizing raw damage. You can manipulate enemy behavior, exploit moments of vulnerability, and use your own aggressive positioning to maintain pressure.

The drawback is that the subclass asks you to accept more risk than the other aspects. Poor positioning can leave you exposed, so defensive support from the party becomes more valuable.

### Progression

**Early:** Establishes an aggressive playstyle based around risky maneuvers and supernatural confidence.

**Mid:** Gains stronger ways to manipulate enemies and turn dangerous situations into advantages.

**End:** Becomes a highly disruptive combatant capable of creating openings through aggressive positioning and psychological pressure.

**Best for:**

- Aggressive playstyles
- Risk-reward combat
- Psychic abilities
- Enemy manipulation
- Close-range disruption

## [[Aspect of the Eyebiter]]

The [[Aspect of the Eyebiter]] focuses on **gaze-based psychic attacks**, using supernatural vision to directly interfere with the minds of enemies.

It is the most straightforward debuff-focused aspect, making it a good choice for players who want to disable dangerous targets rather than rely on deception or risky positioning.

### Combat

The [[Aspect of the Eyebiter]] is a **single-target control and debuff specialist**.

Its gaze abilities allow it to attack enemies mentally, with effects focused on fear, disabling conditions, and mental control. This makes it particularly effective against priority targets that need to be neutralized quickly.

Rather than spreading its power evenly across the battlefield, the Eyebiter gets significant value from identifying the most dangerous enemy and applying debilitating effects to them.

Its main limitation is that much of its value depends on successful mental effects. Enemies resistant to or capable of resisting its psychic abilities can reduce its effectiveness.

### Progression

**Early:** Gains basic gaze-based abilities for pressuring and weakening enemies.

**Mid:** Stronger fear and control effects make it increasingly effective at disabling priority targets.

**End:** Becomes a powerful psychic controller capable of severely disrupting dangerous enemies through its gaze.

**Best for:**

- Crowd control
- Fear effects
- Mental debuffs
- Single-target disruption
- Psychic damage

---

## [[Aspect of the Trickster]]

The [[Aspect of the Trickster]] specializes in **illusions, deception, and psychological manipulation**. It is the most utility-oriented aspect, using enemies' perceptions against them rather than relying exclusively on direct psychic attacks.

Outside combat, this approach also fits characters who prefer deception, misdirection, and unconventional solutions.

### Combat

The [[Aspect of the Trickster]] is a **battlefield manipulation specialist**.

Its abilities focus on confusion, charms, illusions, and mental debuffs. Instead of simply reducing an enemy's health, it can make enemies less effective or manipulate how they behave during an encounter.

This gives the subclass strong tactical potential. Creating confusion or misdirection can prevent enemies from focusing the party correctly, while charms and other mental effects can turn dangerous encounters into more manageable fights.

The main drawback is that its effectiveness depends on understanding enemy behavior and using its abilities at the right time. It is less straightforward than a damage-focused character.

### Progression

**Early:** Establishes its illusion and deception toolkit, providing basic ways to confuse and manipulate enemies.

**Mid:** Gains stronger mental effects and more reliable ways to control encounters.

**End:** Becomes a highly flexible psychological controller capable of manipulating enemies through illusions, charms, and debilitating effects.

**Best for:**

- Illusion magic
- Deception
- Charms
- Confusion
- Battlefield control
- Utility-focused characters
