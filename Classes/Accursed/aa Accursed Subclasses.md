## Accursed

A cursed warrior who has learned to **turn their affliction into a source of supernatural power**. Rather than simply trying to remove their curse, Accursed characters learn to suppress, control, and weaponize it against their enemies. The class is fundamentally a **half-caster focused on curses, debuffs, and weakening enemies**, but its subclasses can push it heavily toward martial combat, destructive magic, tanking, stealth, or other specialized roles.

The Accursed's core abilities revolve around manipulating curses. **Jinx** can impose disadvantage on an enemy's next attack or ability check, while their spellcasting provides a selection of curses, debuffs, and damaging effects such as [[Hex]], [[Bestow Curse]], [[Blindness]], [[Hold Person]], and [[Blight]]. They can also use their spell slots to **Afflict** enemies with their own curse or temporarily **Suppress** the negative effects of their personal curse.

Every Accursed also chooses **Malediction Metamorphoses**, which function somewhat like an invocation system. These can be tailored toward different playstyles, providing things such as bonus-action [[Hide]], improved [[Stealth]], additional [[Necrotic]] damage, improved [[AC]], temporary hit points, stronger [[Jinx]], or enhanced curse abilities. This gives the base class considerable flexibility even before considering the subclass.

The defining feature of the class, however, is also its greatest drawback: **your curse is never truly gone**. Each subclass comes with its own ailment that creates a disadvantage you must learn to manage. You can temporarily suppress these effects, but doing so consumes resources. In exchange, the curse grants increasingly powerful supernatural abilities as you level.

Your **subclass is chosen at Level 1**, giving you your defining curse immediately. Further subclass features are gained at **Levels 3, 5, 11, 15, and 20**, allowing your chosen curse to increasingly shape how you play the class.

Overall, Accursed is best thought of as a **flexible curse-based half-caster**. The base class weakens enemies and provides utility, while the subclass determines what you ultimately become: a supernatural weapon master, explosive fire mage, nearly unkillable tank, immortal mage or warrior, invisible assassin, or vampiric predator.

### Curse of the Armament

Bound to a **cursed weapon that refuses to leave your side**, turning you into a supernatural weapon master. **Physical + curse magic**, focused on martial combat, weapon enhancement, thrown weapons, and powerful Smite-like effects.

Your cursed weapon is effectively your signature companion. You have disadvantage when attacking with other weapons, but your weapon can always return to your hand when thrown. You gain martial weapon proficiency, bonus damage with your cursed weapon, **Extra Attack**, access to several Fighter-style fighting options, and eventually the ability to teleport to enemies after throwing your weapon. At Level 20, your cursed weapon becomes a +3 weapon and can make an additional attack whenever you cast a spell or make a weapon attack.

**Subclass progression:** Level 1 → cursed weapon; Level 3 → bonus weapon damage and ability to change your cursed weapon; Level 5 → Extra Attack; Level 11 → Fighting Style; Level 15 → teleport after throwing; Level 20 → enhanced +3 weapon and additional attacks.

### Curse of Combustion

An Accursed with an **unstable flame burning inside their body**, capable of unleashing devastating fire magic at the risk of losing control. **Physical + fire magic**, heavily focused on explosive AoE damage, burning enemies, and turning yourself into a walking magical bomb.

The major drawback is that taking damage can force you to make a Concentration save even when you aren't concentrating on a spell. Failing causes the flames inside you to erupt, damaging you and nearby creatures. You can deliberately release this power through **Flame Burst**, then later empower it with spell slots. At Level 5, you gain **Overheat**, allowing you to sacrifice your own health to double the Fire damage of your Combustion abilities. Later levels let you protect allies from your explosions, ignore Fire resistance, and eventually bypass Fire immunity when Overheat is active.

**Subclass progression:** Level 1 → unstable flame and Flame Burst; Level 3 → enhanced Flame Burst and melee flame attacks; Level 5 → Overheat; Level 11 → explosions avoid allies; Level 15 → ignore Fire resistance; Level 20 → massive additional Fire damage and Overheat bypasses Fire immunity.

### Curse of the Created

A **stitched-together creation animated by unnatural forces**, built to survive punishment that would kill an ordinary person. **Physical + lightning/defensive magic**, specializing in durability, heavy armor, shields, martial weapons, and surviving lethal damage through its unique resurrection-like mechanics.

The Created is the Accursed's natural **frontline/tank option**. You gain martial weapon and shield proficiency and possess **It's Alive!**, which prevents you from immediately dying when reduced to 0 HP. Instead, you erupt with Lightning damage and gain a large amount of temporary HP, allowing you to continue fighting. Later abilities make you resistant to Lightning, allow you to convert temporary HP into additional melee damage, and eventually give you a powerful transformation that constantly restores health and temporary HP.

**Subclass progression:** Level 1 → martial weapons, shields, and It's Alive!; Level 3 → Shocking Strike and an additional proficiency; Level 5 → Extra Attack; Level 11 → Lightning resistance and temporary-HP mechanics; Level 15 → redistribute physical ability scores; Level 20 → powerful enhanced combat state.

### Curse of Immortality

An Accursed who has **lived far beyond a normal lifespan**, accumulating fragments of knowledge and magical or martial ability over countless years. **Physical + curse magic**, with the unique ability to specialize into either a **Mage** or **Warrior** path.

Immortality comes with a major restriction: **you cannot be healed by spells cast by other creatures**. However, if you are still dead when the day ends, your curse resurrects you at dawn at full health and removes poisons and diseases. You can also eventually share this resurrection ability with another party member.

At Level 3, Immortality splits into two distinct paths. The **Mage path** gains Wizard cantrips and Wizard spells as you level, eventually adding your Charisma modifier to damage from Wizard cantrips. The **Warrior path** instead gains martial weapon and heavy armor proficiency, bonus damage on weapon and unarmed attacks, and eventually **Extra Attack**.

#### Mage

A supernatural spellcaster who has spent centuries accumulating magical knowledge. **Primarily arcane + curse magic**, with a strong emphasis on Wizard cantrips and spells while retaining the Accursed's debuff and curse toolkit.

#### Warrior

An ancient immortal who has **spent centuries mastering weapons and combat**, turning supernatural longevity into martial expertise. **Primarily physical + curse magic**, with martial weapons, heavy armor, Extra Attack, and increasing bonus damage on weapon and unarmed attacks.

### Curse of Invisibility

A cursed individual whose **very existence has been partially erased from sight**, making them extraordinarily difficult to detect. **Physical + illusion/invisibility magic**, specializing in stealth, disappearing during combat, avoiding attacks, and striking from concealment.

The Invisibility curse turns the Accursed into an evasive **stealth-oriented martial character**. You gain enhanced Stealth, martial melee weapon proficiency, and the ability to impose disadvantage on attacks against you when the attacker cannot see through your invisibility. At Level 3, you gain **Vanish**, allowing you to become invisible as a bonus action, alongside additional damage whenever you attack creatures that cannot see you or that you are successfully Hidden from.

As you level, Vanish becomes easier to use and your attacks from concealment become increasingly powerful. Eventually, you can make nearby allies invisible as well, and at Level 20 Vanish becomes essentially a sustained combat invisibility tool while your defensive reaction can impose disadvantage on attacks against you and grant advantage on saving throws against spells.

**Subclass progression:** Level 1 → enhanced Stealth and defensive reaction; Level 3 → Vanish and stealth-attack damage; Level 5 → stronger defensive reaction; Level 11 → Vanish recharges on Short Rest; Level 15 → temporarily share invisibility with allies; Level 20 → greatly enhanced Vanish and defenses.

### Curse of Vampirism

A supernatural predator afflicted with **vampiric hunger and a weakness to sunlight**. **Physical + necrotic/vampiric magic**, built around grappling, biting restrained enemies, draining their life, charming creatures, and eventually gaining the ability to heal from Necrotic damage.

Vampirism gives you Darkvision, Necrotic resistance, several martial weapon proficiencies, and a unique **Bite** attack. Sunlight is your primary weakness, imposing disadvantages when you or your target are exposed to direct sunlight. The real strength of the subclass comes from combining **grappling with your Bite**: biting a Grappled, Restrained, or Incapacitated creature deals additional Necrotic damage and heals you.

At Level 5, you gain Extra Attack and can replace your second attack with an immediate Bite after successfully grappling a creature. Later levels improve your grappling ability, provide additional supernatural magic, and eventually allow you to fly. At Level 20, Necrotic damage against living creatures heals you for the damage dealt, turning your vampiric attacks into a powerful source of sustain.

**Subclass progression:** Level 1 → vampiric weaknesses, Darkvision, Necrotic resistance and Bite; Level 3 → enhanced Bite and Charm; Level 5 → Extra Attack and grapple/Bite combo; Level 11 → advantage on grappling checks; Level 15 → free once-per-long-rest [[Conjure Animals]]; Level 20 → flight and Necrotic damage-based healing.
