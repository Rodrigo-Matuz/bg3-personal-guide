# [[Accursed]]

The [[Accursed]] is a curse-powered half-caster that combines debuff magic with either martial combat, offensive spellcasting, defense, stealth, or supernatural abilities depending on its subclass. Its core toolkit revolves around weakening enemies with curses such as [[Jinx]], [[Afflict]], and various curse-infused attacks while using [[Malediction Metamorphosis]] choices to customize the character. The class uses Charisma as its default spellcasting ability and has access to medium armor, simple weapons, and a selection of curse-focused spells.

The defining feature of the class is that every subclass comes with an actual drawback. These ailments can be temporarily suppressed, or inflicted on enemies through [[Afflict]], but they are part of the class's identity rather than something you simply remove. This makes the [[Accursed]] a class built around turning a weakness into a combat advantage.

In combat, [[Accursed]] can fill several different roles depending on its subclass:

- **Martial damage dealer** — Uses weapons, Extra Attack, curse-enhanced strikes, or thrown attacks.
- **Blaster** — Focuses on powerful area-of-effect and elemental magic.
- **Tank/frontliner** — Uses heavy armor, shields, temporary hit points, and defensive curse mechanics.
- **Mage** — Combines Accursed debuffs with Wizard spells and cantrips.
- **Stealth attacker** — Uses invisibility and concealment to avoid attacks and deal increased damage from hiding.
- **Control/debuffer** — Weakens enemies with curses, disadvantage, movement penalties, silence, fear, and other debilitating effects.
- **Grappler** — Restrains enemies and exploits them with vampiric attacks.

## [[Curse of the Armament]]

The [[Curse of the Armament]] turns the [[Accursed]] into a dedicated weapon specialist bound to a supernatural weapon. The subclass is primarily martial, but its curse magic gives it access to weapon-enhancing and Smite-like spells. Its biggest restriction is also its defining mechanic: attacking with other weapons is heavily penalized, so your cursed weapon should remain the center of the build.

The weapon can be thrown and automatically returns, giving the subclass an unusually reliable ranged option without abandoning its melee identity. Later features improve its damage, defensive utility, mobility, and ability to attack multiple times.

### Combat

Play the [[Curse of the Armament]] as a straightforward weapon-focused frontliner. Use your cursed weapon for virtually every attack and take advantage of its extra damage and returning throw mechanic.

The subclass works particularly well when switching between melee and thrown attacks. You can attack from range, then eventually use the weapon's teleporting functionality to close the distance. Its fighting-style options also let you specialize in one-handed damage, two-handed weapons, or party protection.

Your Accursed spells are best used to complement your weapon attacks rather than replacing them. Weapon-enhancing spells and Smite-like effects give you strong ways to spend spell slots without turning the character into a conventional caster.

### Progression

**Early:** Establish yourself as a dedicated weapon user. Your cursed weapon is already reliable because it returns after being thrown, while the Accursed spell list gives you useful debuffs and utility.

**Mid:** Extra Attack significantly improves your consistent weapon damage. The additional cursed-weapon damage and improved access to weapon-enhancing spells make you a strong sustained martial character.

**End:** The subclass becomes extremely flexible. Fighting-style options improve your specialization, thrown attacks provide mobility, and the capstone allows additional cursed-weapon attacks alongside attacks or spells.

**Best for:**

- Dedicated melee damage
- Thrown-weapon builds
- Martial characters who still want curse magic
- Smite-style weapon attacks
- Weapon-focused frontliners

## [[Curse of Combustion]]

The [[Curse of Combustion]] turns the [[Accursed]] into a high-risk fire blaster. Your body contains an unstable flame that can erupt when you are damaged, while your subclass gives you a dedicated collection of fire spells and abilities.

The important distinction is that the subclass's strongest abilities can also hurt you. Taking damage can trigger your unstable flames, and [[Overheat]] deliberately sacrifices your own health to dramatically increase the damage of your fire abilities.

### Combat

The [[Curse of Combustion]] is built around area damage. Use [[Burning Hands]], [[Fireball]], [[Wall of Fire]], [[Flame Burst]], and other combustion abilities to hit multiple enemies at once.

[[Overheat]] is the main risk-versus-reward mechanic. You take fire damage yourself in exchange for greatly increasing the fire damage of the ability being used. This makes it extremely powerful when you can afford the health cost, but dangerous when already wounded.

Positioning matters more than with most casters because your uncontrolled explosions can initially affect nearby allies. Later progression removes this problem by allowing your combustion effects to avoid allies, making large-area attacks much safer. The subclass eventually ignores Fire resistance and can overcome Fire immunity when using [[Overheat]] at its highest progression.

### Progression

**Early:** You have strong area damage but must constantly manage the possibility of your unstable flames damaging you and nearby allies.

**Mid:** [[Overheat]] turns your fire spells into major burst-damage tools. Your health becomes another resource that can be spent for damage.

**End:** The subclass becomes a dedicated fire-damage specialist. Ally-safe explosions, resistance bypassing, and additional fire damage make its area damage much more reliable.

**Best for:**

- AoE damage
- Fire-focused casters
- High-risk/high-reward play
- Clearing groups of enemies
- Players who enjoy managing health as a resource

## [[Curse of the Created]]

The [[Curse of the Created]] is the defensive martial version of the [[Accursed]]. Built around unnatural durability, lightning, armor, and temporary hit points, it is designed to stand in the middle of combat and keep fighting after other characters would have fallen.

Its defining feature is [[It's Alive!]], which prevents you from simply being reduced to 0 hit points and instead gives you a large amount of temporary hit points while releasing a burst of lightning. Later features allow you to exploit lightning damage and temporary hit points as additional offensive resources.

### Combat

Play the [[Curse of the Created]] as a frontline tank that also deals respectable weapon damage. Heavy armor, shields, martial weapons, [[Extra Attack]], and temporary hit points give it the tools to remain in melee.

[[Shocking Strike]] provides additional lightning damage and encourages enemies to attack you instead of your allies. Later, lightning resistance and temporary hit points make incoming lightning damage potentially beneficial rather than purely harmful.

Your temporary hit points can also be converted into additional melee damage, creating an unusual resource loop: surviving damage can give you more durability, which can then be converted into offensive power.

### Progression

**Early:** You are already durable and difficult to kill. [[It's Alive!]] provides a powerful safety net when you would otherwise fall.

**Mid:** Extra Attack turns you into a reliable martial character while [[Shocking Strike]] and temporary hit points improve both offense and control.

**End:** The subclass becomes an extremely durable frontliner with strong lightning synergy, temporary-hit-point management, and a powerful defensive/offensive transformation.

**Best for:**

- Tanks
- Heavy armor builds
- Shield users
- Frontline damage
- Players who want a hard-to-kill character

## [[Curse of Immortality (Mage)]]

The [[Curse of Immortality (Mage)]] is the caster-oriented version of the immortal [[Accursed]]. It combines the class's curse and debuff toolkit with Wizard cantrips and spells, allowing you to build a versatile arcane caster without giving up the unique mechanics of the [[Accursed]].

The major drawback is that other characters cannot normally heal you with spells. Instead, you rely on your own healing and your unusual ability to return to life at dawn if you die and remain dead until the day ends.

### Combat

Play the [[Curse of Immortality (Mage)]] as a flexible caster. Your Accursed spells provide curses, debuffs, control, and utility, while your Wizard spells give you another layer of offensive and utility options.

The subclass is particularly useful when you want your caster to have answers for different situations rather than specializing in one damage type. Wizard cantrips also receive additional damage from the subclass as you progress.

Your inability to receive normal magical healing means you need to manage your own survivability carefully. The resurrection mechanic makes death less permanent, but dying still imposes a temporary penalty after you return.

### Progression

**Early:** You have fewer Wizard tools but already benefit from additional cantrips, skills, weapon proficiency, and the Accursed's curse-based spellcasting.

**Mid:** Your Wizard spell selection expands while your cantrips gain additional damage. You become a versatile caster capable of mixing arcane magic with curse effects.

**End:** Your large collection of magical tools makes you one of the most flexible Accursed variants, with self-sustain and a powerful resurrection mechanic providing additional safety.

**Best for:**

- Arcane casters
- Spell versatility
- Debuff-focused mages
- Players who want Wizard magic without playing a pure Wizard
- Utility-heavy characters

## [[Curse of Immortality (Warrior)]]

The [[Curse of Immortality (Warrior)]] turns the immortal [[Accursed]] into a heavily armored martial character. Instead of spending centuries studying magic, this version has spent that time mastering weapons and physical combat.

It combines heavy armor and martial weapon proficiency with [[Extra Attack]] and increasing bonus damage on weapon and unarmed attacks. Like the Mage version, it cannot receive normal healing spells from other characters and instead relies on its own healing and immortality.

### Combat

The [[Curse of Immortality (Warrior)]] is a straightforward frontliner. Equip heavy armor, use martial weapons, and repeatedly attack while applying Accursed curses and debuffs when appropriate.

Its bonus-action damage feature gives individual attacks additional impact, while [[Extra Attack]] provides the action economy needed for sustained martial damage. Unlike the [[Curse of the Armament]], you are not locked into a single cursed weapon, giving you considerably more freedom when choosing equipment.

### Progression

**Early:** You begin as a durable martial character with flexible weapon choices and the ability to enhance your attacks.

**Mid:** [[Extra Attack]] significantly improves your sustained damage, while your growing curse toolkit gives you more options than a conventional fighter.

**End:** Your attack-enhancing damage reaches its strongest point, while immortality and self-healing give you exceptional long-term survivability.

**Best for:**

- Heavy armor warriors
- Weapon damage
- Durable frontliners
- Players who want martial combat without a weapon restriction
- Self-sufficient characters

## [[Curse of Invisibility]]

The [[Curse of Invisibility]] is the stealth and evasive version of the [[Accursed]]. Your partially invisible existence makes you difficult to detect and allows you to repeatedly disappear during combat.

The subclass combines martial weapon proficiency with invisibility, stealth bonuses, defensive reactions, and increased damage against enemies who cannot see you. It is particularly effective for players who want to control when they enter and leave combat rather than simply standing in front of enemies.

### Combat

The core combat loop is simple: disappear, position yourself, attack from concealment, and vanish again.

[[Vanish]] uses a bonus action to turn you invisible, while attacks against you can be disrupted through [[Disappearing Duellist]]. Attacking enemies who cannot see you or who you successfully hide from also adds substantial bonus damage.

This makes the subclass excellent at picking targets and avoiding retaliation. However, invisibility is not universal protection: enemies that can see through invisibility or do not rely on sight can bypass many of your defensive advantages.

### Progression

**Early:** You gain strong stealth tools and an effective defensive reaction, allowing you to avoid attacks while striking from concealment.

**Mid:** [[Vanish]] becomes the centerpiece of your combat rotation, while your hidden-attack damage increases substantially.

**End:** Vanish becomes much easier to maintain, and your defensive reaction provides broad protection against attacks and improves your spell-saving defenses while active.

**Best for:**

- Stealth characters
- Hit-and-run combat
- Assassination-style gameplay
- Avoiding enemy attacks
- Players who enjoy invisibility mechanics

## [[Curse of Vampirism]]

The [[Curse of Vampirism]] turns the [[Accursed]] into a melee predator built around grappling, biting, charm, and necrotic damage. It is a more specialized martial subclass that becomes increasingly effective when it can isolate and restrain individual targets.

The major drawback is sunlight. Fighting in direct sunlight imposes penalties to attacks and Perception, so positioning and battlefield conditions matter more than they do for most Accursed subclasses.

### Combat

The main combat loop is to grapple an enemy and then exploit that restrained target with your bite. Biting a Grappled, Restrained, or Incapacitated enemy deals additional necrotic damage and heals you, creating a direct connection between controlling an enemy and sustaining yourself.

At higher progression, your improved grappling ability makes this strategy more reliable. You also gain access to stronger supernatural mobility and control tools, while your necrotic abilities eventually allow you to heal for necrotic damage dealt to suitable targets.

The subclass is less effective against Undead and Constructs because its enhanced bite healing and necrotic effects do not function normally against them.

### Progression

**Early:** You gain Darkvision, necrotic resistance, a bite attack, and the basic tools needed to establish your predator playstyle.

**Mid:** [[Extra Attack]] and the ability to combine grappling with biting make the subclass much more effective against individual targets.

**End:** Improved grappling, additional supernatural abilities, flight, and necrotic-based healing turn the subclass into a highly self-sustaining melee predator.

**Best for:**

- Grappling
- Single-target damage
- Self-healing
- Necrotic damage
- Vampire-themed characters

## Choosing an [[Accursed]]

| Subclass | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Curse of the Armament | Choose it if you want a dedicated weapon master with cursed-weapon attacks and thrown-weapon utility. |
| Curse of Combustion | Choose it if you want explosive fire AoE and are willing to sacrifice health for more damage. |
| Curse of the Created | Choose it if you want a heavily armored tank that combines durability with lightning-enhanced martial attacks. |
| Curse of Immortality (Mage) | Choose it if you want a versatile caster combining Accursed curses with Wizard magic. |
| Curse of Immortality (Warrior) | Choose it if you want a self-sufficient heavy-armor warrior with strong sustained weapon damage. |
| Curse of Invisibility | Choose it if you want stealth, invisibility, evasive combat, and attacks from concealment. |
| Curse of Vampirism | Choose it if you want a grappling melee predator that drains enemies to sustain itself. |

The [[Accursed]] is a flexible curse-powered half-caster that trades conventional weaknesses for specialized supernatural power, with each subclass turning its curse into a completely different combat role.
