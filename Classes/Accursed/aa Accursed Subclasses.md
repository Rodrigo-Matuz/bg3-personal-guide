### [[Curse of the Armament]]

Bound to a **cursed weapon that refuses to leave your side**, turning you into a supernatural weapon master. **Physical + curse magic**, focused on martial combat, weapon enhancement, thrown weapons, and powerful Smite-like effects. Your cursed weapon is essentially your signature companion.

---
### [[Curse of Combustion]]

An Accursed with an **unstable flame burning inside their body**, capable of unleashing devastating fire magic at the risk of losing control. **Physical + fire magic**, heavily focused on explosive AoE damage, burning enemies, and turning yourself into a walking magical bomb. The downside is that your own power can literally become dangerous to you.

---
### [[Curse of the Created]]

A **stitched-together creation animated by unnatural forces**, built to survive punishment that would kill an ordinary person. **Physical + lightning/defensive magic**, specializing in durability, heavy armor, shields, martial weapons, and surviving lethal damage through its unique resurrection-like mechanics.

---
### [[Curse of Immortality (Mage)]]

An Accursed who has **lived far beyond a normal lifespan**, accumulating fragments of knowledge and magical ability over countless years. **Physical + arcane magic**, with a strong emphasis on Wizard spells and cantrips while retaining the Accursed's curse-based toolkit. Your immortality comes at the cost of being unable to receive normal magical healing from others.

---
### [[Curse of Immortality (Warrior)]]

An ancient immortal who has **spent centuries mastering weapons and combat**, turning supernatural longevity into martial expertise. **Primarily physical + curse magic**, with martial weapons, heavy armor, Extra Attack, and increasing bonus damage on weapon and unarmed attacks.

---
### [[Curse of Invisibility]]

A cursed individual whose **very existence has been partially erased from sight**, making them extraordinarily difficult to detect. **Physical + illusion/invisibility magic**, specializing in stealth, disappearing during combat, avoiding attacks, and striking from concealment.

---
### [[Curse of Vampirism]]

A supernatural predator afflicted with **vampiric hunger and a weakness to sunlight**. **Physical + necrotic/vampiric magic**, built around grappling, biting restrained enemies, draining their life, charming creatures, and eventually gaining the ability to heal from necrotic damage.
