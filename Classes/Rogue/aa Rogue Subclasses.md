# [[Rogue]]

[[Rogue]] is a highly mobile martial class built around precision damage, stealth, positioning, and utility. Its core mechanic is [[Sneak Attack]], allowing it to deal significant burst damage when attacking enemies under favorable conditions. [[Cunning Action]] also lets the Rogue use Bonus Actions to Dash, Disengage, or Hide, giving it exceptional control over its own positioning.

Unlike most frontline classes, [[Rogue]] does not need to stand and trade attacks with enemies. It is strongest when it chooses the target, attacks from an advantageous position, and then uses mobility or stealth to avoid retaliation. Its large skill selection and Expertise also make it one of the strongest classes for exploration, traps, locks, scouting, and other non-combat tasks.

The subclasses significantly change how the Rogue creates those advantages. Some focus on stealth and assassination, others on magic, social manipulation, mobility, dirty fighting, or unconventional utility.

In combat, [[Rogue]] can fill several different roles depending on its subclass:

- **Single-target damage dealer** — Uses [[Sneak Attack]] and precise weapon attacks to quickly eliminate priority targets.
- **Stealth specialist** — Uses Hide, darkness, invisibility, and positioning to attack enemies before they can respond.
- **Mobile skirmisher** — Uses [[Cunning Action]] and subclass mobility to attack vulnerable targets and escape retaliation.
- **Assassin** — Focuses on surprise, critical hits, and overwhelming enemies during the opening round.
- **Duelist** — Specializes in fighting individual enemies while avoiding Opportunity Attacks and maintaining mobility.
- **Battlefield controller** — Uses magic, curses, debuffs, or dirty tricks to disable and disrupt enemies.
- **Utility specialist** — Excels at locks, traps, scouting, stealth, pickpocketing, and other exploration tasks.
- **Party face** — Certain subclasses can use Charisma, deception, intimidation, and social manipulation to handle dialogue.
- **Ranged damage dealer** — Uses bows, crossbows, and other ranged weapons to apply Sneak Attack from a safe position.
- **Melee damage dealer** — Uses finesse weapons and mobility to deliver high-damage attacks at close range.

## [[Arcane Trickster]]

[[Arcane Trickster]] combines Rogue mobility and precision with a limited selection of Wizard magic. Its main purpose is not to become a full caster, but to use illusion, enchantment, and utility spells to create opportunities for [[Sneak Attack]] and solve problems that a conventional Rogue cannot. It is particularly useful for exploration because its magical abilities complement stealth, infiltration, and manipulation. In BG3, it is a one-third caster with Intelligence as its spellcasting ability.

**Combat**

The subclass works best when spells create an advantage rather than replacing your weapon attacks. [[Invisibility]], [[Mirror Image]], [[Hold Person]], [[Blur]], and similar spells can help you approach targets, survive retaliation, or disable enemies before following up with Rogue damage. Its spell slots are limited, so normal attacks and [[Sneak Attack]] remain the core of the build.

Its [[Mage Hand Legerdemain]] also provides an invisible, permanent [[Mage Hand]], giving the subclass additional utility outside direct combat.

**Progression**

**Early:** Gains basic illusion and enchantment magic alongside [[Mage Hand Legerdemain]], giving the Rogue new ways to manipulate encounters.

**Mid:** Gains stronger defensive and control spells such as [[Blur]], [[Hold Person]], [[Invisibility]], and [[Mirror Image]].

**End:** Becomes a flexible stealth-mage hybrid with more spell slots and improved magical utility while retaining full Rogue mobility and [[Sneak Attack]].

**Best for:**

- Magical utility
- Stealth and infiltration
- Illusion and enchantment
- Defensive spellcasting
- Setting up [[Sneak Attack]]
- Players who want a Rogue with limited magic

## [[Assassin]]

[[Assassin]] is built around eliminating enemies before they have a chance to fight back. It is the most specialized Rogue for ambushes, surprise rounds, critical hits, and opening-round burst damage. Its exploration style naturally revolves around stealth, scouting, positioning, and choosing the moment combat begins.

**Combat**

The subclass is strongest when you initiate combat from stealth or surprise. Its abilities reward attacking unaware enemies and can make the first round dramatically more dangerous than later rounds. Rather than gradually increasing its damage throughout a prolonged fight, the Assassin wants to remove one or more important targets immediately.

This makes target selection extremely important. Killing a dangerous spellcaster or high-damage enemy before they act can be more valuable than spreading damage across several targets.

**Progression**

**Early:** Establishes the ambush-focused playstyle with stronger opening attacks and improved initiative.

**Mid:** Becomes increasingly effective at exploiting surprise and vulnerable targets.

**End:** Functions as a dedicated burst-damage Rogue capable of deleting priority enemies before the rest of the battlefield becomes active.

**Best for:**

- Stealth
- Surprise attacks
- Critical-hit builds
- Opening-round burst
- Eliminating priority targets
- Assassin-style gameplay

## [[Debonaire]]

[[Debonaire]] turns the Rogue into a social manipulator who uses charisma, deception, charm, and psychological pressure as extensions of its combat abilities. It is especially useful in dialogue-heavy campaigns, where the character can solve problems through persuasion or manipulation instead of direct combat.

**Combat**

The subclass relies on manipulating enemies rather than simply dealing maximum physical damage. Social and enchantment-style abilities can weaken or distract opponents while the Rogue uses [[Sneak Attack]] and mobility to exploit the resulting openings.

It is particularly effective when the battlefield allows you to isolate or manipulate individual enemies.

**Progression**

**Early:** Establishes the social-focused identity while adding basic combat tricks based on deception and manipulation.

**Mid:** Gains stronger ways to exploit enemy behavior and create favorable conditions for attacks.

**End:** Becomes a flexible social Rogue capable of combining strong skill utility with combat manipulation and precision damage.

**Best for:**

- Dialogue-heavy characters
- Persuasion and deception
- Enchantment effects
- Manipulation
- Social utility
- Players who want a charismatic Rogue

## [[Fencer]]

[[Fencer]] is a dedicated duelist that focuses on precision, footwork, and controlling one-on-one engagements. It is less dependent on stealth than an [[Assassin]] and less dependent on magic than an [[Arcane Trickster]], instead relying on superior positioning and weapon technique.

**Combat**

The Fencer wants to isolate an important enemy and fight them without being surrounded. Its mobility and defensive abilities allow it to attack, reposition, and avoid unnecessary retaliation. Finesse weapons fit naturally into the subclass, allowing it to retain Rogue damage while fighting directly in melee.

**Progression**

**Early:** Establishes a mobile melee playstyle focused on precision attacks and safe positioning.

**Mid:** Gains stronger defensive and mobility options, making prolonged duels safer.

**End:** Becomes a highly effective one-on-one fighter that can remain in melee without needing to function like a traditional tank.

**Best for:**

- Melee Rogue builds
- One-on-one combat
- Finesse weapons
- High mobility
- Defensive dueling

## [[Grim Surgeon]]

[[Grim Surgeon]] treats anatomy as a combat system, using knowledge of the body to exploit weaknesses and inflict debilitating effects. Its theme fits a Rogue who approaches combat like surgery: identify the vulnerable point, apply the correct effect, and let precision do the rest.

**Combat**

The subclass adds medical and necrotic-style effects to the Rogue's normal precision damage. It can weaken enemies, exploit injured targets, and potentially manipulate conditions affecting allies or opponents.

It works particularly well when the party can already apply conditions or reduce enemy health, allowing the Grim Surgeon to capitalize on vulnerable targets.

**Progression**

**Early:** Adds anatomical and debilitating effects to conventional Rogue attacks.

**Mid:** Gains stronger ways to exploit enemy weaknesses and manipulate conditions.

**End:** Becomes a specialized debuff Rogue capable of combining high precision damage with medical and necrotic utility.

**Best for:**

- Debuffs
- Exploiting weakened enemies
- Necrotic-themed characters
- Anatomical/medical themes
- Precision combat

## [[Justiciar]]

[[Justiciar]] turns the Rogue toward darkness, stealth, and necrotic power. Its playstyle is particularly effective for characters who want to use [[Darkness]] and shadow-based abilities to control where enemies can see and fight.

**Combat**

The subclass uses darkness and stealth to create favorable attack conditions. Enemies struggling to see the Rogue can be attacked from protected positions, while shadow and necrotic effects provide additional ways to weaken opponents.

It works especially well with parties already built around darkness, stealth, or other effects that restrict enemy vision.

**Progression**

**Early:** Gains shadow-oriented tools that reinforce stealth and dark combat.

**Mid:** Develops stronger darkness, debuff, and necrotic options.

**End:** Becomes a specialized shadow Rogue capable of combining stealth, darkness, and precision damage to control engagements.

**Best for:**

- Darkness-based parties
- Stealth
- Necrotic damage
- Shadow magic
- Debuffing enemies
- Shar-themed characters

## [[Misfortune Bringer]]

[[Misfortune Bringer]] focuses on curses and manipulating probability against enemies. Rather than relying entirely on direct damage, it makes opponents less reliable and then uses Rogue burst damage to capitalize on their weakened state.

**Combat**

The subclass is strongest when its curses are applied before or alongside your main attack. Reducing enemy effectiveness makes the rest of the party more likely to land attacks and succeed against dangerous targets.

The Rogue's mobility remains important because curses do not replace [[Sneak Attack]]; they create better conditions for using it.

**Progression**

**Early:** Introduces curse-based debuffs that make individual enemies easier to handle.

**Mid:** Curses become stronger and more useful against dangerous targets.

**End:** Becomes a hybrid burst/debuff Rogue capable of weakening a priority target before finishing it with precision attacks.

**Best for:**

- Curse-focused builds
- Debuffs
- Single-target control
- Burst damage
- Occult-themed characters

## [[Ruffian]]

[[Ruffian]] abandons the traditional elegant Rogue in favor of brute force, dirty fighting, and aggressive close-range combat. It is designed for players who want Rogue utility without necessarily looking or playing like a conventional stealth assassin.

**Combat**

The subclass focuses on physical attacks and unconventional weapon choices. Strength-based combat and heavier weapons allow it to operate differently from the usual Dexterity-focused Rogue.

Its main advantage is straightforward physical pressure rather than complex magical or stealth mechanics.

**Progression**

**Early:** Establishes a tougher, more aggressive melee playstyle.

**Mid:** Gains stronger physical attacks and additional dirty-fighting options.

**End:** Becomes a durable Rogue bruiser capable of dealing substantial close-range damage while retaining Rogue utility.

**Best for:**

- Strength-based Rogues
- Dirty fighting
- Aggressive melee
- Unconventional weapons
- Physical damage

## [[Scavenger]]

[[Scavenger]] is the Rogue for players who want to turn equipment, consumables, traps, and the environment into combat resources. Its strength comes from resourcefulness rather than a single specialized combat mechanic.

**Combat**

The subclass can make better use of whatever happens to be available. Consumables, improvised weapons, traps, environmental hazards, and unusual equipment become additional tools in the Rogue's arsenal.

This makes it particularly effective in players' hands when they are willing to experiment instead of relying on the same attack every turn.

**Progression**

**Early:** Gains additional utility for using found equipment and unconventional resources.

**Mid:** Becomes more effective at turning consumables, traps, and environmental objects into advantages.

**End:** Functions as a highly adaptable Rogue whose effectiveness depends heavily on how creatively the player uses the game's available resources.

**Best for:**

- Item-heavy gameplay
- Traps
- Consumables
- Environmental interaction
- Exploration
- Improvised combat

## [[Swashbuckler]]

[[Swashbuckler]] is a highly mobile melee Rogue built around direct dueling. It trades some reliance on stealth for the ability to fight enemies face-to-face while still using Rogue mobility to avoid getting trapped. In BG3, [[Fancy Footwork]] prevents enemies you attack in melee from making Opportunity Attacks against you for the rest of your turn, while [[Rakish Audacity]] improves initiative and allows Sneak Attack against nearby enemies without requiring Advantage under its conditions.

**Combat**

The Swashbuckler can enter melee, land [[Sneak Attack]], and move away without necessarily spending its Bonus Action on [[Disengage]]. This is extremely useful because the Rogue can use its Bonus Action for other purposes while maintaining mobility.

It is particularly effective against isolated enemies and works well when you want the Rogue to remain active in melee rather than attacking exclusively from stealth or range.

**Progression**

**Early:** Gains strong melee mobility and improved initiative while making [[Sneak Attack]] easier to trigger against nearby isolated targets.

**Mid:** Develops additional dirty tricks and defensive options that make close-range combat more reliable.

**End:** Becomes a highly mobile melee Rogue capable of attacking several enemies while constantly repositioning around the battlefield.

**Best for:**

- Melee Rogue builds
- Hit-and-run combat
- One-on-one dueling
- High mobility
- Finesse weapons
- Players who dislike staying hidden

## [[Thief]]

[[Thief]] is the Rogue's most action-economy-focused subclass. Its defining advantage is gaining an additional Bonus Action, allowing the Rogue to perform more movement, off-hand attacks, item usage, or other Bonus Action abilities in the same turn.

**Combat**

The extra Bonus Action can dramatically increase the Rogue's flexibility. A Thief can use one Bonus Action for mobility while keeping another for an off-hand attack, or combine multiple Bonus Action abilities in ways other Rogues cannot.

This makes [[Thief]] especially effective with dual-wielding, consumables, mobility tools, and builds that have several strong Bonus Action options. Its strength is not one special attack but the ability to do more things within the same turn.

**Progression**

**Early:** Gains the additional Bonus Action that immediately changes how the Rogue manages its turns.

**Mid:** The extra action economy becomes increasingly valuable as better items, movement options, and Bonus Action abilities become available.

**End:** Becomes one of the most flexible Rogue subclasses, especially when the build is designed around exploiting multiple Bonus Actions every turn.

**Best for:**

- Dual-wielding
- Maximum action economy
- Item-heavy builds
- High mobility
- Consumables
- Bonus Action-heavy builds
