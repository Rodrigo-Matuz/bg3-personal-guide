### [[Arcane Trickster]]

A Rogue who combines **stealth and precision with illusion and enchantment magic**. **Physical + arcane magic**, specializing in trickery, magical utility, disabling enemies, and setting up devastating Sneak Attacks.

---
### [[Assassin]]

A deadly Rogue built around **surprise, critical hits, and eliminating targets before they can react**. **Purely physical**, specializing in stealth, opening-round burst damage, and exploiting vulnerable or unaware enemies.

---
### [[Debonaire]]

A charismatic Rogue who turns **charm, deception, and social manipulation** into combat advantages. **Physical + enchantment/social magic**, with a focus on persuasion, manipulation, debuffs, and exploiting enemies rather than simply overpowering them.

---
### [[Fencer]]

An elegant duelist specializing in **precision, footwork, and one-on-one combat**. **Purely physical**, focused on finesse weapons, mobility, defensive reactions, and overwhelming individual opponents through superior technique.

---
### [[Grim Surgeon]]

A sinister Rogue who treats **surgery, anatomy, and death as weapons**. **Physical + medical/necrotic abilities**, specializing in exploiting weaknesses, inflicting debilitating effects, and manipulating the condition of both allies and enemies.

---
### [[Justiciar]]

A shadowy Rogue inspired by the **dark justice of the Shar-worshipping Justiciars**, wielding stealth and supernatural darkness. **Physical + shadow/necrotic magic**, focused on darkness, debuffs, stealth, and punishing enemies from the shadows.

---
### [[Misfortune Bringer]]

A Rogue who specializes in **curses, bad luck, and manipulating the odds against their enemies**. **Physical + curse/occult magic**, weakening opponents with debilitating effects while using Rogue burst damage to finish them off.

---
### [[Ruffian]]

A rough-and-ready Rogue who fights with **brute force rather than elegance**, turning dirty fighting and heavy weapons into an advantage. **Primarily physical**, focused on Strength-based attacks, close-range damage, improvised tactics, and fighting dirty.

---
### [[Scavenger]]

A resourceful Rogue who turns **whatever they can find into weapons, tools, and advantages**. **Primarily physical + utility**, specializing in unconventional equipment, consumables, traps, and exploiting the environment.

---
### [[Swashbuckler]]

A flashy Rogue who dominates combat through **mobility, confidence, and stylish one-on-one swordplay**. **Purely physical**, specializing in hit-and-run attacks, dueling, disengaging safely, and fighting multiple enemies without being pinned down.

---
### [[Thief]]

A master of **speed, utility, and exploiting every advantage**, gaining additional opportunities to act during combat. **Purely physical**, with exceptional mobility, item usage, dual-wielding potential, and the ability to turn extra Bonus Actions into huge advantages.
