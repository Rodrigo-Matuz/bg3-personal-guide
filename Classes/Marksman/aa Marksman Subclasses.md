# [[Marksman]]

The [[Marksman]] is a ranged martial class built around dealing consistent damage from a distance while maintaining control over positioning. Its subclasses determine whether that ranged combat is purely physical, mobility-focused, or enhanced with spiritual or arcane magic.

The class is strongest when it can maintain distance from enemies and attack from advantageous positions. It generally has less frontline durability than dedicated melee classes, so positioning, elevation, line of sight, and movement are important parts of its gameplay.

In combat, [[Marksman]] can fill several different roles depending on its subclass:

- **Ranged damage dealer** — Consistently attacks enemies from a safe distance.
- **Mobile skirmisher** — Uses movement and positioning to stay difficult to engage.
- **Scout** — Exploits stealth and advantageous positions before combat and during encounters.
- **Ranged controller** — Uses special shots or magical effects to disrupt enemy positioning.
- **Hybrid damage dealer** — Combines ranged weapons with elemental, spiritual, or arcane abilities.

---

## [[Buccaneer]]

The [[Buccaneer]] is a mobile ranged fighter inspired by pirates and naval skirmishers. It focuses on firearms, trick shots, and aggressive positioning rather than simply standing still and firing. Outside combat, it fits characters who rely on mobility, opportunistic tactics, and a more unconventional approach to ranged combat.

### Combat

The [[Buccaneer]] is a **mobile ranged damage dealer**.

Its main strength is combining ranged weapon damage with movement and special attacks that interfere with enemy positioning. Firearms give it strong ranged pressure, while dirty tricks and trick shots provide additional ways to control the battlefield.

The subclass benefits from constantly repositioning rather than remaining in one location. You can attack from range, move away from approaching enemies, and use positioning-based abilities to keep opponents from reaching you.

Its main drawback is that it is still primarily a physical ranged character. It does not have the broad magical utility of the [[Shaman]] or [[Spellslinger]].

### Progression

**Early:** Establishes a strong ranged playstyle through firearms, mobility, and basic trick shots.

**Mid:** Gains stronger ranged attacks and more ways to manipulate enemy positioning.

**End:** Becomes a highly mobile ranged specialist capable of maintaining distance while consistently applying pressure.

**Best for:**

- Firearms
- Ranged damage
- Mobile combat
- Trick shots
- Pirate-themed characters

## [[Scout]]

The [[Scout]] is a ranged specialist focused on **stealth, exploration, mobility, and attacking from advantageous positions**. It is particularly effective for players who want their ranged character to contribute before combat even begins.

Outside combat, the subclass is one of the strongest choices for scouting ahead, finding enemies, and setting up favorable engagements.

### Combat

The [[Scout]] is a **positioning and stealth-based ranged attacker**.

Its main advantage comes from attacking enemies before they can close the distance. Stealth, movement, and long-range attacks allow you to choose where fights happen and exploit elevation, cover, and enemy positioning.

The subclass is less focused on special magical effects or direct battlefield control. Instead, it wins encounters through preparation and consistently favorable positioning.

This makes the Scout especially effective when you can begin combat from stealth or from a strong elevated position.

### Progression

**Early:** Strong mobility and ranged attacks establish the subclass as an effective scout and backline damage dealer.

**Mid:** Improved movement and stealth tools make it easier to consistently attack from advantageous positions.

**End:** Becomes highly effective at controlling engagement distance and eliminating targets before they can threaten the party.

**Best for:**

- Stealth
- Exploration
- Scouting
- Long-range attacks
- Ambushes
- High-mobility builds

## [[Shaman]]

The [[Shaman]] combines ranged weapon combat with **spiritual and elemental magic**. Instead of relying exclusively on physical projectiles, it channels supernatural forces through its attacks and abilities.

This gives the subclass more utility than a conventional ranged martial character, particularly when dealing with enemies that resist normal weapon damage.

### Combat

The [[Shaman]] is a **magical ranged hybrid**.

Its ranged attacks remain the foundation of the subclass, but spiritual effects, elemental damage, buffs, and summoned or spirit-based abilities give it additional ways to contribute.

This allows you to adapt to different encounters rather than relying exclusively on weapon damage. Elemental effects can provide alternative damage types, while buffs and spiritual abilities can improve the party's effectiveness.

The trade-off is that the Shaman has more resources and abilities to manage than a purely physical Marksman. You need to decide when using a magical ability provides more value than simply making another weapon attack.

### Progression

**Early:** Adds basic spiritual and elemental effects to the Marksman's ranged attacks.

**Mid:** Gains stronger supernatural abilities, buffs, and additional elemental options.

**End:** Becomes a flexible ranged hybrid capable of dealing weapon damage while supporting the party and adapting to different enemy resistances.

**Best for:**

- Ranged weapon combat
- Elemental damage
- Spiritual abilities
- Party support
- Hybrid martial/magic builds

## [[Spellslinger]]

The [[Spellslinger]] combines **ranged weapons with arcane magic**, treating magical projectiles and firearms as parts of the same combat style. It is the most offensively magical of the Marksman subclasses.

Outside combat, its arcane abilities also provide more utility than the purely physical subclasses.

### Combat

The [[Spellslinger]] is a **ranged magical damage dealer**.

Its core gameplay revolves around combining weapon attacks with arcane effects. Magical ammunition and spell-enhanced attacks give it additional damage and utility options, while direct spellcasting provides tools for situations where normal ranged attacks are less effective.

The subclass can switch between conventional ranged attacks and magical burst depending on the encounter. Battlefield-control spells also allow it to influence enemy positioning rather than focusing exclusively on damage.

Its main limitation is resource management. Unlike the [[Buccaneer]] or [[Scout]], it cannot rely exclusively on weapon attacks if it wants to make full use of its magical capabilities.

### Progression

**Early:** Combines ranged weapons with basic arcane effects, establishing the hybrid playstyle.

**Mid:** Gains stronger magical ammunition, spell-enhanced attacks, and additional ranged control options.

**End:** Becomes a powerful ranged hybrid capable of combining weapon damage with substantial arcane burst and battlefield control.

**Best for:**

- Ranged spellcasting
- Magical ammunition
- Burst damage
- Battlefield control
- Arcane ranged builds
