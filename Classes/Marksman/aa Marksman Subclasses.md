### [[Buccaneer]]

A mobile ranged fighter inspired by **pirates and naval skirmishers**, using firearms and dirty tricks to dominate from a distance. **Primarily physical**, focused on ranged weapon damage, mobility, trick shots, and controlling enemies through positioning.

---
### [[Scout]]

A highly mobile ranged specialist who excels at **exploration, stealth, and striking enemies from advantageous positions**. **Purely physical**, focused on long-range attacks, movement, stealth, scouting, and exploiting enemies before they can close the distance.

---
### [[Shaman]]

A Marksman who channels **spiritual and elemental forces** through their ranged attacks. **Physical + nature/spiritual magic**, combining ranged weapon damage with elemental effects, spirits, buffs, and supernatural utility.

---
### [[Spellslinger]]

A ranged combatant who combines **weapons with arcane spellcasting**, turning magical projectiles and firearms into one cohesive fighting style. **Physical + arcane magic**, focused on ranged burst damage, magical ammunition, spell-enhanced attacks, and battlefield control.
