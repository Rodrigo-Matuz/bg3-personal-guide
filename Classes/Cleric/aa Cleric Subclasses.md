# [[Cleric]]

The [[Cleric]] is a Wisdom-based full caster built around divine magic, healing, buffs, debuffs, and battlefield control. Unlike a pure support class, a Cleric can also deal substantial damage, wear armor, and fight in melee depending on its Domain. Its spell list gives it answers to many common threats, while [[Channel Divinity]] provides additional subclass-specific abilities that can be used without spending spell slots.

The [[Cleric]] is particularly valuable because its basic class features remain useful regardless of Domain. You retain access to healing, defensive spells, conditions, restoration, and powerful area effects while your Domain determines the additional spells, proficiencies, and abilities that shape your combat role.

In combat, [[Cleric]] can fill several different roles depending on its subclass:

- **Healer** — Restores HP, removes harmful conditions, and keeps allies fighting.
- **Support caster** — Provides buffs, defensive effects, and party-wide enhancements.
- **Damage caster** — Uses radiant, fire, lightning, necrotic, psychic, or other damage types.
- **Battlefield controller** — Uses conditions, fear, silence, darkness, forced movement, and other control effects.
- **Frontliner** — Uses armor, shields, weapons, and defensive divine magic.
- **Tank** — Protects nearby allies and reduces incoming damage.
- **Debuffer** — Weakens enemies through curses, disadvantage, and other negative effects.
- **Utility caster** — Uses specialized Domain spells to handle exploration and unusual encounters.
- **Anti-undead specialist** — Uses radiant and divine abilities particularly effectively against undead enemies.

## [[Ambition Domain]]

The [[Ambition Domain]] is an aggressive and deceptive Cleric that uses divine magic to pursue power rather than simply protect others. Its spell selection combines conventional Cleric support with shadow-oriented tools such as [[Disguise Self]], [[Mirror Image]], [[Bestow Curse]], and [[Vampiric Touch]].

In exploration, it is useful for deception and infiltration, particularly when disguises and illusion-based defenses are valuable. In combat, it plays more aggressively than traditional support Domains, using curses and defensive illusions to survive while applying pressure to important targets.

### Combat

The [[Ambition Domain]] is a **debuff-focused hybrid Cleric**. Rather than spending every turn healing or standing behind the party, it can use curses and shadow magic to weaken priority targets.

[[Mirror Image]] gives you a defensive option without requiring concentration, while [[Bestow Curse]] allows you to significantly interfere with an important enemy. [[Vampiric Touch]] gives you an offensive and self-sustaining option when fighting in closer range.

Its main strength is flexibility. You can still perform normal Cleric support when necessary, but you have more tools for attacking and manipulating enemies directly.

### Progression

**Early:** Disguise and defensive illusion spells give you utility and survivability while your normal Cleric spells handle support.

**Mid:** Curse-based abilities become more important, allowing you to weaken dangerous enemies rather than simply damaging them.

**End:** Stronger offensive and shadow-oriented spells let you function as an aggressive divine caster with good self-sustain and control.

**Best for:**

- Aggressive Clerics
- Debuffs
- Shadow magic
- Deception and infiltration
- Hybrid offense/support

## [[Arcana Domain]]

The [[Arcana Domain]] combines divine spellcasting with arcane knowledge. It is designed for Clerics who want access to additional magical tools normally associated with Wizards while retaining the Cleric's core support and defensive spell list.

In exploration, the Domain improves magical utility and knowledge-based problem solving. In combat, it gives you more options for damaging or controlling enemies, particularly when specialized arcane spells provide a better solution than standard Cleric magic.

### Combat

The [[Arcana Domain]] is a **versatile caster** rather than a dedicated frontline Cleric. Its additional arcane options allow you to cover damage types, utility effects, and magical situations that the standard Cleric spell list does not handle as well.

This makes it particularly useful in parties where you want one character to cover several magical roles. You can use your Action for offensive or control spells while retaining Cleric Bonus Actions and defensive options.

The drawback is that versatility does not automatically mean specialization. A dedicated Wizard will generally have deeper arcane specialization, while a dedicated Cleric will have stronger Domain-specific specialization.

### Progression

**Early:** Additional arcane cantrips and spells immediately expand your magical options.

**Mid:** Your larger spell selection makes it easier to adapt to enemies with specific weaknesses or resistances.

**End:** You become a highly flexible full caster capable of combining strong divine support with a broader arcane toolkit.

**Best for:**

- Spell versatility
- Arcane/divine hybrids
- Magical utility
- Mixed damage types
- Players who want a Cleric with Wizard-like options

## [[Death Domain]]

The [[Death Domain]] is the offensive necrotic Cleric. It turns divine magic toward killing, necrotic damage, and spreading death through groups of enemies.

In exploration, it is less utility-focused than several other Domains, but its supernatural identity can complement characters built around necromancy or darker divine powers. In combat, it is primarily an offensive caster that uses necrotic abilities and death-oriented spells to eliminate enemies quickly.

### Combat

The [[Death Domain]] focuses on **offensive spellcasting and necrotic damage**. Its subclass abilities improve your ability to kill enemies rather than protect them.

It is especially effective when you can exploit clustered enemies or vulnerable targets. Your normal Cleric spell list remains available, so you can still heal or support the party when necessary, but your Domain gives you more reasons to spend turns dealing damage.

The main drawback is that necrotic-focused abilities are less useful against enemies resistant or immune to necrotic damage. Keeping alternative damage types available prevents the subclass from becoming too dependent on one damage type.

### Progression

**Early:** Necrotic abilities immediately give your Cleric stronger offensive options.

**Mid:** Your damage and death-oriented spells become more effective against groups and priority targets.

**End:** You become a dedicated offensive divine caster with strong necrotic damage while retaining the Cleric's normal support toolkit.

**Best for:**

- Necrotic damage
- Offensive Clerics
- Necromantic themes
- Killing priority targets
- Damage-focused spellcasting

## [[Eldritch Domain]]

The [[Eldritch Domain]] is a Cleric that draws on forbidden and otherworldly powers. Its magic focuses on madness, psychic effects, supernatural knowledge, and manipulating the minds of enemies. The BG3 implementation is based on the Grim Hollow Eldritch Domain and is explicitly designed around invoking madness in enemies and gaining strange supernatural advantages.

In exploration, it provides an unusual magical identity and additional utility related to supernatural knowledge. In combat, it is primarily a control-oriented caster that uses mental effects instead of relying exclusively on physical or elemental damage.

### Combat

The [[Eldritch Domain]] is a **mental-control Cleric**. Its strongest contribution comes from disrupting enemies rather than simply reducing their HP.

Psychic and madness-themed abilities can make dangerous enemies less reliable, giving your party opportunities to focus on other targets. This is particularly useful when fighting enemies that are difficult to kill quickly but vulnerable to mental manipulation.

The subclass remains a full Cleric, so you can still switch into healing, defensive buffs, or conventional divine damage when an enemy is resistant to your eldritch abilities.

### Progression

**Early:** Eldritch abilities provide additional control and supernatural utility beyond normal Cleric magic.

**Mid:** Stronger mental effects allow you to interfere more reliably with enemy actions.

**End:** You become a specialized controller capable of combining divine support with powerful eldritch manipulation.

**Best for:**

- Psychic/mind control
- Madness effects
- Debuffing
- Supernatural-themed Clerics
- Crowd control

## [[Forge Domain]]

The [[Forge Domain]] is the equipment-focused martial Cleric. It combines divine magic with fire, armor, weapons, and defensive abilities, allowing the Cleric to operate much closer to the frontline than a conventional caster.

In exploration, its crafting and equipment-oriented identity fits characters who want their gear to be an important part of their power. In combat, it is one of the better choices for a durable armored Cleric.

### Combat

The [[Forge Domain]] is a **durable frontline Cleric**. Armor and defensive abilities allow you to remain in dangerous positions while continuing to cast divine spells.

Fire magic provides additional offensive options, while equipment-enhancing abilities improve the effectiveness of your armor or weapons. This gives the subclass a useful combination of physical and magical defenses.

You can still use normal Cleric concentration spells while fighting on the frontline, but positioning is important because losing concentration can reduce the value of your strongest buffs and control effects.

### Progression

**Early:** Defensive abilities and equipment enhancements make you substantially harder to kill than a typical caster.

**Mid:** Stronger fire magic and improved equipment allow you to contribute more directly to frontline combat.

**End:** The subclass becomes a durable battle Cleric capable of combining strong defenses, equipment bonuses, and divine spellcasting.

**Best for:**

- Frontline Clerics
- Heavy armor
- Defensive builds
- Fire damage
- Equipment-focused characters

## [[Grave Domain]]

The [[Grave Domain]] specializes in the boundary between life and death. Rather than simply maximizing healing, it focuses on preventing allies from dying and punishing enemies that are close to death.

In exploration, it provides useful divine and anti-undead capabilities. In combat, it is a reactive support Domain that becomes particularly valuable when allies are in serious danger or when enemies are already weakened.

### Combat

The [[Grave Domain]] is a **damage-prevention and emergency-healing Cleric**. Its abilities are designed around making sure allies survive dangerous situations rather than simply keeping everyone at maximum HP.

This makes reaction timing important. You gain more value from the Domain when you wait for a genuinely dangerous attack or vulnerable ally instead of automatically spending every defensive resource.

The subclass can also amplify damage against weakened enemies, allowing you to switch from defensive support to offensive pressure when an enemy is near death.

### Progression

**Early:** Emergency healing and death-prevention tools establish the subclass as a reactive support specialist.

**Mid:** Your ability to protect allies and punish weakened enemies becomes increasingly reliable.

**End:** You become extremely effective at preventing deaths while retaining strong Cleric healing and offensive magic.

**Best for:**

- Emergency healing
- Death prevention
- Anti-undead combat
- Damage amplification
- Reactive support

## [[Inquisition Domain]]

The [[Inquisition Domain]] is a dedicated mage hunter. It combines martial capabilities with divine anti-magic tools, allowing the Cleric to confront enemy spellcasters directly.

In exploration, it is useful for characters who want a combat-focused divine identity. In combat, it is strongest against magical enemies, where Silence, Banishment, and bonus Force damage against spellcasters give it a clear specialization.

### Combat

The [[Inquisition Domain]] is an **anti-caster frontline Cleric**. Martial weapons and heavy armor let you approach enemy spellcasters directly instead of trying to defeat them exclusively from range.

[[Silence]] can shut down enemy spellcasting in an area, while [[Banishment]] can temporarily remove a dangerous target from the battlefield. Specialized attacks against spellcasters add additional damage when fighting magical enemies.

The subclass is less specialized against purely physical enemies. Against ordinary warriors, much of its anti-magic toolkit becomes less important, so you rely more heavily on the standard Cleric spell list and martial abilities.

### Progression

**Early:** Martial equipment and anti-magic tools allow you to begin pressuring enemy spellcasters directly.

**Mid:** Silence and specialized attacks make you increasingly effective against magical encounters.

**End:** Banishment and stronger anti-caster abilities make you a dedicated threat to high-level spellcasters.

**Best for:**

- Anti-mage combat
- Heavy armor
- Martial Clerics
- Silence
- Spellcaster-heavy encounters

## [[Knowledge Domain]]

The [[Knowledge Domain]] is the utility-focused Cleric. It values information, skills, magical knowledge, and understanding enemies rather than specializing exclusively in damage or defense.

In exploration, this is one of the strongest Domains for covering skill checks and gathering information. In combat, it provides control and utility while retaining the full Cleric spell list.

### Combat

The [[Knowledge Domain]] is primarily a **utility and control caster**. Its value comes from having the correct tool available rather than maximizing one specific form of damage.

Its knowledge-oriented abilities can improve your ability to understand enemies and situations, while control spells let you interfere with important targets.

This makes it particularly good for a party that already has dedicated damage dealers and wants a Cleric who can fill whatever utility gap appears.

### Progression

**Early:** Skill and knowledge features immediately improve your exploration utility.

**Mid:** Additional magical tools and control effects allow you to contribute more consistently in difficult encounters.

**End:** Your combination of skills, information, and Cleric spellcasting makes you one of the most adaptable utility characters.

**Best for:**

- Skill coverage
- Exploration
- Utility
- Control
- Information-focused parties

## [[Life Domain]]

The [[Life Domain]] is the dedicated healing and protection Cleric. It focuses heavily on restoring HP, maintaining allies through prolonged encounters, and improving the effectiveness of healing magic.

In exploration, it is straightforward and reliable. In combat, it is the best fit when the party needs someone whose primary responsibility is keeping everyone alive.

### Combat

The [[Life Domain]] is a **dedicated healer/support**. Its healing improvements make ordinary healing spells substantially more valuable, allowing you to recover large amounts of HP without requiring excessive resource expenditure.

The important action-economy consideration is that healing is reactive. You generally do not want to spend every turn healing characters who are already safe. Instead, use your healing when it prevents a character from losing an action, falling unconscious, or dying.

You still have access to the full Cleric spell list, so you can cast buffs, control spells, or damage spells when healing is unnecessary.

### Progression

**Early:** Healing becomes significantly more efficient, making the party much more resilient.

**Mid:** Stronger healing and defensive support allow you to stabilize difficult encounters while conserving resources.

**End:** You become an exceptionally reliable dedicated healer capable of restoring large amounts of HP and keeping the entire party operational.

**Best for:**

- Dedicated healing
- Party protection
- Long encounters
- New players
- Support-focused parties

## [[Light Domain]]

The [[Light Domain]] specializes in radiant and fire damage while retaining the Cleric's defensive and support capabilities. It is one of the strongest options for a Cleric who wants to actively deal damage with spells rather than primarily supporting the party.

In exploration, its divine identity is straightforward. In combat, it is designed around powerful area damage, radiant effects, and defensive reactions.

### Combat

The [[Light Domain]] is an **offensive caster**. It has strong tools for damaging groups of enemies, particularly through radiant and fire spells.

Its defensive reaction can interfere with attacks against you or nearby allies, allowing you to remain useful even when an offensive spell is not the best choice.

The subclass is particularly effective when enemies are grouped together. You can use an AoE spell for your Action and continue providing defensive support through reactions.

### Progression

**Early:** Radiant and fire spells provide strong offensive options while your defensive reaction protects the party.

**Mid:** Your AoE capabilities expand, allowing you to clear groups more efficiently.

**End:** Powerful radiant and fire magic makes you a dedicated offensive Cleric with strong defensive utility.

**Best for:**

- AoE damage
- Radiant/fire magic
- Offensive Clerics
- Defensive reactions
- Group encounters

## [[Mind Domain]]

The [[Mind Domain]] combines divine magic with psychic power. It focuses on mental manipulation, psychic damage, and protecting allies from effects that target the mind.

In exploration, it provides a strong supernatural identity and mental utility. In combat, it is primarily a control and psychic-damage caster.

### Combat

The [[Mind Domain]] specializes in **mental warfare**. Psychic attacks provide an alternative to the Cleric's more traditional radiant, fire, or physical options, while mental control abilities can interfere with enemy actions.

Its defensive abilities are particularly useful when facing enemies that rely on fear, charm, confusion, or other mental effects.

The subclass is strongest when the encounter allows its psychic and mental effects to function reliably. Enemies with strong mental defenses can reduce the value of this specialization.

### Progression

**Early:** Psychic abilities provide additional damage and control options.

**Mid:** Mental manipulation becomes more effective, allowing you to interfere with enemy actions while protecting allies.

**End:** You become a dedicated psychic controller with strong defenses against mental effects.

**Best for:**

- Psychic damage
- Mental contro
- Anti-charm/fear utility
- Debuffing
- Supernatural Clerics

## [[Nature Domain]]

The [[Nature Domain]] combines divine magic with nature and elemental abilities. It provides tools for controlling terrain, dealing elemental damage, and interacting with natural environments.

In exploration, it is useful for characters who want additional nature-oriented utility while retaining Cleric spellcasting. In combat, it can control areas of the battlefield while using elemental damage to punish enemies.

### Combat

The [[Nature Domain]] is a **terrain-control hybrid**. Its strength comes from controlling where enemies can safely move and combining that control with elemental effects.

This can be particularly effective when your party benefits from keeping enemies at range or forcing them through hazardous areas.

It is less specialized than Light or Tempest for direct elemental damage, but its combination of control, defense, and elemental effects makes it more flexible.

### Progression

**Early:** Nature-based abilities provide defensive and elemental utility.

**Mid:** Terrain and elemental control become more impactful in larger encounters.

**End:** You become a flexible battlefield controller capable of combining divine magic with environmental effects.

**Best for:**

- Terrain control
- Elemental damage
- Defensive utility
- Nature-themed parties
- Battlefield manipulation

## [[Night Domain]]

The [[Night Domain]] uses darkness and shadow as both offensive and defensive tools. It specializes in controlling visibility, protecting allies inside darkness, and exploiting enemies that cannot see through your magical concealment.

In exploration, it is particularly useful for stealth-oriented parties. In combat, it can fundamentally change how an encounter works by controlling who can see whom.

### Combat

The [[Night Domain]] is a **darkness and stealth controller**. [[Darkness]] can deny enemy ranged attacks and interfere with line of sight, while your own party can be built around fighting effectively within or around the darkness.

[[Greater Invisibility]] gives you a strong defensive and offensive option later, while [[Fear]] provides direct crowd control.

The main drawback is party compatibility. Darkness can also interfere with your allies if their attacks require line of sight, so the Domain works best when the rest of the party can exploit the same conditions.

### Progression

**Early:** Darkness provides immediate battlefield control and can shut down enemies that rely on sight.

**Mid:** Fear and stronger shadow abilities give you additional ways to control encounters.

**End:** Greater Invisibility and stronger darkness-based tools make you exceptionally effective at stealth and concealment.

**Best for:**

- Stealth parties
- Darkness builds
- Invisibility
- Crowd control
- Ranged denial

## [[Order Domain]]

The [[Order Domain]] is a battlefield commander that uses divine authority to coordinate allies and punish enemies. It combines control effects with abilities that allow allies to make attacks or otherwise take advantage of your commands.

In exploration, it retains normal Cleric utility while fitting characters who prefer disciplined, command-oriented play. In combat, it becomes particularly effective when the party contains strong martial characters who can capitalize on its support.

### Combat

The [[Order Domain]] is a **support/controller**. Its core mechanic is making other party members more effective rather than maximizing its own damage.

When an effect allows an ally to make an attack, using it on a high-damage martial character can produce significantly more value than making a weak attack yourself. This makes party composition important.

The Domain also provides enchantment and control effects, allowing you to interfere with enemies when supporting allies is not the best use of your turn.

### Progression

**Early:** Command-based support gives your allies additional offensive opportunities.

**Mid:** Your ability to coordinate attacks and control enemies becomes more powerful.

**End:** You become a dedicated divine commander capable of turning a strong party into a more efficient combat unit.

**Best for:**

- Martial-heavy parties
- Ally attacks
- Battlefield coordination
- Enchantment
- Tactical support

## [[Peace Domain]]

The [[Peace Domain]] is built around keeping the party connected and improving the effectiveness of everyone at once. Rather than specializing in one type of damage or one defensive mechanic, it focuses on party-wide cooperation and damage mitigation.

In exploration, it remains a flexible support Cleric. In combat, it is strongest when several allies remain close enough to benefit from its protective and cooperative effects.

### Combat

The [[Peace Domain]] is a **party-wide support specialist**. Its strongest effects reward keeping allies together and allowing its bonuses to spread across the group.

This makes positioning important. A Peace Cleric gets more value when the party can operate as a coordinated group instead of scattering across the battlefield.

Its support can reduce incoming damage and improve important rolls while normal Cleric healing remains available when someone actually needs restoration.

### Progression

**Early:** Party-wide support effects immediately improve the group's consistency and survivability.

**Mid:** Damage mitigation and cooperative effects become stronger and more reliable.

**End:** You become one of the strongest dedicated party-support Clerics, particularly when the group stays coordinated.

**Best for:**

- Party-wide buffs
- Damage mitigation
- Coordinated parties
- Defensive support
- Team-oriented gameplay

## [[Penance Domain]]

The [[Penance Domain]] is a high-risk Cleric that becomes stronger as its own HP falls. Its subclass features reward fighting while injured and allow the Cleric to sacrifice HP to improve important Wisdom and Intelligence checks or saving throws. The BG3 implementation explicitly centers the subclass around abilities becoming stronger below 50% HP.

In exploration, its reaction-based support can help the party succeed on important mental checks. In combat, the subclass deliberately operates closer to danger than a conventional support Cleric.

### Combat

The [[Penance Domain]] is a **high-risk damage/support hybrid**. Below half HP, it gains additional damage on attacks, with the damage type depending on the character's alignment.

Its [[Prayer of Penance]] can also spend a Reaction and some of your HP to improve a Wisdom- or Intelligence-based check or saving throw for yourself or a nearby ally. This means your own health is effectively another resource.

The subclass rewards careful HP management, but there is an important difference between deliberately staying injured and simply allowing yourself to die. You want to remain below the threshold long enough to exploit your bonuses without becoming so vulnerable that an enemy can finish you.

### Progression

**Early:** The below-half-HP damage bonus establishes the subclass's risk/reward playstyle while Prayer of Penance provides reactive support.

**Mid:** More powerful abilities make operating at low HP increasingly rewarding while giving you additional ways to turn suffering into power.

**End:** The subclass becomes a high-risk divine combatant that can deal strong damage while using its own HP to improve important party rolls.

**Best for:**

- High-risk gameplay
- Low-HP damage
- Reactive support
- Radiant/necrotic damage
- Players comfortable managing HP carefully

## [[Protection Domain]]

The [[Protection Domain]] is a dedicated defensive Cleric focused on preventing damage before it reaches allies. Its abilities are designed around shielding nearby party members, improving defenses, and reducing the effectiveness of enemy attacks.

In exploration, it provides conventional Cleric utility. In combat, its value increases when the party has vulnerable characters who need protection from concentrated enemy attacks.

### Combat

The [[Protection Domain]] is a **dedicated defensive support**. Its abilities allow you to reduce or redirect incoming damage rather than simply healing the party after they have already been injured.

Positioning is important because many protective effects depend on proximity. You should remain close enough to the allies you are protecting without exposing yourself unnecessarily.

The subclass works especially well alongside fragile damage dealers, allowing them to take greater risks while you provide defensive coverage.

### Progression

**Early:** Defensive abilities immediately improve the party's survivability.

**Mid:** Stronger protection allows you to mitigate larger amounts of incoming damage and protect multiple allies more effectively.

**End:** You become a dedicated defensive anchor capable of significantly reducing the party's dependence on raw healing.

**Best for:**

- Damage mitigation
- Protecting fragile allies
- Defensive support
- Tank/support hybrids
- Survival-focused parties

## [[Solidarity Domain]]

The [[Solidarity Domain]] focuses on cooperation and strengthening the group rather than maximizing the Cleric's individual power. This is the Amonkhet implementation of the Domain and is designed around turning coordinated party play into a mechanical advantage.

In exploration, it functions as a normal versatile Cleric. In combat, it is strongest when several allies can benefit from the same support effects and remain coordinated.

### Combat

The [[Solidarity Domain]] is a **team-oriented buffer**. Its abilities reward having multiple allies contribute to the same encounter rather than relying on one character to carry the fight.

The Cleric generally spends its Action on support or control while allies use their own Actions to deal damage. This makes the Domain particularly effective when paired with characters that have strong individual turns.

Unlike a dedicated healer, Solidarity aims to prevent problems and improve party performance before healing becomes necessary.

### Progression

**Early:** Party-support abilities improve the effectiveness of coordinated allies.

**Mid:** Stronger buffs allow multiple party members to benefit from the Domain simultaneously.

**End:** The subclass becomes a dedicated group enhancer that rewards coordinated party composition and positioning.

**Best for:**

- Party buffs
- Teamwork
- Coordinated parties
- Martial-heavy groups
- Support-focused gameplay

## [[Strength Domain]]

The [[Strength Domain]] combines divine magic with physical enhancement. It is designed for Clerics who want to use Strength, athleticism, and direct physical combat rather than relying entirely on spellcasting.

In exploration, it improves physical utility and allows the Cleric to handle strength-based obstacles more effectively. In combat, it plays as a martial-support hybrid with divine enhancements.

### Combat

The [[Strength Domain]] is a **physical enhancement Cleric**. Its spells and abilities improve physical performance, allowing you to hit harder, move more effectively, and perform better at strength-based tasks.

You can fight in melee while still retaining Cleric spellcasting. This makes it useful when you want your Cleric to remain active in the frontline without committing completely to the War Domain.

### Progression

**Early:** Strength-enhancing abilities improve your physical combat and exploration capabilities.

**Mid:** Stronger enhancements make your martial contribution more meaningful while your Cleric spells remain available.

**End:** You become a powerful physical hybrid capable of combining divine magic with exceptional Strength-based combat.

**Best for:**

- Strength-based Clerics
- Physical enhancement
- Melee hybrids
- Athletic utility
- Frontline support

## [[Tempest Domain]]

The [[Tempest Domain]] is the dedicated lightning and thunder Cleric. It combines powerful elemental damage with battlefield control and the Cleric's normal defensive and support magic.

In exploration, it has less specialized utility than Knowledge or Trickery, but in combat it is one of the strongest choices for explosive elemental damage.

### Combat

The [[Tempest Domain]] is an **elemental blaster**. Lightning and thunder spells provide strong burst damage, while the Domain's abilities can punish enemies and manipulate their positioning.

Its major strength is burst damage. When an ability allows you to maximize or substantially increase lightning or thunder damage, using it on a high-damage spell can produce a very large spike of damage.

This makes resource timing important. Your strongest Channel Divinity and spell combinations should generally be reserved for important groups or high-value targets rather than spent on minor enemies.

### Progression

**Early:** Lightning and thunder spells provide strong offensive options and establish the subclass as an elemental damage dealer.

**Mid:** Stronger elemental spells and Channel Divinity greatly increase burst potential.

**End:** You become a powerful lightning/thunder caster capable of combining high burst damage with Cleric support and control.

**Best for:**

- Lightning damage
- Thunder damage
- Burst AoE
- Elemental spellcasting
- Offensive Clerics

## [[Trickery Domain]]

The [[Trickery Domain]] focuses on deception, illusion, stealth, and misdirection. It is designed for Clerics who prefer avoiding direct confrontation or manipulating enemy positioning rather than simply overpowering enemies.

In exploration, it is particularly useful for stealth and infiltration. In combat, it can create distractions, improve stealth, and use illusion-based effects to control how enemies perceive the battlefield.

### Combat

The [[Trickery Domain]] is a **stealth and deception specialist**. Its abilities allow you to approach encounters indirectly and create opportunities for the party to attack from advantageous positions.

Its biggest advantage is utility rather than raw damage. It can help a party avoid unnecessary fights, infiltrate dangerous locations, or establish favorable positions before combat begins.

In direct combat, however, it generally lacks the raw offensive specialization of Light or Tempest and the defensive specialization of Life or Protection.

### Progression

**Early:** Stealth and illusion tools immediately improve infiltration and positioning.

**Mid:** Stronger deception and control effects make it easier to manipulate encounters.

**End:** The subclass becomes a highly effective stealth-oriented Cleric capable of supporting unconventional approaches to combat and exploration.

**Best for:**

- Stealth
- Infiltration
- Illusion
- Deception
- Ambushes

## [[Twilight Domain]]

The [[Twilight Domain]] protects allies through defensive twilight magic, temporary HP, and strong party-wide support. It is designed for prolonged encounters where preventing damage and maintaining the party's effective HP matters more than maximizing burst damage.

In exploration, it provides useful darkvision and night-oriented utility. In combat, it functions as a defensive support anchor that keeps the party operational over many rounds.

### Combat

The [[Twilight Domain]] is a **defensive support Cleric**. Its temporary HP and protective abilities allow you to reduce the amount of healing the party needs during an encounter.

This is especially useful when multiple allies are taking moderate amounts of damage every round. Instead of repeatedly spending Actions on emergency healing, you can maintain a protective effect and allow allies to continue attacking.

Its defensive tools also complement concentration spells well because preventing damage makes it easier for you to maintain important buffs and control effects.

### Progression

**Early:** Darkvision and defensive twilight abilities improve party utility and survivability.

**Mid:** Temporary HP and protective effects become increasingly effective across prolonged encounters.

**End:** The subclass becomes an exceptionally strong defensive support Cleric capable of keeping several allies protected simultaneously.

**Best for:**

- Temporary HP
- Defensive support
- Long encounters
- Party survivability
- Darkvision and night utility

## [[War Domain]]

The [[War Domain]] is the dedicated martial Cleric. It combines divine spellcasting with martial weapons, armor, offensive buffs, and additional weapon attacks, allowing the Cleric to function alongside dedicated martial characters.

In exploration, it remains a versatile Cleric, but its physical capabilities make it more comfortable handling weapon-based situations. In combat, it is designed to remain in the frontline and contribute meaningful weapon damage while still casting divine spells.

### Combat

The [[War Domain]] is a **martial damage/support hybrid**. It can use its Bonus Action for additional weapon attacks under the appropriate conditions, giving it more offensive action economy than a conventional Cleric.

Its divine buffs can improve the performance of itself or other martial characters, while its armor and weapon proficiencies allow it to safely remain near the frontline.

The main limitation is that your Bonus Action can become crowded. You may want to use it for an extra attack, healing, movement, or another Cleric ability, so choosing the most valuable option each turn is important.

### Progression

**Early:** Martial weapons and armor immediately make you more capable in direct combat.

**Mid:** Additional weapon attacks and offensive buffs increase your sustained martial contribution.

**End:** You become a strong battle Cleric capable of combining repeated weapon attacks with powerful divine spellcasting.

**Best for:**

- Martial Clerics
- Frontline combat
- Weapon damage
- Offensive buffs
- Hybrid melee/caster builds

The [[Cleric]] is a highly adaptable Wisdom-based full caster whose Domain determines whether its divine power is primarily used for healing, protection, control, damage, utility, or frontline combat.
