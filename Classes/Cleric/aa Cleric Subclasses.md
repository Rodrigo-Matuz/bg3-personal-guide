### [[Ambition Domain]]

A Cleric devoted to **ambition, power, and the pursuit of personal greatness**, using divine magic to manipulate and overcome opponents. **Divine + shadow/debuff magic**, with tools such as Bane, Disguise Self, Mirror Image, Bestow Curse, and Vampiric Touch. A strong choice for a more aggressive, deceptive Cleric.

---
### [[Arcana Domain]]

A Cleric who studies **arcane magic through divine devotion**, blending the spellcasting of a Cleric with wizard-like magical knowledge. **Divine + arcane magic**, focused on magical utility, spell damage, and expanding your options against enemies that rely on magic.

---
### [[Death Domain]]

A dark Cleric who embraces **death and necrotic power**, turning divine magic into a weapon against the living. **Divine + necrotic magic**, specializing in killing enemies, dealing necrotic damage, and spreading death through powerful offensive abilities.

---
### [[Eldritch Domain]]

A Cleric who communes with **ancient and unknowable powers**, using forbidden knowledge to invoke madness and supernatural effects. **Divine + eldritch/psychic magic**, focused on manipulating minds, inflicting madness, and gaining strange supernatural advantages.

---
### [[Forge Domain]]

A Cleric devoted to **crafting, fire, and the creation of powerful equipment**, becoming exceptionally capable in frontline combat. **Physical + fire/divine magic**, with strong defenses, weapon and armor proficiency, and abilities that make you a durable battle Cleric.

---
### [[Grave Domain]]

A guardian of the **boundary between life and death**, specializing in preventing premature death and punishing creatures that disturb the natural order. **Divine + necrotic/healing magic**, offering strong healing, anti-undead tools, and ways to amplify damage against weakened enemies.

---
### [[Inquisition Domain]]

A dedicated **mage hunter** who believes arcane magic must be controlled and eradicated when it threatens the faithful. **Physical + divine/anti-magic abilities**, with martial weapons, heavy armor, Silence, Banishment, and specialized attacks that deal extra Force damage against spellcasters.

---
### [[Knowledge Domain]]

A scholarly Cleric who seeks **knowledge, secrets, and mastery of information**. **Primarily divine + utility magic**, excelling at skills, identifying threats, controlling enemies, and gaining magical tools that make the party more versatile.

---
### [[Life Domain]]

The ultimate **dedicated healer and protector**, channeling divine power into restoring and preserving the party. **Pure divine/healing magic**, focused on massive healing, support, buffs, and keeping allies alive through difficult fights.

---
### [[Light Domain]]

A Cleric who channels **radiant light and divine fire** to burn away darkness and enemies alike. **Divine + radiant/fire magic**, specializing in AoE damage, radiant attacks, defensive reactions, and destroying groups of enemies with powerful spellcasting.

---
### [[Mind Domain]]

A Cleric who combines **divine devotion with psychic power**, treating the mind as both their greatest weapon and greatest defense. **Divine + psychic/mental magic**, focused on mental manipulation, psychic damage, protecting allies from mental effects, and controlling enemies.

---
### [[Nature Domain]]

A Cleric who draws divine power from **nature, animals, and the natural world**. **Divine + nature/elemental magic**, with tools for controlling terrain, dealing elemental damage, supporting allies, and interacting with the environment.

---
### [[Night Domain]]

A Cleric who embraces the **cover of darkness**, using shadows to protect allies while obscuring and weakening enemies. **Divine + shadow/darkness magic**, featuring Darkness, Greater Invisibility, Fear, and unique night-themed spells. Excellent for stealth-oriented parties and controlling fights through darkness.

---
### [[Order Domain]]

A Cleric devoted to **law, discipline, and absolute divine authority**, commanding allies while punishing enemies who resist. **Divine + control/support magic**, focused on coordination, forced attacks, enchantments, and making the party fight as a disciplined unit.

---
### [[Peace Domain]]

A Cleric dedicated to **unity, cooperation, and protecting the bonds between allies**. **Divine + support magic**, specializing in party-wide buffs, damage mitigation, healing, and making the entire group stronger when fighting together.

---
### [[Penance Domain]]

A high-risk Cleric who turns **suffering and self-inflicted pain into divine power**. **Divine + radiant/necrotic magic**, becoming stronger while below half health and even sacrificing their own HP to improve allies' Wisdom or Intelligence checks and saving throws.

---
### [[Protection Domain]]

A dedicated **guardian Cleric** who uses divine power to shield allies from harm. **Divine + defensive magic**, focused on damage reduction, defensive buffs, protecting nearby allies, and making the party significantly harder to kill.

---
### [[Solidarity Domain]]

A Cleric devoted to **unity, teamwork, and the strength of the group**, empowering allies rather than standing alone. **Divine + support/buff magic**, designed around strengthening companions and turning coordinated party play into a major advantage. This is the Amonkhet implementation of the Solidarity Domain.

---
### [[Strength Domain]]

A Cleric who worships **physical might, athleticism, and overcoming obstacles through sheer strength**. **Physical + divine enhancement magic**, combining martial capability with buffs and abilities that make you stronger, tougher, and more effective in direct combat.

---
### [[Tempest Domain]]

A Cleric who commands the **raw fury of storms**, wielding thunder and lightning as divine weapons. **Divine + lightning/thunder magic**, specializing in explosive AoE damage, battlefield control, and devastating elemental attacks.

---
### [[Trickery Domain]]

A Cleric who embraces **deception, illusion, stealth, and misdirection** rather than meeting enemies head-on. **Divine + illusion/shadow magic**, excellent for stealth, disguises, creating distractions, and manipulating the battlefield from unexpected positions.

---
### [[Twilight Domain]]

A Cleric who stands between **light and darkness**, protecting allies through the calming power of twilight. **Divine + radiant/shadow magic**, focused on defensive buffs, temporary HP, protection, and keeping the party alive through prolonged encounters.

---
### [[War Domain]]

A battle-hardened Cleric who combines **divine spellcasting with martial weapon mastery**. **Physical + divine magic**, built for frontline combat, extra weapon attacks, offensive buffs, and fighting alongside the party's dedicated martial characters.
