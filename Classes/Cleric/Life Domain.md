## Desc.

A dedicated divine healer and protector who keeps the party alive through powerful healing, buffs, and defensive magic. Focuses on supporting allies rather than dealing damage.

**Playstyle:** Stay near the party, maintain buffs and concentration spells, heal injured allies, and use defensive/support spells to keep everyone fighting. Wear heavy armor and a shield for strong survivability.

**Notes:** 
- Focused healer/support. 
- Very durable thanks to heavy armor. 
- Not particularly dependent on Charisma, making it ideal as a non-party-face character. 
- Can function as a frontline support/tank when needed.

## Feats

- [[Durable]]: If you want to be a tank
