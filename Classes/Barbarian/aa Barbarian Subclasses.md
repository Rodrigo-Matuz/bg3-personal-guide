### [[Ancestral Guardian]]

A defensive Barbarian who calls upon **ancestral spirits to protect allies and hinder enemies**. **Primarily physical + spiritual magic**, focused on tanking, damage mitigation, and forcing enemies to deal with you.

---
### [[Battlerager]]

A brutal close-range Barbarian who turns **spiked armor and reckless aggression** into weapons. **Purely physical**, specializing in aggressive melee combat, durability, and dealing damage simply by getting into the enemy's face.

---
### [[Beast]]

A primal Barbarian who channels their **inner beast**, gaining natural weapons such as claws, fangs, or a powerful tail. **Purely physical**, focused on unarmed/natural attacks and adapting your fighting style to the situation.

---
### [[Berserker]]

The classic **rage-fueled Barbarian**, sacrificing finesse for overwhelming strength and repeated attacks. **Purely physical**, built around Frenzy, throwing enemies and objects, bonus-action attacks, and relentless melee damage.

---
### [[Giant]]

A Barbarian empowered by the **might of giants**, growing larger and turning their enormous strength into devastating attacks. **Physical + giant-themed magical abilities**, with increased size, powerful throws, elemental effects, and exceptional battlefield presence.

---
### [[Ginnungagap]]

A Barbarian drawing power from **Ginnungagap, the primordial Norse void**, transforming rage into destructive supernatural energy. **Physical + void/elemental magic**, combining brutal melee combat with dark, otherworldly effects.

---
### [[Juggernaut]]

An unstoppable Barbarian built around **raw momentum, immense durability, and crushing physical force**. **Purely physical**, specializing in charging through enemies, resisting control, knocking creatures around, and becoming increasingly difficult to stop.

---
### [[Path of the Corpse Eater]]

A savage Barbarian who draws strength from **consuming the flesh and essence of their fallen enemies**. **Physical + necrotic abilities**, focused on brutality, self-sustain, and turning kills into additional power.

---
### [[Rage Mage]]

A Barbarian who learns to channel their **rage directly into arcane power**, combining spellcasting with their overwhelming physical strength. **Physical + arcane magic**, allowing you to fight in melee while using destructive and defensive spells fueled by Rage.

---
### [[Storm Herald]]

A Barbarian surrounded by a **persistent elemental storm**, choosing powers associated with fire, cold, or lightning. **Physical + elemental magic**, providing area damage and defensive effects while remaining primarily a front-line melee combatant.

---
### [[Wild Magic (Bar)]]

A Barbarian whose rage unleashes **unpredictable magical effects**, making every battle slightly different. **Physical + chaotic magic**, combining conventional Barbarian combat with random magical surges that can damage, buff, teleport, or otherwise disrupt the battlefield.

---
### [[Wildheart]]

A primal Barbarian who channels the **power of different animal spirits**, gaining unique abilities based on their chosen Bestial Heart. **Primarily physical + primal abilities**, offering a highly customizable playstyle ranging from mobility and defense to control and damage.

---
### [[Zealot]]

A fanatical warrior whose **unshakable devotion fuels their rage**, allowing them to fight with supernatural determination. **Physical + divine/necrotic or radiant abilities**, focused on devastating melee damage, survivability, and refusing to stay down.
