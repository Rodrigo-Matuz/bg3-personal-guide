### [[Farmer]]

A Commoner who relies on **farming, animal handling, and a loyal ox** to survive adventuring. **Physical + utility**, with a scaling Loyal Livestock companion, the ability to feed allies produce for temporary HP, and eventually the ability to recreate Plant Growth outside combat. The ox can fight alongside you and becomes stronger as you level.

---
### [[Innkeeper]]

A Commoner who survives through **social skills, stories, and knowing how to handle people** rather than martial training. **Support + limited magic**, using Grit to replicate effects such as Friends, Guidance, Vicious Mockery, Heroism, Tasha's Hideous Laughter, and Enthrall. Later, resting can provide the entire party with temporary HP and protection against Charm and Fear.

---
### [[Labourer]]

A Commoner built around **physical labor and sheer toughness**, sacrificing versatility for the ability to take punishment and carry ridiculous amounts of equipment. **Purely physical**, gaining proficiency with heavy weapons such as mauls, warhammers, greataxes, and battleaxes, increased carrying capacity, bludgeoning resistance, and resistance to another damage type depending on their chosen labor.

---
### [[Town Guard]]

A Commoner with **actual combat training**, but still far from being a professional adventurer or Fighter. **Physical / martial**, gaining medium armor, shields, a chosen martial weapon, and a limited selection of Battle Master maneuvers that use Grit instead of Superiority Dice. Their Old Reliable weapon becomes increasingly accurate and deadly as they level.
