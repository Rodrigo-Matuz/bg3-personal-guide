# [[Druid]]

The [[Druid]] is a versatile nature-based spellcaster that can adapt to different situations through powerful spells, environmental control, healing, summoning, and [[Wild Shape]]. Unlike classes that specialize in a single combat role, Druids can change how they contribute depending on their Circle.

Their spell list gives them strong control over terrain and positioning, along with healing, elemental damage, defensive effects, and summoning. Some Circles make spellcasting the primary focus, while others shift the Druid toward melee combat, companions, transformation, or specialized elemental damage.

[[Wild Shape]] is also an important part of the Druid's overall toolkit. Depending on the Circle, it can be a major combat feature, a secondary option, or something that is largely replaced by another transformation mechanic.

In combat, [[Druid]] can fill several different roles depending on its subclass:

- **Spellcaster** — Uses elemental, nature, and control spells from range.
- **Controller** — Manipulates terrain, movement, and enemy positioning.
- **Healer/Support** — Keeps allies alive and provides defensive utility.
- **Frontliner** — Uses Wild Shape or martial abilities to fight at close range.
- **Summoner** — Creates creatures and spirits that occupy the battlefield.
- **Specialist** — Focuses on a specific damage type such as fire, cold, lightning, or necrotic damage.

## [[Circle of the Blighted]]

The [[Circle of the Blighted]] is a corrupted Druid that uses decay and disease as weapons. Instead of protecting nature in its healthy form, this subclass turns corruption itself into a combat tool.

Its defining feature is **Defiled Ground**, which corrupts an area of the battlefield. The zone hinders hostile creatures and causes additional Necrotic damage when they take damage, making it particularly effective when your party can keep enemies inside the area. The feature uses a Bonus Action, allowing you to establish the zone without giving up your main Action for the turn.

Exploration and combat both benefit from the subclass's corrupted-nature theme, but its strongest value comes from setting up an area where your entire party can repeatedly trigger additional damage.

### Combat

The [[Circle of the Blighted]] is an **area-control damage specialist**.

Start encounters by placing Defiled Ground where several enemies are likely to remain. Once the area is active, your spells and your allies' attacks can repeatedly take advantage of the additional Necrotic damage.

The subclass works especially well with other characters who make multiple attacks or can force enemies to remain in the affected area. Its main weakness is that enemies can simply move away from the corrupted zone, reducing its value.

The area also becomes substantially larger and its bonus damage improves later in the progression.

### Progression

**Early:** Defiled Ground immediately gives the Druid a strong way to establish an advantageous combat zone.

**Mid:** Improved corruption damage and additional Druid tools make the subclass better at combining battlefield control with offensive spellcasting.

**End:** The larger Defiled Ground radius and stronger Necrotic damage make positioning increasingly important and allow the effect to influence much more of the battlefield.

**Best for:**

- Area control
- Necrotic damage
- Damage-over-time setups
- Party compositions that attack the same targets
- Corrupted/decay-themed Druids

## [[Circle of the Blood]]

The [[Circle of the Blood]] is a high-risk Druid that uses **Sangromancy**, sacrificing its own vitality to empower magical abilities. Instead of treating HP purely as something to protect, the subclass turns health into another resource.

The playstyle rewards aggressive spellcasting and careful resource management. You deliberately accept some self-damage to gain stronger effects, then use the subclass's blood-themed abilities and enemies' deaths to recover some of what you spent.

### Combat

The [[Circle of the Blood]] is an **offensive caster with a health-for-power mechanic**.

Its biggest advantage is that it can convert its own HP into additional magical power. This allows it to produce stronger effects than a conventional Druid might be able to achieve in the same situation.

The drawback is obvious: using your strongest tools makes you easier to kill. You need to balance your HP against your spell resources and avoid sacrificing too much health before the enemy can be finished.

The subclass becomes particularly effective when you can safely damage yourself, eliminate enemies, and recover enough vitality to continue fighting. The mod's later progression also expands its blood-based magical toolkit and summons.

### Progression

**Early:** Sangromancy introduces the core health-for-power mechanic and gives the Druid a more aggressive spellcasting role.

**Mid:** More blood magic and recovery tools make the risk/reward system easier to exploit during longer encounters.

**End:** The subclass has enough offensive and restorative options to sustain a high-pressure playstyle while continuing to use HP as a resource.

**Best for:**

- Aggressive spellcasting
- High-risk/high-reward builds
- Blood magic
- Necrotic-themed characters
- Players comfortable managing HP as a resource

## [[Circle of the Dreams]]

The [[Circle of the Dreams]] is a Fey-focused support Druid centered on healing, protection, mobility, and supernatural utility. Rather than maximizing damage, it helps the party survive and reposition while maintaining the Druid's normal nature magic.

Its Fey influence also makes it useful outside combat. Stealth, invisibility, teleportation, and restorative abilities give the subclass several ways to solve problems without simply attacking them.

### Combat

The [[Circle of the Dreams]] is primarily a **support and defensive specialist**.

Its healing tools can restore allies from a distance, while temporary HP provides additional protection before damage is taken. Mobility and invisibility also allow the Druid to reposition or help an ally escape a dangerous situation.

The subclass is strongest when the party already has sufficient damage and needs someone who can keep everyone functioning. Its main weakness is that it gives up some of the specialized offensive power of more damage-focused Circles.

### Progression

**Early:** Ranged healing and Fey utility establish the subclass as a reliable support character.

**Mid:** Temporary HP, mobility, and stealth tools make it increasingly effective at preventing damage rather than simply repairing it.

**End:** Stronger restorative and teleportation options give the subclass excellent party utility throughout difficult encounters.

**Best for:**

- Healing
- Defensive support
- Temporary HP
- Mobility
- Stealth-oriented parties

## [[Circle of the Land]]

The [[Circle of the Land]] is the most traditional spellcasting-focused Druid. It draws additional magical power from a chosen environment, expanding the Druid's available spells and giving it more options for different encounters.

This is the most straightforward choice if you want to play the Druid primarily as a **full spellcaster**. Its expanded magical toolkit is useful both in combat and exploration because you have more ways to respond to different problems.

### Combat

The [[Circle of the Land]] is a **versatile controller and caster**.

Its greatest strength is having access to a broader selection of spells than a more specialized Druid. You can use terrain control, elemental damage, healing, defensive effects, and utility depending on the situation.

The subclass does not revolve around a single combat gimmick, which makes it easier to adapt to different encounters. The tradeoff is that it lacks the highly specialized strengths of subclasses such as Moon, Shepherd, or Wildfire.

### Progression

**Early:** Expanded spell options make the Land Druid highly flexible.

**Mid:** Additional spells allow it to cover more combat situations without relying on one specific strategy.

**End:** The large spell selection makes the subclass one of the most adaptable Druid choices for long encounters and varied parties.

**Best for:**

- Full spellcasting
- Battlefield control
- Elemental magic
- General-purpose Druid gameplay
- Players who want maximum spell flexibility

## [[Circle of the Moon]]

The [[Circle of the Moon]] is the dedicated Wild Shape Druid. Instead of primarily fighting through spells, it transforms into powerful beasts and uses those forms to occupy the frontline.

This changes how the Druid approaches both combat and exploration. Wild Shape becomes a major part of the character rather than simply an additional utility ability.

### Combat

The [[Circle of the Moon]] is a **frontline transformation specialist**.

You enter combat by transforming into a powerful creature and use the beast's health pool, attacks, movement, and special abilities to fight directly. This allows you to absorb damage that would otherwise be directed at your party.

The subclass is particularly effective when enemies have to spend multiple turns attacking your Wild Shape instead of your vulnerable party members. You can also return to your normal form and continue casting when the situation requires it.

The drawback is that your effectiveness depends heavily on the available Wild Shape forms and the circumstances of the encounter.

### Progression

**Early:** Wild Shape becomes a practical combat option rather than just an exploration tool.

**Mid:** Stronger beast forms significantly improve your durability and frontline damage.

**End:** Powerful transformations allow the Druid to remain an effective frontline character while retaining access to its normal spellcasting toolkit.

**Best for:**

- Frontline combat
- High durability
- Beast transformations
- Absorbing enemy attacks
- Players who want to spend significant time in Wild Shape

## [[Circle of the Shepherd]]

The [[Circle of the Shepherd]] specializes in **summoning and supporting creatures**. It protects beasts and spirits while turning summoned allies into a major part of the Druid's combat strategy.

The subclass becomes increasingly valuable in encounters where multiple creatures can remain on the battlefield for several turns. Outside combat, its nature-focused abilities also fit naturally into exploration involving animals and wilderness.

### Combat

The [[Circle of the Shepherd]] is a **summoner and creature-support specialist**.

Rather than relying entirely on your own actions, you create additional combatants and use Spirit Totems and other abilities to improve their effectiveness. This effectively increases the number of bodies your party has on the battlefield.

The main advantage is action economy: summoned creatures can attack, move, and occupy enemies independently while the Druid continues casting or supporting the party.

The drawback is battlefield management. Large numbers of summons can make positioning more complicated, and cramped encounters can reduce the usefulness of having many creatures.

### Progression

**Early:** Summoning and Spirit Totem abilities establish the subclass's creature-focused identity.

**Mid:** Stronger summons and improved creature support increase your battlefield presence.

**End:** A large collection of enhanced summons can turn the Shepherd into one of the strongest options for building an army of allied creatures.

**Best for:**

- Summoning
- Companion-heavy parties
- Action economy
- Battlefield control through additional bodies
- Players who enjoy managing multiple creatures

## [[Circle of the Spores]]

The [[Circle of the Spores]] uses fungi and decay as offensive tools. It combines Druid magic with temporary HP, additional Necrotic damage, fungal abilities, and undead manipulation.

The subclass can function as either a spellcaster or a more aggressive close-range Druid, depending on how heavily you use its temporary HP and spore-based abilities.

### Combat

The [[Circle of the Spores]] is a **durable Necrotic damage hybrid**.

Temporary HP gives you a buffer that allows you to operate closer to enemies than a typical caster. Your spores then add additional damage and provide specialized fungal effects.

The subclass becomes particularly useful when you can maintain your temporary HP and repeatedly exploit its additional Necrotic damage. Its ability to raise or control dead creatures also adds extra battlefield bodies.

### Progression

**Early:** Temporary HP and additional Necrotic damage make the Druid more durable and aggressive.

**Mid:** Stronger fungal abilities and undead options expand the subclass's combat toolkit.

**End:** The combination of Necrotic damage, temporary HP, and undead support allows the subclass to function as a durable caster with strong secondary battlefield presence.

**Best for:**

- Necrotic damage
- Temporary HP
- Fungal/undead builds
- Hybrid caster gameplay
- Durable Druids

## [[Circle of the Stars]]

The [[Circle of the Stars]] replaces traditional Wild Shape specialization with celestial transformations called **Starry Forms**. Each form emphasizes a different combat function, allowing the Druid to adapt its role during an encounter.

The subclass focuses on radiant and cosmic magic, but its real strength is flexibility. You can choose a form that improves damage, healing, or concentration depending on what the party needs.

### Combat

The [[Circle of the Stars]] is a **flexible ranged caster**.

Starry Forms allow you to specialize temporarily for the encounter. Offensive forms improve your ranged damage, while other forms improve healing or help maintain concentration.

This makes the subclass particularly good at maintaining important concentration spells while still contributing meaningful damage. Its main limitation is that its power depends on selecting the correct form for the situation.

### Progression

**Early:** Starry Forms immediately give the Druid multiple ways to specialize.

**Mid:** Stronger cosmic abilities improve both offensive and support options.

**End:** The subclass becomes highly flexible, allowing you to switch between damage, healing, and concentration-focused roles depending on the encounter.

**Best for:**

- Ranged spellcasting
- Radiant damage
- Concentration support
- Flexible combat roles
- Players who want alternatives to Wild Shape

## [[Circle of the Stormchasers]]

The [[Circle of the Stormchasers]] specializes in elemental storm magic, focusing on **Lightning, Thunder, and Cold**. It is one of the more aggressively offensive Druid Circles and is designed to turn elemental damage into its primary method of controlling encounters.

Its abilities also help the subclass deal with elemental resistances, making its specialized damage types more reliable against enemies that would normally reduce their effectiveness.

### Combat

The [[Circle of the Stormchasers]] is an **elemental blaster and battlefield controller**.

It uses storm-themed spells and abilities to deal significant elemental damage from range while manipulating the battlefield through lightning, thunder, and cold effects.

The subclass is especially effective when you build around one or more of its supported damage types and use elemental effects to exploit enemy weaknesses.

Its biggest limitation is specialization: enemies resistant to your preferred elements can reduce your effectiveness unless you have ways to bypass or work around those resistances.

### Progression

**Early:** Storm abilities establish the subclass as an aggressive elemental caster.

**Mid:** Stronger lightning, thunder, and cold effects give you more ways to damage and control groups.

**End:** Resistance-bypassing tools and stronger elemental abilities make the subclass considerably more consistent against enemies that would otherwise counter it.

**Best for:**

- Lightning damage
- Thunder damage
- Cold damage
- Elemental spellcasting
- Aggressive ranged Druids

## [[Circle of the Wildfire]]

The [[Circle of the Wildfire]] combines fire magic with a **Wildfire Spirit** companion. It treats destruction and renewal as complementary forces, giving the Druid access to both offensive fire abilities and restorative tools.

The Wildfire Spirit also provides excellent mobility, making the subclass useful for repositioning both allies and enemies during encounters.

### Combat

The [[Circle of the Wildfire]] is a **fire damage and mobility specialist**.

The Wildfire Spirit provides an additional battlefield presence while your fire spells handle groups of enemies. Its teleportation tools can reposition allies, allowing you to rescue characters from dangerous positions or move them into better ones.

The combination of AoE fire damage, healing, teleportation, and a companion makes the subclass highly versatile.

Its main weakness is that fire-resistant enemies can reduce the effectiveness of its primary offensive tools.

### Progression

**Early:** The Wildfire Spirit gives you both a companion and additional mobility.

**Mid:** Stronger fire and healing abilities allow you to alternate between offense and support.

**End:** The subclass gains enough damage, mobility, and healing to function as a highly flexible caster while its spirit remains an important battlefield tool.

**Best for:**

- Fire damage
- AoE
- Mobility
- Healing
- Companion gameplay

## [[Circle of the Winter]]

The [[Circle of the Winter]] focuses on the battlefield control potential of **Cold and Ice**. Rather than simply dealing damage, it uses freezing effects, slowing, and hazardous terrain to limit enemy movement.

This makes the subclass particularly useful for controlling groups and creating favorable positioning for the rest of the party.

### Combat

The [[Circle of the Winter]] is a **Cold-based controller**.

Its spells and abilities can slow enemies, create hazardous terrain, and punish creatures that remain in affected areas. This allows the Druid to influence where enemies can safely move.

The subclass works especially well when combined with ranged allies who can attack enemies while they struggle to approach.

Its main drawback is the same as most elemental specialists: enemies resistant to Cold reduce your damage.

### Progression

**Early:** Cold damage and slowing effects establish the subclass's control-focused identity.

**Mid:** Larger and stronger ice effects make terrain manipulation more significant.

**End:** The subclass can control substantial portions of the battlefield while continuing to deal reliable Cold damage.

**Best for:**

- Battlefield control
- Cold damage
- Slowing enemies
- Area denial
- Ranged party compositions

## [[Circle of the Witchcraft]]

The [[Circle of the Witchcraft]] is a specialized Druid that replaces some of the traditional nature-priest identity with **hexes, poisons, curses, and supernatural tricks**.

Its Witch Mother training gives it a toolkit that sits between Druid magic and traditional witchcraft. This makes it especially effective at weakening individual enemies rather than simply dealing raw AoE damage.

### Combat

The [[Circle of the Witchcraft]] is a **debuffer and poison specialist**.

Wild Hexcraft gives the subclass unusual ways to weaken enemies, while poison abilities provide additional damage and status effects. Spiritual summons add another layer of battlefield utility.

The subclass rewards targeting important enemies with debilitating effects before allowing the rest of the party to finish them.

Its main weakness is that poison-focused abilities can be unreliable against enemies with strong Poison resistance or immunity.

### Progression

**Early:** Hexes and poison effects give you immediate tools for weakening enemies.

**Mid:** More powerful Wild Hexcraft effects increase your ability to manipulate individual targets.

**End:** Stronger curses, poison effects, and spiritual abilities give the subclass a broad collection of debuffs and supernatural tools.

**Best for:**

- Debuffing
- Poison damage
- Hexes
- Single-target control
- Witch-themed characters

## [[Circle of the Unbroken Circle]]

The [[Circle of the Unbroken Circle]] turns the Druid into a **primal martial character**. Instead of relying primarily on Wild Shape or traditional spellcasting, it channels nature directly through weapons and physical attacks.

The subclass is ideal for players who want a Druid that can stand beside Fighters, Paladins, and other martial characters while still retaining access to Druid utility.

### Combat

The [[Circle of the Unbroken Circle]] is a **weapon-based Druid**.

Shillelagh-enhanced attacks allow the subclass to make effective use of Wisdom-based weapon combat, while martial weapon access and additional attacks give it a much more conventional martial action economy.

You can fight in melee while using Druid spells for utility, control, and defensive support when necessary.

The major advantage is that you do not have to choose between being a Druid and being a melee character. The major drawback is that a dedicated martial class will generally have more specialized offensive tools.

### Progression

**Early:** Weapon enhancement and martial capabilities immediately shift the Druid toward melee combat.

**Mid:** Additional attacks and stronger nature-enhanced weapons significantly improve sustained weapon damage.

**End:** The subclass becomes a reliable martial character that retains the Druid's spellcasting and utility.

**Best for:**

- Melee Druid builds
- Wisdom-based weapon attacks
- Martial combat
- Hybrid martial/caster gameplay
- Players who want a Druid without relying on Wild Shape
