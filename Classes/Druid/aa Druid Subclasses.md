### [[Circle of the Blighted]]

A corrupted Druid who embraces **decay, disease, and the corruption of nature** rather than protecting nature in its pure form. **Nature + necrotic magic**, specializing in creating Defiled Ground, weakening enemies, spreading corruption, and turning the battlefield itself into a source of damage.

---
### [[Circle of the Blood]]

A Druid who practices **sangromancy**, sacrificing their own vitality to fuel powerful blood magic. **Nature + blood/necrotic magic**, built around a high-risk, high-reward playstyle where you damage yourself to empower offensive spells and abilities, then recover some of that lost vitality from dying enemies.

---
### [[Circle of the Dreams]]

A Druid touched by the **Feywild and the restorative power of dreams**, acting as a supernatural healer and protector. **Nature + Fey/support magic**, focused on healing allies from range, temporary HP, stealth support, invisibility, and teleportation rather than raw damage.

---
### [[Circle of the Land]]

A traditional Druid deeply connected to a **specific natural environment**, gaining additional magic based on the land they choose. **Pure nature/elemental magic**, focused on expanded spellcasting, utility, terrain manipulation, and adapting your magic to different situations.

---
### [[Circle of the Moon]]

A Druid who embraces **the primal power of Wild Shape**, becoming a powerful frontline beast rather than relying primarily on spellcasting. **Primarily physical + nature magic**, focused on transforming into increasingly powerful creatures, absorbing damage, and fighting directly as a beast.

---
### [[Circle of the Shepherd]]

A Druid devoted to **protecting beasts and spirits**, calling upon supernatural allies to fight alongside them. **Nature + summoning/support magic**, specializing in summons, Spirit Totems, buffing allied creatures, and turning a battlefield full of companions into a major advantage.

---
### [[Circle of the Spores]]

A Druid who embraces **fungal growth, decay, and the cycle of death**, using spores as both weapons and sources of power. **Nature + necrotic magic**, focused on temporary HP, extra necrotic damage, fungal abilities, and raising or controlling the dead.

---
### [[Circle of the Stars]]

A Druid who draws power from **the stars and cosmic forces**, replacing traditional Wild Shape with powerful celestial transformations. **Nature + radiant/cosmic magic**, specializing in ranged radiant damage, healing, concentration support, and versatile Starry Forms that change your role in combat.

---
### [[Circle of the Stormchasers]]

A Druid who channels the **raw fury of nature's storms**, bringing lightning, thunder, and freezing winds into combat. **Nature + lightning/thunder/cold magic**, designed as an aggressive elemental caster with powerful storm-based abilities and ways to bypass certain elemental resistances.

---
### [[Circle of the Wildfire]]

A Druid who understands that **destruction and renewal are both parts of nature**, wielding magical fire while commanding a Wildfire Spirit. **Nature + fire magic**, combining AoE fire damage, healing, teleportation, and a fiery companion that can reposition allies and attack enemies.

---
### [[Circle of the Winter]]

A Druid who embraces **the destructive and preserving power of winter**, freezing enemies and controlling the battlefield through cold. **Nature + cold/ice magic**, focused on frost damage, slowing and controlling enemies, defensive cold effects, and turning the battlefield into a frozen hazard.

---
### [[Circle of the Witchcraft]]

A Druid trained by the mysterious **Witch Mother**, using hexes, poisons, and supernatural trickery rather than conventional druidic magic. **Nature + poison/hex magic**, specializing in debilitating enemies, Wild Hexcraft effects, poison damage, and strange spiritual summons.

---
### [[Circle of the Unbroken Circle]]

A martial Druid who abandons the traditional image of a peaceful nature priest and becomes a **primal warrior**, empowering weapons with nature itself. **Physical + nature magic**, focused on melee combat, martial weapons, Shillelagh-enhanced attacks, extra attacks, and growing stronger as the Druid levels.
