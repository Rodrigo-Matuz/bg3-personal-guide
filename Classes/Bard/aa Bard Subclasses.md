# [[Bard]]

The [[Bard]] is a Charisma-based full caster built around spellcasting, [[Bardic Inspiration]], control, support, and exceptional utility. Unlike classes that specialize almost entirely in damage or defense, the Bard can influence almost every part of a party's performance: improving allies, weakening enemies, controlling encounters, solving dialogue and skill checks, and, depending on the subclass, fighting effectively with weapons.

[[Bardic Inspiration]] is the class's defining resource. It allows the Bard to improve important rolls and becomes a major part of several subclass mechanics. Because many Bard abilities use a Bonus Action or Reaction, managing these resources alongside spellcasting is an important part of playing the class effectively.

The Bard is also one of the strongest classes for general party utility. Its high Charisma makes it naturally suited to dialogue, while its broad skill access and spell selection allow it to cover situations outside combat. Subclasses then push the Bard toward specialized roles such as battlefield support, martial combat, magical versatility, or direct control.

In combat, [[Bard]] can fill several different roles depending on its subclass:

- **Support caster** — Improves allies through Bardic Inspiration, buffs, and defensive effects.
- **Battlefield controller** — Uses charms, commands, fear, confusion, and other control spells.
- **Debuffer** — Reduces enemy attack rolls, saving throws, and other important checks.
- **Martial damage dealer** — Uses weapons, Extra Attack, and subclass-specific combat abilities.
- **Ranged damage dealer** — Combines ranged weapons with Bard spells and specialized attacks.
- **Utility caster** — Uses a broad spell selection to solve different combat and exploration problems.
- **Party face** — Uses high Charisma and strong skill access for dialogue and social encounters.
- **Skill specialist** — Particularly strong at covering multiple skill checks and adapting to different party needs.

## [[College of Cantors]]

The [[College of Cantors]] is a divine-oriented Bard that combines Bardic spellcasting with Cleric-inspired magic and abilities. Instead of replacing Bard support with conventional martial combat, it expands the Bard's access to divine tools, allowing it to provide healing, protection, offensive support, and anti-undead utility.

In exploration, the subclass is particularly useful for a party that wants a character capable of covering both Bard and divine skill requirements. Its implementation also gives the Bard Arcana and Religion proficiency, while its divine spell access provides additional utility outside combat.

### Combat

The [[College of Cantors]] plays primarily as a **support and utility caster**. Its Cleric spell access gives it additional options for healing, protection, and dealing with undead while its Bard spellcasting remains intact.

Its [[Channel Divinity]] options give it additional ways to spend its resources beyond ordinary Bard spells. These include offensive options such as Bardic Smite as well as effects designed around undead and party protection.

This makes the Cantor particularly useful when you want one character to cover multiple support functions. You can use your Action for spells or divine abilities while retaining Bardic Inspiration and other Bard resources for supporting the party.

### Progression

**Early:** Divine spell access immediately expands your utility while Arcana and Religion proficiency improves your skill coverage.

**Mid:** Channel Divinity gives you additional combat tools, allowing you to contribute through healing, offense, protection, and anti-undead effects.

**End:** The subclass develops into a highly versatile support caster capable of combining Bard and divine magic without fully specializing in either one.

**Best for:**

- Divine support
- Healing and protection
- Anti-undead utility
- Support-heavy parties
- Bard/Cleric-style gameplay

## [[College of Command]]

The [[College of Command]] is a battlefield leader that focuses on directing allies rather than simply increasing its own damage. It uses Bardic abilities to reposition allies, improve their attacks, and coordinate the party while still retaining normal Bard spellcasting.

In exploration, it remains a strong Charisma-based utility character. In combat, its value increases when the party has several strong martial characters that can take advantage of the Bard's commands and positioning tools. The subclass is explicitly designed around leading allies, maneuvering them around the battlefield, and supporting the group as a tactical unit.

### Combat

The [[College of Command]] is a **team-oriented support subclass**. Rather than spending every turn attacking or casting offensive spells, you frequently use your actions and Bardic resources to make another party member's turn more effective.

Positioning is particularly important. Moving an ally into attack range, helping them reach a high-value target, or setting up a better position can produce more value than making a mediocre attack yourself.

The subclass works especially well with parties containing strong melee characters, because your support can directly increase the number and quality of attacks those characters make.

### Progression

**Early:** Your command abilities establish the subclass as a battlefield coordinator capable of improving ally positioning and offensive effectiveness.

**Mid:** Your control and support options become more impactful, allowing you to coordinate several party members during difficult encounters.

**End:** You become a dedicated battlefield commander who can spend actions and Bardic resources to maximize the effectiveness of the entire party.

**Best for:**

- Party coordination
- Martial-heavy parties
- Ally positioning
- Support and control
- Tactical players

## [[College of Dueling]]

The [[College of Dueling]] is an offensive Bard built around combining weapon attacks with damaging cantrips. Unlike support-focused Bard colleges, it is designed to remain active in direct combat while still retaining Bardic spellcasting.

In exploration, it retains the Bard's normal Charisma and utility advantages. In combat, it plays as a magical duelist who alternates between weapon attacks and offensive cantrips depending on range, target defenses, and available actions. The subclass is specifically implemented as a cantrip-focused Bard subclass in BG3.

### Combat

The [[College of Dueling]] is best played as a **hybrid damage dealer**. You can use a weapon when getting into melee is advantageous, then use offensive cantrips when a ranged magical attack is more appropriate.

This gives the subclass more flexibility than a Bard that commits entirely to weapon attacks. You do not have to spend every turn maintaining support effects, although your Bard spells remain available when control or utility is more valuable than direct damage.

The main limitation is specialization. A Dueling Bard does not reach the same dedicated martial identity as a heavily weapon-focused subclass, nor does it replace a full offensive caster. Its strength is combining both approaches.

### Progression

**Early:** Offensive cantrips give you reliable ranged magical options while weapon attacks cover close combat.

**Mid:** Your spellcasting and combat abilities become strong enough to switch naturally between magical and physical attacks depending on the encounter.

**End:** The subclass becomes a flexible magical duelist capable of maintaining offensive pressure at different ranges.

**Best for:**

- Hybrid melee/caster builds
- Offensive cantrips
- One-on-one combat
- Flexible damage
- Players who want a more aggressive Bard

## [[College of Glamour]]

The [[College of Glamour]] uses Fey magic, charm, and supernatural presence to protect allies and control enemies. Its defining feature is its ability to turn Bardic performance into a party-wide combat advantage, making it particularly effective as a support controller.

In exploration, its high Charisma and social abilities fit naturally with the Bard's role as a party face. In combat, it focuses on temporary hit points, charm effects, movement, and command-style control rather than weapon damage.

### Combat

The [[College of Glamour]] is a **support/controller**. Its temporary-hit-point effects allow you to protect multiple allies while giving them additional mobility, which can immediately change the positioning of the party.

Later abilities allow you to use charm and command effects to interfere with enemy turns. This can be particularly strong when several enemies are grouped together because a single control effect can prevent multiple attacks or force enemies into unfavorable positions.

The main action-economy consideration is that Bardic Inspiration and Glamour abilities can compete for your Bonus Action. You need to decide whether your current turn is more valuable for directly supporting an ally or controlling an enemy.

### Progression

**Early:** Mantle-based support gives your party additional temporary HP and mobility while establishing the subclass as a dedicated support Bard.

**Mid:** Your charm and command effects become much stronger, allowing you to interfere directly with enemy turns.

**End:** The subclass becomes a powerful combination of party protection and battlefield control, with your presence affecting both allies and enemies.

**Best for:**

- Party support
- Crowd control
- Temporary HP
- Charm and command effects
- Players who want a dedicated support/controller

## [[College of Lore]]

The [[College of Lore]] is the most versatile traditional caster-oriented Bard. It focuses on knowledge, skills, debuffs, and learning spells from outside the normal Bard spell list.

In exploration, it is one of the strongest Bard options because it gains additional skill proficiencies and naturally combines high Charisma with broad utility. In combat, [[Cutting Words]] gives the Bard an extremely flexible Reaction that can reduce an enemy's attack roll, ability check, or saving throw.

### Combat

The [[College of Lore]] is primarily a **control and utility caster**. [[Cutting Words]] lets you react to important enemy rolls and reduce their chance of succeeding, making it useful both defensively and offensively.

Its biggest advantage is [[Magical Secrets]]. The subclass gains access to powerful non-Bard spells earlier than other Bard colleges, allowing you to fill gaps in the party's spell list. This can turn the Lore Bard into a dedicated controller, damage caster, defensive specialist, or combination of several roles.

The subclass has less direct weapon combat capability than the martial colleges, so its main contribution comes from spells, reactions, and support.

### Progression

**Early:** Extra skill proficiencies make you an excellent party utility character while Cutting Words provides strong reactive control.

**Mid:** Magical Secrets dramatically expands your spell options and allows you to acquire important spells from outside the Bard list.

**End:** Your broad spell selection and powerful reactions make you one of the most adaptable caster-oriented Bards.

**Best for:**

- Spellcasting
- Crowd control
- Debuffing
- Skill coverage
- Magical Secrets
- Party-face characters

## [[College of Spirits]]

The [[College of Spirits]] is a supernatural Bard that channels spirits through storytelling. Its abilities draw on random or spirit-themed effects, allowing it to provide damage, support, control, and utility depending on which spirit effect is invoked.

In exploration, the subclass provides additional supernatural utility while retaining the Bard's strong social and skill-based identity. In combat, it works best when you are comfortable adapting your turn around the effect currently available rather than following a completely fixed rotation.

### Combat

The [[College of Spirits]] is a **flexible support and damage caster**. Its spirit-based effects can provide different benefits, meaning you should evaluate what the current effect does before deciding how to spend your Action and Bonus Action.

This makes the subclass less predictable than Lore or Swords. You cannot always force the exact result you want, but you can often find a useful way to incorporate the available spirit effect into your turn.

The subclass works particularly well in parties that already have dedicated damage dealers because you can use its effects to fill whatever support or control gap exists during the encounter.

### Progression

**Early:** Spirit effects add an unpredictable secondary layer to your Bard spellcasting.

**Mid:** Your spirit abilities become stronger and give you more ways to contribute damage, support, or utility.

**End:** The subclass becomes a flexible supernatural Bard capable of adapting its contribution to the needs of the encounter.

**Best for:**

- Flexible support
- Supernatural-themed characters
- Randomized abilities
- Damage and utility hybrids
- Players who enjoy adapting each turn

## [[College of Swords]]

The [[College of Swords]] is the dedicated martial Bard. It combines Bardic spellcasting with weapon combat, [[Blade Flourish]], and a Fighting Style, allowing the Bard to function as a genuine damage dealer rather than merely carrying a weapon for emergencies.

In exploration, it retains Bard utility and Charisma-based skills. In combat, it uses weapon attacks as its main source of damage while spending Bardic Inspiration on powerful flourishes that add damage, defense, or mobility. BG3 gives the subclass medium armor, scimitar proficiency, Fighting Style options, and several Blade Flourish variants.

### Combat

The [[College of Swords]] is a **martial damage dealer with full Bard utility**. Its Blade Flourishes let your attacks do more than simply deal damage.

Defensive Flourish improves your defenses, Slashing Flourish allows you to hit additional targets, and Mobile Flourish provides mobility and forced movement. This means Bardic Inspiration directly improves your martial action economy instead of being reserved purely for supporting allies.

Extra Attack later gives the subclass a major increase in sustained weapon damage. This makes Swords particularly effective when you want a character that can attack repeatedly while still having access to Bard control and utility spells.

### Progression

**Early:** Medium armor, a Fighting Style, and Blade Flourishes establish the subclass as a real martial combatant.

**Mid:** Extra Attack significantly improves weapon damage and makes Flourishes more valuable because you have more attacks to use them with.

**End:** The subclass becomes a highly effective weapon-based Bard capable of combining strong martial damage with full Bard spellcasting.

**Best for:**

- Martial Bard builds
- Melee or ranged weapon combat
- High sustained damage
- Mobile combat
- Players who want a Bard that can fight directly

## [[College of Tragedy]]

The [[College of Tragedy]] turns failure and critical failures into resources. Rather than relying exclusively on successful rolls and conventional Bard support, it can react to bad outcomes and convert them into additional Bardic power.

In exploration, it remains a normal Bard for dialogue and utility, but its subclass mechanics are most noticeable in combat. The BG3 implementation specifically allows the Bard to respond when itself or an ally rolls a natural 1 on an attack or saving throw, potentially recovering Bardic Inspiration.

### Combat

The [[College of Tragedy]] is a **reactive support/debuff Bard**. Its main advantage comes from turning failed rolls into opportunities.

When an important attack or saving throw fails badly, you can use the subclass's reaction to gain additional Bardic resources. Other abilities can manipulate attack and saving-throw results, allowing you to create high-risk, high-reward situations.

At higher progression, [[Tragic Heroism]] can temporarily turn an ally into an extremely powerful combatant by granting improved defenses, Advantage, and additional damage. The drawback is severe: when the effect ends, the empowered creature drops to 0 hit points.

This makes the subclass particularly strong when you understand exactly when to spend its high-impact abilities rather than using them automatically.

### Progression

**Early:** Failed rolls can become resources, giving you additional Bardic Inspiration and establishing the subclass's reactive playstyle.

**Mid:** Your ability to manipulate rolls and exploit failure becomes increasingly useful for controlling the outcome of important attacks and saves.

**End:** Tragic Heroism gives you an extremely powerful temporary buff at the cost of dropping the target to 0 HP when it ends.

**Best for:**

- Reactive support
- High-risk/high-reward gameplay
- Roll manipulation
- Critical-moment buffs
- Players who enjoy turning failure into an advantage

## [[College of Valour]]

The [[College of Valour]] is a frontline support Bard that combines weapon combat with defensive and offensive Bardic Inspiration. It is designed to fight alongside the party rather than remain entirely in the backline.

In exploration, it retains the Bard's normal utility and social strengths. In combat, it uses medium armor, shields, martial weapons, and Combat Inspiration to support allies while contributing its own weapon damage.

### Combat

The [[College of Valour]] is a **martial support Bard**. Unlike Swords, which primarily uses Bardic Inspiration to enhance its own attacks through Flourishes, Valour focuses more heavily on empowering allies.

Combat Inspiration can improve an ally's combat performance, including offensive or defensive rolls depending on the situation. This makes the subclass useful when you want to remain close to the frontline while still spending resources on party support.

Extra Attack later gives you substantially better personal damage, allowing you to contribute directly without giving up your support role.

### Progression

**Early:** Martial weapon proficiency, medium armor, and shields allow you to safely participate in frontline combat while supporting allies.

**Mid:** Extra Attack improves your personal damage while Combat Inspiration continues strengthening your party.

**End:** The subclass becomes a durable hybrid capable of fighting beside martial characters while providing Bardic support.

**Best for:**

- Martial support
- Frontline Bard builds
- Shield users
- Party buffs
- Players who want to fight alongside their allies

|---|---|
|[[College of Cantors]]|Choose it if you want Bardic spellcasting combined with divine magic, healing, and support.|
|[[College of Command]]|Choose it if you want to act as a battlefield commander who improves and positions your allies.|
|[[College of Dueling]]|Choose it if you want an offensive hybrid that combines weapon attacks with damaging cantrips.|
|[[College of Glamour]]|Choose it if you want strong party support through temporary HP, charm, mobility, and battlefield control.|
|[[College of Lore]]|Choose it if you want maximum spell versatility, debuffs, skills, and early access to Magical Secrets.|
|[[College of Spirits]]|Choose it if you want a supernatural Bard with flexible spirit-based damage, support, and utility.|
|[[College of Swords]]|Choose it if you want a genuine martial Bard with strong weapon damage, mobility, and Blade Flourishes.|
|[[College of Tragedy]]|Choose it if you want to manipulate failure and use risky, high-impact abilities to turn bad situations around.|
|[[College of Valour]]|Choose it if you want a frontline Bard who fights with weapons while directly supporting allies.|

The [[Bard]] is a Charisma-based full caster and party specialist that combines powerful control, support, utility, and social skills, with its subclasses determining how much of that power is devoted to magic, leadership, or martial combat.
