### [[College of Cantors]]

A Bard who turns **sacred music and powerful vocal performances** into divine-inspired magic. **Divine/support magic + Bardic spellcasting**, focused on enhancing allies, manipulating the battlefield through song, and turning performance into a powerful source of utility.

---
### [[College of Command]]

A martial leader who uses **Bardic magic to direct allies and control the battlefield**. **Physical + support/control magic**, focused on issuing commands, improving allies' attacks, coordinating the party, and turning the Bard into a battlefield commander.

---
### [[College of Dueling]]

A Bard who combines **swordplay with aggressive cantrip-based spellcasting**, making them a more offensively magical duelist. **Physical + arcane magic**, focused on one-on-one combat, weapon attacks, and slinging offensive cantrips rather than functioning purely as a support caster.

---
### [[College of Glamour]]

A Fey-touched Bard who uses **beauty, charm, and supernatural presence** to manipulate enemies and empower allies. **Enchantment/Fey magic + support**, specializing in temporary HP, charm and control effects, mobility, and turning your presence into a major battlefield advantage.

---
### [[College of Lore]]

A scholarly Bard who collects **knowledge from every magical and martial tradition**, allowing them to learn tricks normally unavailable to Bards. **Primarily magical/support**, specializing in additional spells, debuffs, utility, and adapting to almost any situation.

---
### [[College of Spirits]]

A mystical storyteller who communicates with **spirits and the dead**, using tales to channel supernatural effects. **Physical + spiritual/necrotic magic**, with unpredictable magical effects, support abilities, damage, and utility drawn from the spirits they invoke.

---
### [[College of Swords]]

A martial Bard who turns **flourishes and swordplay into deadly combat techniques**. **Physical + limited Bardic magic**, specializing in melee or ranged weapon damage, mobility, defensive flourishes, and fighting as a genuine martial character.

---
### [[College of Tragedy]]

A Bard who embraces **despair, failure, and the darker side of performance**, turning tragic moments into sources of power. **Psychic/necrotic-flavored magic + Bardic support**, focused on exploiting failure, inflicting debilitating effects, and becoming more dangerous as the battle descends into chaos.

---
### [[College of Valour]]

A heroic martial Bard who inspires allies while fighting **directly alongside them on the front line**. **Physical + divine/support magic**, combining weapon combat with defensive and offensive Bardic Inspiration to strengthen the entire party.
