### [[aa Accursed Subclasses]]

Accursed is a **curse-based hybrid class** that turns supernatural afflictions into sources of power. Its gameplay revolves around **Maledictions, curses, debuffs, and curse-specific abilities**, with different curses dramatically changing how the character fights. Pick Accursed if you want a character whose **main gimmick is weaponizing a permanent supernatural curse**, with options ranging from martial combat and stealth to fire, vampirism, necromancy, and arcane magic.

---
### [[aa Artificer Subclasses]]

Artificer is a **magical engineer and crafter**, combining Intelligence-based spellcasting with equipment enhancement, crafting, alchemy, and magical inventions. Rather than relying purely on spells, Artificers improve themselves and their allies through **Infusions and specialized creations**. Pick Artificer if you want a **high-utility hybrid** that can support the party, enhance equipment, fight with magical technology, or specialize into alchemy, armor, artillery, or mechanical companions.

---
### [[aa Barbarian Subclasses]]

Barbarian is a **frontline martial class built around Rage**, trading defensive flexibility and conventional spellcasting for overwhelming physical power and durability. Rage enhances combat performance while many Barbarian features reward staying in the middle of the fight and hitting things extremely hard. Pick Barbarian if you want **simple but powerful melee combat**, exceptional durability, throwing, mobility, or a primal/supernatural combat style.

---

### [[aa Bard Subclasses]]

Bard is a **Charisma-based hybrid caster and support class** built around Bardic Inspiration, versatile spellcasting, and exceptional utility. Bards can weaken enemies, strengthen allies, control encounters, provide skill expertise, or become surprisingly capable martial characters. Pick Bard if you want to be a **jack-of-all-trades**, especially if you enjoy supporting the party while still having strong dialogue, exploration, and combat options.

---

### [[aa Cleric Subclasses]]

Cleric is a **divine full caster** whose abilities are heavily shaped by their chosen Domain. They can heal, protect, buff, control, deal radiant or elemental damage, fight in melee, or specialize in stranger forms of divine magic. Pick Cleric if you want **strong spellcasting with a clearly defined role**, particularly if your party needs healing, support, defensive tools, or powerful divine damage.

---

### [[aa Commoner Subclasses]]

Commoner is an intentionally **low-powered class based around ordinary professions rather than heroic adventuring abilities**. Its core mechanics revolve around Grit, practical expertise, and profession-specific abilities instead of the enormous combat toolkits of traditional classes. Pick Commoner if you want to play a **non-heroic character who relies on practical skills**, with subclasses representing occupations such as farming, innkeeping, labor, and town guard duty.

---

### [[aa Druid Subclasses]]

Druid is a **Wisdom-based nature caster** combining powerful spellcasting with Wild Shape and abilities connected to animals, plants, elements, and the natural world. Depending on the subclass, Druids can become frontline beasts, summon creatures, heal allies, control terrain, or specialize in elemental and supernatural nature magic. Pick Druid if you want **one of the most versatile classes in the game**, particularly if you like shapeshifting, summons, environmental control, or nature-themed magic.

---

### [[aa Fighter Subclasses]]

Fighter is the game's **pure martial specialist**, built around exceptional weapon proficiency, Extra Attack, Action Surge, and consistent physical damage. Its subclasses determine whether you become a tactical warrior, magical fighter, archer, mounted combatant, psychic warrior, or another specialized martial archetype. Pick Fighter if you want **reliable weapon combat with minimal dependence on spellcasting**, while still having a huge variety of combat styles.

---

### [[aa Inquisitor Subclasses]]

Inquisitor is a **divine monster-hunter and investigative martial class**, combining weapon combat with Wisdom-based spellcasting, Judgments, and Monster Lore. You study enemies, identify their weaknesses, and apply specialized Judgments that can improve your offense, defense, or party effectiveness. Pick Inquisitor if you want a **Paladin/Ranger-style divine hunter** with more emphasis on analyzing enemies, exploiting weaknesses, and adapting to specific threats.

---

### [[aa Magus Subclasses]]

Magus is a dedicated **spellsword**, designed from the ground up to combine weapon attacks and spellcasting rather than choosing between them. Its core mechanics revolve around **Mystic Marks, weapon-enhanced spells, War Magic, and Spell Surge**, allowing the Magus to weave magic directly into martial combat. Pick Magus if you want to **fight with a weapon in one hand and magic in the other**, especially if you want a true arcane frontline hybrid.

---

### [[aa Marksman Subclasses]]

Marksman is a **dedicated ranged martial class** focused almost entirely on fighting from a distance. Instead of being a general-purpose Fighter or Ranger, it specializes in ranged weapons, positioning, accuracy, and specialized ranged techniques. Pick Marksman if you want to be the party's **dedicated ranged damage dealer**, with subclasses allowing you to specialize in mobility, firearms, magic, nature, or other ranged combat styles.

---

### [[aa Mesmerist Subclass]]

Mesmerist is a **Charisma-based psychic manipulator** who weaponizes perception, fear, charm, and mental control. Rather than relying primarily on raw physical damage, the class uses supernatural influence to **debilitate enemies, manipulate their behavior, and create openings for the party**. Pick Mesmerist if you want a character focused on **psychological warfare, deception, control, and disabling enemies**.

---

### [[aa Monk Subclasses]]

Monk is a highly mobile **Dexterity/Wisdom-based martial class** centered around unarmed combat, Ki, movement, and specialized martial techniques. Monks can become devastating strikers, defensive masters, shadow assassins, elemental warriors, or supernatural martial artists depending on their subclass. Pick Monk if you want **fast, weapon-independent melee combat**, exceptional mobility, and a class that rewards active positioning and tactical play.

---

### [[aa Paladin Subclasses]]

Paladin is a **Charisma-based divine martial class** combining strong weapon combat, Divine Smite, healing, defensive abilities, and oath-specific powers. Your Oath determines the principles and supernatural abilities that shape your character, ranging from holy protection and healing to vengeance, conquest, darkness, and other specialized paths. Pick Paladin if you want a **powerful frontline warrior with divine magic**, especially if you enjoy burst damage through Smites while still providing party support.

---

### [[aa Pugilist Subclasses]]

Pugilist is a **dedicated unarmed martial class** focused on fists, toughness, dirty fighting, and fighting through sheer physical skill rather than traditional weapons. Its subclasses can turn the class into everything from a straightforward boxer to a street brawler, armored fistfighter, supernatural bruiser, or companion-based fighter. Pick Pugilist if you want to **fight primarily with your fists** while having more specialized unarmed mechanics than a traditional Monk.

---

### [[aa Ranger Subclasses]]

Ranger is a **martial/nature hybrid** combining ranged or melee weapon combat with nature magic, exploration abilities, and specialized hunting techniques. Subclasses can heavily change the role, turning the Ranger into a beast companion user, stealthy ambusher, elemental caster, monster hunter, summoner, or nature guardian. Pick Ranger if you want a **weapon-focused character with useful magic and strong versatility**, especially for ranged combat, exploration, and fighting specific types of enemies.

---

### [[aa Rogue Subclasses]]

Rogue is a **Dexterity-based specialist built around Sneak Attack, mobility, stealth, and exploiting enemy weaknesses**. Rather than winning through sustained brute force, Rogues aim to create advantageous situations and then punish enemies with precise attacks. Pick Rogue if you want **high burst damage, stealth, mobility, traps, skill expertise, and exceptional out-of-combat utility**, with subclasses ranging from assassins and thieves to magical and supernatural specialists.

---

### [[aa Sorcerer Subclasses]]

Sorcerer is a **Charisma-based full caster whose magic is innate**, rather than learned through study or granted through a pact. Its defining feature is Metamagic, allowing Sorcerers to modify spells by making them stronger, wider, longer-lasting, or capable of affecting targets in unusual ways. Pick Sorcerer if you want a **highly explosive and flexible caster** with exceptional burst potential and a strong personal magical identity determined by your origin.

---

### [[aa Warlock Subclasses]]

Warlock is a **Charisma-based magical hybrid whose power comes from a pact with a supernatural patron**. Unlike Wizards or Sorcerers, Warlocks rely heavily on their unique Pact and Eldritch Invocations, while their limited spell slots are recovered on a Short Rest. Pick Warlock if you want a **strong magical identity with excellent at-will abilities**, customizable invocations, powerful control or damage, and a subclass that fundamentally changes the source and flavor of your magic.

---

### [[aa Wizard Subclasses]]

Wizard is the ultimate **Intelligence-based arcane specialist**, defined by an enormous spellbook and the ability to learn spells from scrolls. Its subclass determines what area of magic you specialize in, while the core Wizard remains exceptionally versatile because it can prepare different spells for different situations. Pick Wizard if you want **the widest possible selection of arcane spells**, maximum magical versatility, and the ability to completely change your prepared toolkit between adventures.
