### [[Arcane Order]]

A scholarly spellsword that approaches magic like a **Wizard**, using arcane knowledge to enhance their weapons and manipulate the battlefield. **Physical + arcane magic**, with a broad Wizard-like spell list including Fireball, Haste, Misty Step, Hold Monster, and Disintegrate. Their Mystic Mark can convert weapon damage to Force and can be sacrificed to knock enemies back or counter attacks.

---
### [[Heaven's Guard]]

A protective Magus dedicated to **shielding allies and intercepting attacks**, turning their magic into a defensive weapon. **Physical + divine/protective magic**, with healing, buffs, Warding Bond, Counterspell, Spirit Guardians, and powerful damage-redirection abilities. Excellent for a frontline support/tank hybrid.

---
### [[Knights Stygian]]

A dark spellsword who wields **necromancy, curses, fear, and death magic** to protect allies through terrifying means. **Physical + necrotic magic**, specializing in weakening and frightening enemies, draining life, and commanding undead. Their Mystic Mark can convert weapon damage to Necrotic and can be sacrificed to inflict Fear.

---
### [[Primordial Seal]]

A Magus sworn to contain the **ancient elemental forces of the world**, channeling fire, cold, lightning, thunder, acid, and other elements through their weapons and spells. **Physical + elemental magic**, with a huge elemental spell selection and the ability to change their weapon's damage type and temporarily make enemies vulnerable to that element.

---
### [[Sidereal Cabal]]

A mysterious spellsword who manipulates **luck, probability, and fate itself**, intervening at exactly the right moment to change the outcome of events. **Physical + fate/psychic/arcane magic**, focused on rerolling attacks, modifying enemy rolls, protecting allies from failed saves, and manipulating the battlefield through probability.

---
### [[Sylvan Circle]]

A nature-bound Magus who combines **Druidic magic with swordplay**, acting as a magical guardian of the natural world. **Physical + nature/elemental magic**, with healing, terrain control, animal magic, Polymorph, plant-based spells, and the ability to temporarily transform into a beast.
