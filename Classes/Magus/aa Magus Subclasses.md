# [[Magus]]

The [[Magus]] is a spellsword class that combines weapon combat with specialized spellcasting. Rather than functioning as either a full martial class or a traditional caster, it uses weapon attacks, weapon-enhancing magic, spell slots, and [[Mystic Mark]] abilities together.

Unlike a conventional Fighter, the Magus does not rely on [[Extra Attack]] as its primary way of scaling weapon damage. Instead, its subclasses provide specialized weapon cantrips and spell lists that determine how the class mixes attacks and magic. The class can also use spell slots for Smite-like effects, while Mystic Marks can enhance weapons or protect allies.

This makes subclass choice particularly important. Each Eldritch Society gives the Magus a different spellcasting identity, from Wizard-style arcane magic to defensive divine magic, necromancy, elemental manipulation, fate magic, or Druidic abilities.

In combat, [[Magus]] can fill several different roles depending on its subclass:

- **Spellsword** — Combines weapon attacks with offensive spells and weapon cantrips.
- **Frontline support** — Uses defensive magic and Mystic Marks to protect allies.
- **Elemental damage dealer** — Converts weapon attacks and spells into different elemental damage types.
- **Control specialist** — Uses magical effects to weaken, frighten, or reposition enemies.
- **Utility caster** — Uses a broad selection of spells to solve problems outside direct combat.
- **Hybrid damage dealer** — Alternates between weapon attacks and spellcasting depending on the situation.

---

## [[Arcane Order]]

The [[Arcane Order]] is the most traditional magical Magus, approaching combat through **Wizard-like arcane knowledge**. It combines weapon attacks with a broad offensive and utility spell list, making it the closest Magus equivalent to an arcane spellsword.

It is particularly useful outside combat because its broad spell selection gives it tools for movement, exploration, control, and solving magical problems.

### Combat

The [[Arcane Order]] is a **versatile arcane spellsword**.

Its spell list provides powerful options such as Fireball, Haste, Misty Step, Hold Monster, and Disintegrate. This means you can switch between weapon-focused turns and spell-focused turns depending on what the encounter requires.

Its Mystic Mark can convert weapon damage to Force damage, providing a reliable alternative damage type. The mark can also be sacrificed to knock enemies back and knock them prone.

The main drawback is that it has to balance weapon attacks against spellcasting. Casting a major spell uses your Action, so you need to choose whether the current turn benefits more from magic or your weapon cantrip and martial abilities.

### Progression

**Early:** Basic arcane spells and Mystic Mark establish the Magus as a weapon-and-magic hybrid.

**Mid:** Stronger Wizard-style spells provide substantially more offensive, defensive, and mobility options.

**End:** High-level arcane spells allow the Arcane Order to handle enemies through both powerful magic and enhanced weapon attacks.

**Best for:**

- Arcane spellswords
- Offensive magic
- Wizard-style utility
- Force damage
- Flexible combat

## [[Heaven's Guard]]

The [[Heaven's Guard]] is a defensive Magus focused on **protecting allies and redirecting damage**. It combines weapon combat with healing, defensive buffs, and protective magic.

This is the strongest choice for turning the Magus into a frontline support character rather than a primarily offensive spellsword.

### Combat

The [[Heaven's Guard]] is a **tank/support hybrid**.

Its spell list provides tools such as Bless, Cure Wounds, Warding Bond, Counterspell, Spirit Guardians, Death Ward, and defensive barriers. This gives it ways to improve allies while remaining capable of fighting directly.

Its Mystic Mark and defensive abilities allow it to intervene when allies are attacked, making positioning especially important. You want to stay close enough to vulnerable party members that your protective abilities can actually reach them.

The subclass sacrifices some offensive specialization for this utility, but it can contribute meaningful damage while supporting the party.

### Progression

**Early:** Defensive buffs and healing give the Magus immediate party utility.

**Mid:** Warding and damage-redirection abilities make the subclass increasingly effective at protecting allies.

**End:** High-level defensive magic and powerful healing allow the Heaven's Guard to remain a frontline character while providing substantial party protection.

**Best for:**

- Frontline support
- Tanking
- Damage redirection
- Healing
- Defensive parties

## [[Knights Stygian]]

The [[Knights Stygian]] is a dark spellsword built around **necromancy, curses, fear, and death magic**. It combines martial combat with supernatural abilities that weaken enemies and can eventually bring the dead under your control.

It is particularly effective when you want a melee character with strong debuffing and necromantic utility.

### Combat

The [[Knights Stygian]] is a **necrotic damage and debuff specialist**.

Its magic focuses on weakening enemies, frightening them, draining their health, and using undead forces to create additional battlefield pressure.

Its Mystic Mark can convert weapon damage to Necrotic damage. Sacrificing the mark can also inflict Fear, giving the subclass a useful way to turn a successful weapon attack into crowd control.

The subclass works best when you combine weapon attacks with curses or debuffs rather than treating the magic and martial sides as completely separate tools.

### Progression

**Early:** Necromantic abilities and Mystic Mark effects add debuffs to normal weapon combat.

**Mid:** Stronger curses, fear effects, and life-draining magic make the subclass more effective against individual targets.

**End:** Powerful necromancy and undead-related abilities give the Knights Stygian substantial control and sustained battlefield presence.

**Best for:**

- Necromancy
- Fear effects
- Curses
- Necrotic damage
- Dark spellsword builds

## [[Primordial Seal]]

The [[Primordial Seal]] specializes in **elemental magic**, allowing the Magus to combine weapons with fire, cold, lightning, thunder, acid, and other damage types.

It is the most adaptable Magus when dealing with enemies that have specific elemental weaknesses or resistances.

### Combat

The [[Primordial Seal]] is an **elemental damage specialist**.

Its large elemental spell selection allows you to choose the appropriate damage type for the encounter. Its weapon abilities can also change your weapon's damage type, letting the martial side of the build benefit from the same elemental specialization.

One of its most important advantages is the ability to temporarily make enemies vulnerable to a chosen element. When used correctly, this can significantly increase the damage produced by both your spells and weapon attacks.

The subclass rewards preparation and enemy knowledge. Using the wrong element against resistant enemies wastes much of its potential.

### Progression

**Early:** Basic elemental effects give the Magus multiple damage options.

**Mid:** Stronger elemental spells and weapon conversions allow you to specialize against different enemy types.

**End:** High-level elemental magic and vulnerability effects make the Primordial Seal a powerful specialist against appropriately chosen targets.

**Best for:**

- Elemental damage
- Exploiting weaknesses
- Fire, cold, lightning, and other elements
- Flexible damage types
- Element-focused spellswords

## [[Sidereal Cabal]]

The [[Sidereal Cabal]] manipulates **luck, probability, and fate** rather than relying entirely on conventional offensive magic. It is designed around changing the results of important rolls and preventing critical failures.

This gives the Magus strong tactical utility both in and out of combat.

### Combat

The [[Sidereal Cabal]] is a **fate manipulation and support specialist**.

Its abilities can modify attack rolls, saving throws, and other outcomes, allowing you to interfere with enemy successes or protect allies from failures.

This makes the subclass particularly useful during important turns. Instead of simply adding damage, you can use its abilities to ensure a critical attack connects, prevent an enemy's dangerous spell from succeeding, or protect an ally from a failed saving throw.

Its effectiveness depends heavily on timing. Wasting a fate-manipulation ability on an unimportant roll provides far less value than saving it for a decisive attack or saving throw.

### Progression

**Early:** Fate-based abilities provide immediate control over important rolls.

**Mid:** Stronger probability manipulation makes the Magus increasingly effective at preventing bad outcomes.

**End:** The subclass becomes a powerful tactical support character capable of influencing critical attacks, saves, and other decisive moments.

**Best for:**

- Roll manipulation
- Defensive support
- Critical turns
- Tactical utility
- Probability/fate builds

## [[Sylvan Circle]]

The [[Sylvan Circle]] combines Magus weapon combat with **Druidic magic and nature abilities**. It provides access to healing, terrain manipulation, animal magic, plant effects, and transformation abilities while retaining the Magus's martial foundation.

It is particularly useful for players who want a nature-themed spellsword that can also contribute meaningful utility outside combat.

### Combat

The [[Sylvan Circle]] is a **nature-based hybrid spellsword**.

Its spell selection gives it tools for healing, terrain control, elemental damage, and enemy manipulation. It can also temporarily transform into a beast, giving it another option when direct physical combat is preferable.

Its Mystic Mark can interact with its nature-focused abilities, allowing the martial side of the class to benefit from its Druidic specialization.

The subclass has a broad toolkit rather than one overwhelming combat specialization. Its strength comes from switching between weapons, spells, and nature abilities as the situation changes.

### Progression

**Early:** Druidic magic adds healing, utility, and nature-based effects to the Magus's martial abilities.

**Mid:** Stronger terrain control, elemental spells, and nature abilities expand its combat options.

**End:** High-level Druidic magic and transformation abilities allow the Sylvan Circle to function as a highly versatile nature-based spellsword.

**Best for:**

- Druidic spellswords
- Nature magic
- Healing and utility
- Terrain control
- Beast transformation

|[[Heaven's Guard]]|Choose it if you want a defensive frontline Magus focused on protecting and supporting allies.|
|[[Knights Stygian]]|Choose it if you want a necromantic spellsword focused on curses, fear, and death magic.|
|[[Primordial Seal]]|Choose it if you want a highly adaptable elemental spellsword that exploits enemy weaknesses.|
|[[Sidereal Cabal]]|Choose it if you want to manipulate luck, rolls, and critical moments in combat.|
